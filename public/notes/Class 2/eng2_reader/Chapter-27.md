• **Grammar Rule 1**: Use 'will' to talk about things that are going to happen.
• **Grammar Rule 2**: 'Will' remains the same regardless of the subject (e.g., He will, They will).
• **Grammar Rule 3**: The base form of the verb follows 'will' (e.g., will eat, not will eating).
• **Sentence Pattern**: Subject + will + base verb + time expression (e.g., "I will sleep tomorrow").
• **Negative Pattern**: Subject + will not + base verb (e.g., "I will not go to school tomorrow").
• **Question Pattern**: Will + subject + base verb? (e.g., "Will you play tomorrow?").
• **Usage of 'Tomorrow'**: Always place 'tomorrow' at the beginning or end of the sentence for clarity.
• **Common Errors**: Avoiding the use of 'to' after 'will' (e.g., "I will to play" is incorrect).
• **Consistency**: Maintaining the future tense throughout a paragraph when discussing holiday plans.
• **Clarity**: Using simple verbs to ensure the future tense is easily understood by young learners.
• **Emphasis**: Using 'will' to show determination or a firm plan for the holiday.

• **Visualizing the Future**: Encouraging students to imagine their activities to better use the future tense.
• **Reviewing Tense**: Checking that the verb form does not change when 'will' is added.
