• **Chapter Title**: Chapter 8 of the DAV Class UKG English Reader & Rhymes curriculum is officially titled "My School", introducing young learners to their educational environment.
• **Core Subject**: English Language Arts and early childhood literacy, specifically designed for Upper Kindergarten (UKG) students under the DAV Public School Board.
• **Primary Objective**: To familiarize students with the concept of a school, common school objects, classroom routines, and foundational English vocabulary associated with daily school life.
• **Pedagogical Approach**: Employs a whole-language and phonics-based methodology to help four- and five-year-old learners connect spoken words with written text.
• **Thematic Relevance**: Bridges the gap between home environments and institutional learning spaces, fostering a sense of comfort, security, and belonging in the school setting.
• **Vocabulary Development**: Introduces essential nouns such as school, classroom, teacher, friend, desk, chair, bag, book, pencil, and eraser within thematic contexts.
• **Sight Words Integration**: Integrates high-frequency sight words (e.g., *this*, *is*, *my*, *we*, *go*) that appear frequently in early childhood reading materials.
• **Phonics Integration**: Emphasizes initial consonant sounds related to school vocabulary, such as the /s/ sound in *school* and /t/ sound in *teacher*.
• **Sentence Structure**: Utilizes simple, repetitive sentence patterns (e.g., "This is my school," "I go to school") to build early reading fluency and syntactic awareness.
• **Visual Literacy**: Encourages children to associate illustrations of school scenes with corresponding textual descriptions to enhance reading comprehension.
• **Listening Skills**: Promotes active listening through teacher-led read-alouds, rhyming verses, and verbal instructions given during classroom activities.
• **Speaking Skills**: Encourages oral expression by prompting students to talk about their school experiences, favorite classroom activities, and school friends.
• **Value Education**: Cultivates foundational values such as sharing, caring, respect for teachers, and maintaining cleanliness in the classroom environment.
• **Curriculum Alignment**: Fully aligned with the National Curriculum Framework (NCF) guidelines for early childhood care and education (ECCE) implemented across DAV institutions.
• **Holistic Growth**: Supports cognitive, linguistic, and emotional development by transforming everyday school observations into structured learning opportunities.

• **School Building Recognition**: Identifying the physical structure of the school, including the main gate, playground, classrooms, and administrative offices.

• **Teacher-Student Relationship**: Building emotional security and mutual respect between young learners and their class teacher or facilitator.
• **Peer Interaction**: Encouraging positive social interactions, cooperative play, and sharing among classmates to develop early interpersonal skills.
• **Daily Morning Routine**: Highlighting the significance of arriving punctually, standing in lines, greeting teachers with reverence, and attending the morning assembly.

• **Seating Discipline**: Practicing proper posture, sitting correctly on chairs or benches, and keeping walkways clear of bags and personal belongings.
• **Cleanliness Habits**: Inculcating hygienic practices such as using dustbins, keeping desks tidy, and washing hands before consuming snacks.
• **Sense of Belonging**: Developing pride in being a DAV student through the recitation of school anthems and participation in school events.

• **Sentence Pattern 1 - Identification**: Mastering the structural pattern "This is a/my [noun]" to identify objects in the immediate surroundings.
• **Example Sentence Analysis**: Breaking down the sentence "This is my school" into individual component words for phonics and sight recognition.
• **Pronoun Usage - My**: Understanding the possessive pronoun **my** to indicate personal ownership and connection to the school and its objects.
• **Pronoun Usage - I**: Using the first-person pronoun **I** to express personal actions, feelings, and daily routines (e.g., "I go to school").
• **Verb Usage - Go**: Learning the action verb **go** in the present tense to describe movement toward the educational institution.
• **Verb Usage - Love**: Expressing emotional attachment and fondness through sentences like "I love my school" and "I love my teacher."
• **Plural Formation - Bag to Bags**: Introduction to basic pluralization rules by adding 's' to singular nouns (e.g., bag/bags, book/books).
• **Adjective Integration - Big School**: Using simple descriptive adjectives like **big**, **clean**, **beautiful**, and **happy** to modify school nouns.
• **Color Adjectives**: Identifying the colors of school uniforms, blackboards, chalk, and playground equipment (e.g., red bag, green board).
• **Preposition Introduction - In**: Using the preposition **in** to describe location (e.g., "The children are **in** the classroom").
• **Preposition Introduction - On**: Using the preposition **on** to specify placement (e.g., "The book is **on** the desk").
• **Interrogative Forms**: Simple question structures introduced orally by teachers, such as "What is this?" answered with "This is a pencil."
• **Affirmative Sentences**: Constructing positive statements about daily school experiences to build linguistic confidence.
• **Negative Prohibition**: Understanding gentle classroom rules framed through simple negative structures (e.g., "Do not run in the corridors").
• **Reading Fluency Drills**: Choral reading of chapter sentences to improve intonation, rhythm, phrasing, and word recognition speed.
