• **Chapter Title:** 'चींटी ने पाठ पढ़ाया' (The Ant Taught a Lesson) is a moral-based narrative from the DAV Class 3 Hindi textbook.
• **Core Theme:** The story revolves around the virtues of hard work, persistence, and the importance of not underestimating small creatures.
• **Protagonist:** The story features a young boy who initially lacks focus and discipline in his daily tasks.
• **The Ant's Role:** The ant serves as a symbolic teacher, demonstrating that even the smallest beings possess great wisdom through their actions.
• **Setting the Scene:** The narrative begins with the boy feeling frustrated and lazy, unable to complete his assigned work.
• **Observation:** The boy observes an ant carrying a grain of food that is significantly larger than its own body size.
• **Persistence:** The ant faces multiple obstacles, such as uneven surfaces and small pebbles, yet it does not give up.
• **Falling and Rising:** The ant slips multiple times while climbing, but it immediately resumes its journey without showing signs of defeat.
• **The Lesson of Effort:** The boy realizes that if such a tiny creature can show such immense determination, he should also be capable of completing his studies.
• **Self-Reflection:** The boy starts to compare his own lack of effort with the tireless work ethic of the ant.
• **Transformation:** The boy decides to change his attitude toward his responsibilities and adopts a disciplined approach.
• **Character Development:** The story highlights the shift from a state of lethargy to a state of active engagement.
• **Moral Significance:** The chapter emphasizes that success is not achieved through speed, but through consistent, steady effort.
• **Nature as a Teacher:** The story promotes the DAV philosophy of learning from the natural world and the environment.
• **Vocabulary Focus:** Key terms like 'परिश्रम' (Hard work), 'धैर्य' (Patience), and 'दृढ़ संकल्प' (Determination) are central to the chapter.
