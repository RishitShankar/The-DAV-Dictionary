• **Term - चतुराई (Chaturai)**: Meaning **Cleverness or Wit**; highlights Bettina's smart execution of her rescue plans.
• **Term - सहायता (Sahayata)**: Meaning **Help or Assistance**; underscores the altruistic motive behind Bettina's courageous actions.
• **Term - घबराना (Ghabrana)**: Meaning **To Panic**; an action that Bettina consciously avoids doing, unlike others in distress.
• **Term - दृढ़ निश्चय (Dridh Nishchay)**: Meaning **Firm Determination**; the mental state that enables Bettina to stick to her rescue plan.
• **Term - प्रशंसा (Prashansa)**: Meaning **Praise or Appreciation**; what Bettina rightfully receives from everyone after saving the day.
• **Term - अघटित / अचानक (Achanak)**: Meaning **Suddenly or Unexpectedly**; describes how the crisis entered the peaceful storyline.
• **Term - सुरक्षा (Suraksha)**: Meaning **Safety or Protection**; the ultimate goal achieved by Bettina's brave interventions.
• **Term - बहादुरी (Bahaduri)**: Meaning **Valor**; synonymous with sahas, used frequently in classroom discussions of this chapter.
• **Term - सूझ-बूझ (Sujh-bojh)**: Meaning **Presence of mind and common sense**; Bettina's greatest asset during the crisis.
