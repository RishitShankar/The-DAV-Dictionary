• **Chapter Title:** बाल दिवस (Children’s Day) is a celebratory chapter focusing on the significance of November 14th in Indian schools.
• **Context:** The chapter introduces the concept of Children’s Day as a day dedicated to the joy, innocence, and future of children.

• **Affectionate Title:** Children fondly called him 'Chacha Nehru' (Uncle Nehru) due to his immense love for them.
