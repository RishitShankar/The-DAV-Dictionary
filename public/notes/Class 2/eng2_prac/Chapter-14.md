• **Chapter Overview:** "The Silver Tray" is a moral-based narrative designed for Class 2 students to understand the values of honesty, integrity, and the importance of returning lost property.
• **Core Theme:** The story revolves around a young boy who finds a valuable object and faces a moral dilemma regarding its ownership.
• **Setting:** The story takes place in a bustling marketplace and a residential neighborhood, providing a realistic backdrop for the events.

• **Observation:** The boy notices the intricate craftsmanship of the tray, noting its shine and weight, which indicates its high value.

• **Decision Making:** The protagonist chooses to prioritize honesty over personal gain, deciding to search for the rightful owner.
