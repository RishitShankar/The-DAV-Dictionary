• **Chapter Theme:** The chapter "चींटी ने पाठ पढ़ाया" (The Ant Taught a Lesson) focuses on the virtues of hard work, foresight, and perseverance.
• **Core Moral:** Success is achieved through consistent effort rather than laziness or procrastination.
• **Protagonist 1:** The Ant, representing the values of diligence, planning, and preparation for the future.
• **Protagonist 2:** The Grasshopper (or the lazy companion), representing the dangers of idleness and living only for the present moment.
• **Setting the Scene:** The story begins during the warm, sunny summer months when food is abundant and nature is thriving.
• **Ant’s Strategy:** The ant spends the entire summer collecting grains, seeds, and food supplies to store for the winter.
• **Ant’s Foresight:** The ant understands the concept of **Seasonal Change**, knowing that the harsh winter will make food gathering impossible.
• **Grasshopper’s Attitude:** The grasshopper spends his time singing, dancing, and mocking the ant for working so hard during the pleasant weather.
• **Concept of Laziness:** The grasshopper believes that since food is currently available, there is no need to worry about the future.
• **The Passage of Time:** As the seasons transition, the warmth of summer fades and the cold, biting winter arrives.
• **Environmental Shift:** Winter brings snow and frost, covering the ground and making food sources scarce or inaccessible.
• **The Ant’s Reward:** Because of her hard work, the ant is safe, warm, and well-fed inside her home during the winter.
• **The Grasshopper’s Plight:** The grasshopper finds himself hungry, cold, and without any shelter or food reserves.
• **Realization:** The grasshopper realizes his mistake and understands that his lack of planning has led to his suffering.
• **The Lesson:** The ant teaches the grasshopper that "Work today for a better tomorrow" is the key to survival.
