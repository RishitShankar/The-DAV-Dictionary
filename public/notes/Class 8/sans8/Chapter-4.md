* `[GOLD]` **Principle of Non-Retaliation**: The chapter demonstrates that responding to bad words (*दुर्वचनानि*) with silence and goodness deprives evil of its power source.
* `[BLUE]` **Psychological Transformation**: Retaliation breeds further malice; unexpected benevolence (*दयालुता*) induces self-reflection (*पश्चात्तापः/अनुतापः*) in the wrongdoer.
* `[RED]` **Virtue of the Strong**: *क्षमा वीरस्य भूषणम्* (Forgiveness is the ornament of the brave). Maharshi Dayanand possessed the physical and spiritual power to punish, yet chose absolute forgiveness.

### 🔬 SECTION 2.4: THEORY OF GRAMMATICAL CONSTRUCTS IN CHAPTER 4

* 🔸 **The Imperative Framework (लोट् लकारः)**:
  * Used for commands, requests, benedictions, and permissions.
  * Essential form in chapter title: **"क्षमस्व"** (*Root: क्षम्, Ātmanepada, Lōṭ Lakāra, Medium Purusha, Ekavacana*).
* 🔸 **Past Tense Indicator using 'स्म'**:
  * Construction: `Present Tense Verb (लट् लकार) + स्म = Past Tense (लङ् लकार meaning)`.
  * *Example*: **वसति स्म** = **अवसत्** (lived); **वदति स्म** = **अवदत्** (spoke).
* 🔸 **Conjunctive Suffix Mechanics**:
  * **क्त्वा**: Connects two actions performed by the same subject sequentially ($V_1 \to V_2$).
  * **ल्यप्**: Used instead of *क्त्वा* when the root verb is preceded by a prefix (*उपसर्ग*).
  * **तुमुन्**: Expresses the purpose or intention of performing an action (*dative function*).

# PAGE 3: LINGUISTIC LAWS, FORMULAS, EQUATIONS & PRINCIPLES

### 📐 SECTION 3.1: SANSHKRIT SANDHI FORMULAS (सन्धि-सूत्राणि)

* `[GOLD]` **FORMULA 1: गुण-सन्धिः (Guna Sandhi)**
  $$\text{अ / आ} + \text{ऋ} \longrightarrow \text{अर्}$$
  * *Application*: $\text{महा} + \text{ऋषिः} = \mathbf{महर्षिः}$
  * *Textual Evidence*: "क्षमस्व **महर्षे**!" (Vocative case of महर्षिः).

* `[GOLD]` **FORMULA 2: दीर्घ-सन्धिः (Deergha Sandhi)**
  $$\text{अ / आ} + \text{अ / आ} \longrightarrow \text{आ}$$
  * *Application 1*: $\text{गङ्गा} + \text{तीरे} = \mathbf{गङ्गातीरे}$ (Compound)
  * *Application 2*: $\text{तत्र} + \text{एकः} = \mathbf{तत्रैकः}$ (Growth Sandhi: $\text{अ} + \text{ए} = \text{ऐ}$)

* `[GOLD]` **FORMULA 3: विसर्ग-सन्धिः (Visarga Sandhi Rules)**
  $$\text{विसर्गः (ः)} + \text{च / छ} \longrightarrow \text{श्}$$
  * *Application*: $\text{मुनिः} + \text{च} = \mathbf{मुनिश्च}$
  $$\text{विसर्गः (ः)} + \text{अ} \longrightarrow \text{ओ} + \text{ऽ (अवग्रह)}$$
  * *Application*: $\text{सो} + \text{अपि} = \mathbf{सोऽपि}$ ($\text{सः} + \text{अपि}$)

### 📐 SECTION 3.4: 'स्म' TRANSFORMATIONAL RULE
$$\text{Subject (कर्त्ता)} + \text{Object (कर्म)} + \mathbf{\text{Present Verb (लट्)}} + \mathbf{\text{स्म}} \equiv \text{Subject} + \text{Object} + \mathbf{\text{Past Verb (लङ्)}}$$

* *Formula Instance A*:
  $$\text{दुष्टः जनः दुर्वचनानि} + \mathbf{\text{वदति स्म}} \Longleftrightarrow \text{दुष्टः जनः दुर्वचनानि} + \mathbf{\text{अवदत्}}$$
* *Formula Instance B*:
  $$\text{महर्षिः गङ्गातीरे} + \mathbf{\text{वसति स्म}} \Longleftrightarrow \text{महर्षिः गङ्गातीरे} + \mathbf{\text{अवसत्}}$$
