• **Definition of Word Formation (शब्द-निर्माण)**: In Hindi grammar, new words are constantly created to expand vocabulary through processes like compounding, prefixation, and suffixation.
• **Role of Affixes (शब्दांश)**: Affixes are not independent words; rather, they are dependent linguistic particles or syllables that attach to other words to alter their meaning.
• **Concept of Root Words (मूल शब्द)**: A root word, also known as 'प्रकृति' or primary base, possesses an independent, inherent meaning to which affixes are attached.
• **Classification of Affixes**: Based on their placement relative to the root word, dependent word elements are primarily divided into two categories: उपसर्ग and प्रत्यय.
• **Position of उपसर्ग (Prefix)**: An उपसर्ग is a dependent linguistic particle that is always placed *before* (प्रारंभ में) the root word.
• **Position of प्रत्यय (Suffix)**: A प्रत्यय is a dependent linguistic particle that is always placed *after* (अंत में) the root word.
• **Semantic Transformation**: Both prefixes and suffixes possess the unique capability to completely change, invert, or intensely modify the meaning of the original root word.
• **Etymological Roots**: Many prefixes and suffixes in Hindi trace their historical origins to Sanskrit, Arabic-Persian, or native regional dialects (देशज).
• **Absence of Independent Existence**: Prefixes and suffixes cannot stand alone in a sentence or convey complete thoughts by themselves; they require a host root word.
• **Productive Morphology**: Highly productive affixes can attach to dozens of different root words to generate entire families of related terms.
• **Dynamic Language**: The inclusion of grammar topics like prefixes and suffixes in Class 7 emphasizes the transition from basic literacy to advanced morphological manipulation.
• **Contextual Relevance**: In the chapter "स्कूल की छुट्टियाँ", students encounter narratives about vacation planning, which naturally introduce descriptive and action-oriented vocabulary.
• **Grammatical Precision**: Correct attachment of affixes requires adherence to sandhi (phonetic joining) and matra (vowel sign) placement rules in Hindi orthography.
• **Cognitive Development**: Analyzing word structures trains the student brain to deconstruct complex auditory and visual stimuli into meaningful component parts.

• **Detailed Definition of उपसर्ग (Prefix)**: उपसर्ग वे शब्दांश हैं जो किसी शब्द के आरंभ में जुड़कर उसके अर्थ में विशेषता लाते हैं या उसके अर्थ को बदल देते हैं।
• **Etymology of उपसर्ग**: The term is derived from Sanskrit, where 'उप' means 'near' or 'towards' and 'सर्ग' means 'creation' or 'placement'.
• **Primary Function**: The fundamental function of an उपसर्ग is to act as a semantic modifier, shifting a base word into a new conceptual dimension.
• **Examples of Modification**: Adding the prefix 'प्र' to the root word 'हार' (defeat) transforms it into 'प्रहार' (strike/attack), completely altering the word's action profile.
• **Inversion of Meaning**: Prefixes are frequently used to create antonyms, such as adding 'अ' or 'अन' to positive root words to denote absence or negation.
• **Four-Fold Classification**: In standard Hindi grammar taught at the middle-school level, prefixes are categorized based on their linguistic origin.
• **Sanskrit Prefixes (तत्सम/संस्कृत के उपसर्ग)**: Prefixes borrowed directly from Sanskrit that are predominantly used with Sanskrit-derived (तत्सम) words.
• **Hindi Prefixes (तद्भव/हिंदी के उपसर्ग)**: Native prefixes derived from Sanskrit through historical phonetic evolution, used primarily with native Hindi words.
• **Foreign Prefixes (विदेशी उपसर्ग)**: Prefixes borrowed from Urdu, Persian, Arabic, and English that have been seamlessly integrated into everyday Hindi vocabulary.
• **Prefixes used as Avyay (उपसर्ग के रूप में प्रयुक्त अव्यय)**: Certain indeclinable words that function structurally as prefixes in compound formations.
• **Consistency of Form**: Unlike root words that change forms based on gender and number, true prefixes remain completely uninflected (अविकारी).
• **Attachment Mechanism**: When an उपसर्ग attaches to a root word, the resulting fusion often obeys standard phonetic rules, though many simply concatenate.
• **Recognition Strategy**: To identify a prefix, students must strip away the initial particle and verify if the remaining portion is a valid, meaningful root word.
• **Common Misconceptions**: Students must ensure that the remaining portion after removing a suspected prefix is actually a recognized root word, not a random fragment.
• **Textbook Context**: The DAV Class 7 curriculum emphasizes practical identification of these prefixes within reading passages and narrative texts.

• **Detailed Overview of Sanskrit Prefixes (संस्कृत के उपसर्ग)**: There are traditionally twenty-two recognized Sanskrit prefixes in Hindi grammar that form the bedrock of formal vocabulary.
• **Prefix 'अति' (Ati)**: Means beyond, excess, or high degree; attached to root words like 'अन्त' to form 'अत्यन्त' (extreme) or 'वृष्टि' for 'अतिवृष्टि' (heavy rain/flood).
• **Prefix 'अधि' (Adhi)**: Denotes authority, superiority, or ruling position; attached to 'कार' to form 'अधिकार' (authority/right) or 'पति' for 'अधिपति'.
• **Prefix 'अनु' (Anu)**: Signifies following, similarity, or succession; combined with 'करणीय' to form 'अनुकरणीय' (emulable) or 'शासन' for 'अनुशासन' (discipline).
• **Prefix 'अप' (Apa)**: Indicates negation, badness, or inferiority; combines with 'यश' to create 'अपयश' (disgrace) and 'शब्द' for 'अपशब्द' (abuse).
• **Prefix 'अभि' (Abhi)**: Represents direction, near, or intensity; fuses with 'मान' to make 'अभिमान' (pride) and 'शाप' for 'अभिशाप' (curse).
• **Prefix 'अव' (Ava)**: Denotes descent, lowering, or disrespect; attaches to 'गुण' to form 'अवगुण' (demerit) and 'तरण' for 'अवतरण' (descending/passage).
• **Prefix 'आ' (Aa)**: Signifies limitation, return, or up to; combines with 'जीवन' to form 'आजीवन' (lifelong) and 'गमन' for 'आगमन' (arrival).
• **Prefix 'उत् / उद्' (Ut / Ud)**: Indicates elevation, superiority, or rising; pairs with 'कर्ष' for 'उत्कर्ष' (progress) and 'थान' for 'उत्थान' (upliftment).
• **Prefix 'उप' (Upa)**: Denotes secondary, near, or smaller; combines with 'कार' to form 'उपकार' (favor) and 'वन' for 'उपवन' (garden/park).
• **Prefix 'दुर / दुस्' (Dur / Dus)**: Signifies difficulty, badness, or hardship; attaches to 'दिन' to form 'दुर्दिन' (bad days) and 'साहस' for 'दुःसाहस' (audacity).
• **Prefix 'नि' (Ni)**: Indicates negation, depth, or certainty; combines with 'डर' for 'निडर' (fearless - though often classed as mixed) and 'बन्ध' for 'निबंध' (essay).
• **Prefix 'निर् / निः' (Nir / Nih)**: Represents without, out, or negation; attaches to 'धन' to form 'निर्धन' (destitute) and 'बल' for 'निर्बल' (weak).
• **Prefix 'परा' (Para)**: Signifies opposition, reverse, or detachment; combines with 'जय' for 'पराजय' (defeat) and 'मर्श' for 'परामर्श' (consultation).
• **Prefix 'परि' (Pari)**: Denotes around, fully, or surrounding; pairs with 'पूर्ण' for 'परिपूर्ण' (complete) and 'वारिवार' for 'परिवार' (family).

• **Overview of Hindi Prefixes (हिंदी के उपसर्ग)**: Native Hindi prefixes, often called 'तद्भव उपसर्ग', evolved from Sanskrit roots for smoother everyday usage in popular speech.
• **Prefix 'अ' (A)**: Denotes negation or absence; attached to native root words like 'अछूत' (untouchable) or 'अथाह' (bottomless) to signify lack of quality.
• **Prefix 'अध' (Adh)**: Signifies half or incomplete state; combines with 'पका' to form 'अधपका' (half-cooked) and 'कचरा' or 'खिला' for 'अधखिला' (half-bloomed).
• **Prefix 'उन' (Un)**: Represents one less than a specific number (minus one); pairs with 'तीस' to form 'उनतीस' (29) and 'चालीस' for 'उनतालीस' (39).
• **Prefix 'कु' (Ku)**: Denotes bad, evil, or inferior quality; attaches to 'पुत्र' (though Sanskrit crossover) and native words like 'रूप' to make 'कुरूप' (ugly) and 'चाल' for 'कुचाल' (misconduct).
• **Prefix 'चौ' (Chou)**: Signifies four or a collection of four; combines with 'राहा' to form 'चौराहा' (crossroads) and 'पाया' for 'चौपाया' (four-footed animal).
• **Prefix 'दु' (Du)**: Indicates bad or deficient; pairs with 'बाला' for 'दुबाला' or 'गिना' for 'दुगुना' / 'दुविधा' (dilemma).
• **Prefix 'नि' (Ni)**: Signifies complete lack or restriction; combines with 'डर' for 'निडर' (fearless) and 'ठाला' for 'निठल्ला' (idle).
• **Prefix 'पर' (Par)**: Denotes other, outer, or subsequent; attaches to 'दादा' to form 'परदादा' (great-grandfather) and 'लोक' for 'परलोक' (other world).
• **Prefix 'भर' (Bhar)**: Signifies full, complete, or entire; combines with 'पेट' to form 'भरपेट' (stomach-full) and 'पूर' for 'भरपूर' (abundant).
• **Prefix 'स' / 'सु' (Sa / Su)**: Denotes with, good, or along with; pairs with 'पूत' for 'सपूत' (worthy son) and 'जीवित' for 'सजीव' (living).
• **Distinction from Sanskrit**: Unlike Sanskrit counterparts, Hindi prefixes are typically affixed to colloquial, native (देशज/तद्भव) vocabulary items.
• **Simplicity in Structure**: Hindi prefixes maintain a direct, transparent phonetic structure that is easy for middle-school learners to parse.
• **Vocabulary Expansion**: These prefixes empower students to describe everyday objects, quantities, and conditions with native linguistic accuracy.
• **Workbook Integration**: The "अभ्यास सागर" workbook provides targeted exercises where students fill in blanks using appropriate Hindi prefixes based on contextual clues.

• **Overview of Foreign Prefixes (विदेशी उपसर्ग)**: Modern Hindi incorporates numerous prefixes from Urdu, Persian, Arabic, and English due to historical cultural exchanges.
• **Prefix 'कम' (Kam - Urdu/Persian)**: Signifies little, deficient, or scarce; combines with 'अक्ल' to form 'कमअक्ल' (foolish) and 'जोर' for 'कमजोर' (weak).
• **Prefix 'खुश' (Khush)**: Denotes happiness, goodness, or auspiciousness; pairs with 'किस्मत' for 'खुशकिस्मत' (lucky) and 'बू' for 'खुशबू' (fragrance).
• **Prefix 'गैर' (Gair)**: Signifies absence, other, or non-; attaches to 'कानूनी' to form 'गैरकानूनी' (illegal) and 'हाजिर' for 'गैरहाजिर' (absent).
• **Prefix 'दर' (Dar)**: Indicates in, within, or upon; combines with 'असल' to form 'दरअसल' (actually) and 'कारार' for 'दरकिनार' (aside).
• **Prefix 'ना' (Na)**: Represents negation, lack, or bad; pairs with 'पसंद' for 'नापसंद' (disliked) and 'लायक' for 'नालायक' (worthless).
• **Prefix 'बद' (Bad)**: Denotes bad, evil, or wicked; attaches to 'नाम' to form 'बदनाम' (infamous) and 'नसीब' for 'बदकिस्मत/बदबू'.
• **Prefix 'बे' (Be)**: Signifies without, lacking, or void of; combines with 'सहारा' for 'बेसहारा' (helpless) and 'कार' for 'बेकार' (useless).
• **Prefix 'बर' (Bar)**: Indicates upon, high, or proper; pairs with 'वक्त' for 'बरवक्त' (timely).
• **Prefix 'हम' (Ham)**: Denotes equal, shared, or companion; attaches with 'सफ़र' to form 'हमसफ़र' (travel companion) and 'उम्र' for 'हमउम्र' (peer).
• **Prefix 'हर' (Har)**: Signifies every, each; combines with 'दिन' for 'हरदिन' (daily) and 'एक' for 'हरएक' (everyone).
• **English Prefixes in Hindi**: Loan prefixes from English such as 'चीफ' (Chief), 'हेड' (Head), 'सब' (Sub), and 'वाइस' (Vice) are widely used in official and modern contexts.
• **Examples of English Prefixes**: 'हेडमास्टर' (Headmaster), 'सबइंसपेक्टर' (Sub-inspector), 'वाइसराय' (Viceroy), and 'चीफ मिनिस्टर' (Chief Minister).

• **Detailed Definition of प्रत्यय (Suffix)**: प्रत्यय वे शब्दांश हैं जो किसी शब्द के अंत में जुड़कर नए शब्दों का निर्माण करते हैं या उसके अर्थ में परिवर्तन लाते हैं।
• **Etymology of Suffixes**: In linguistic terminology, suffixes modify the grammatical category or semantic nuance of a base word from the rear position.
• **Primary Classification**: Suffixes in Hindi are fundamentally divided into two major branches: कृत प्रत्यय (Krit Pratyay) and तद्धित प्रत्यय (Taddhit Pratyay).
• **Concept of कृत प्रत्यय (Primary Suffixes)**: Suffixes that are attached specifically to verb roots (धातु) to form nouns, adjectives, or other verbal derivatives.
• **Examples of कृत प्रत्यय**: Adding the suffix 'आकू' to the verb root 'पढ़' creates 'पढ़ाकू' (studious); adding 'वाला' to 'लिख' creates 'लिखनेवाला'.
• **Concept of तद्धित प्रत्यय (Secondary Suffixes)**: Suffixes that attach to nouns, pronouns, or adjectives (not verb roots) to derive new words or alter properties.
• **Examples of तद्धित प्रत्यय**: Adding 'इत' to the noun 'प्रकृति' creates 'प्राकृतिक' (natural); adding 'पन' to the adjective 'छोटा' creates 'छोटपन' (smallness).
• **Distinction Between Krit and Taddhit**: The definitive test is whether the base word is an action verb root (धातु) or a substantive noun/adjective.
• **Morphological Impact**: Suffixes frequently convert words from one part of speech to another, such as turning a verb into a noun or an adjective into an abstract noun.
• **Orthographic Changes**: When a suffix is added, the final vowel of the root word often undergoes a compounding transformation or matra shift (e.g., lengthening of initial vowels).
• **Absence of Independent Standing**: Like prefixes, suffixes cannot exist in isolation and hold meaning only when bound to a host word.
• **Productivity in Word Building**: Suffixes are immensely productive in Hindi, allowing for the generation of diminutives, abstract nouns, occupational titles, and relational adjectives.
• **Textbook Alignment**: Chapter 7 devotes significant workbook space to distinguishing between the two types of suffixes through guided practice.
• **Analytical Practice**: Students learn to deconstruct a suffixed word by isolating the suffix and confirming the integrity of the base word.
• **Exam Importance**: Differentiating between कृत प्रत्यय and तद्धित प्रत्यय is a recurring high-scoring question format in DAV grammatical evaluations.

• **Deep Dive into कृत प्रत्यय (Krit Suffixes)**: Exploring specific suffixes attached to verb roots (क्रिया की धातु) and the derivative words they produce.
• **Suffix 'आई' (Aai)**: Attached to verb roots like 'चढ़' to form 'चढ़ाई' (climb) and 'पढ़' to form 'पढ़ाई' (studies - bridging to vacation context).
• **Suffix 'आऊ' (Aau)**: Combined with verb roots such as 'कमा' to form 'कमाऊ' (earning) and 'बिक' to form 'बिकाऊ' (for sale).
• **Suffix 'आक' (Aak)**: Attached to roots like 'तैरा' or 'चाल' to form 'तैराक' (swimmer) and 'चालक' (driver).
• **Suffix 'आन' (Aan)**: Paired with verb roots like 'उड़' to create 'उड़ान' (flight) and 'थक' to create 'थकान' (tiredness).
• **Suffix 'आवट' (Aavat)**: Combined with roots like 'सजा' to form 'सजावट' (decoration) and 'लिख' to form 'लिखावट' (handwriting).
• **Suffix 'आवनी' (Aavani)**: Attached to verbs to form descriptive nouns, enhancing narrative texture.
• **Suffix 'वाला' (Vaala)**: A highly versatile suffix attached to verb forms like 'जानेवाला' (one who is going) or 'खानेवाला' (eater).
• **Suffix 'ता' (Ta)**: Attached to present participle verb bases to denote agent nouns or ongoing states.
• **Suffix 'इया' (Iya)**: Combined with roots like 'घाट' or 'जल' to form descriptive agent nouns.
• **Semantic Role in Sentences**: Krit pratyay derivations frequently function as subjects or direct objects in sentences, expanding syntactic flexibility.
• **Connection to Chapter Theme**: Words like 'पढ़ाई' (studies) and 'छुट्टी' relate directly to the student lifestyle discussed in "स्कूल की छुट्टियाँ".
• **Phonetic Fusion Rules**: Notice how the addition of these suffixes sometimes lengthens the vowel sound of the root word's first syllable (वृद्धि).
• **Systematic Categorization**: Memorizing common verb roots alongside their corresponding Krit suffixes ensures flawless performance in morphological derivation tasks.
• **Practice Methodology**: The "अभ्यास सागर" workbook prompts students to derive words by combining provided verb roots with specified primary suffixes.

• **Deep Dive into तद्धित प्रत्यय (Taddhit Suffixes)**: Examining secondary suffixes that attach to nouns, pronouns, and adjectives to form new lexical units.
• **Suffix 'आई' (Aai)**: Attached to adjectives and nouns like 'बुढ़ा' to form 'बुढ़ापा' (though पा) or 'भला' to form 'भलाई' (kindness) and 'मीठा' to form 'मिठाई'.
• **Suffix 'आन' / 'पन' (Pan)**: Attached to adjectives to form abstract nouns; e.g., 'छोटा' + 'पन' = 'छोटपन' (petty behavior), 'बाल' + 'पन' = 'बालपन' (childhood).
• **Suffix 'ता' (Ta)**: Attached to adjectives to form abstract nouns denoting quality; e.g., 'सुंदर' + 'ता' = 'सुंदरता' (beauty), 'मित्र' + 'ता' = 'मित्रता' (friendship).
• **Suffix 'त्व' (Tva)**: A Sanskrit-derived Taddhit suffix denoting essence; e.g., 'मनुष्य' + 'त्व' = 'मनुष्यता' / 'मनुष्यत्व' (humanity), 'गुरु' + 'त्व' = 'गुरुत्व'.
• **Suffix 'इया' (Iya)**: Attached to place names or nouns to denote origin or association; e.g., 'पश्चिम' + 'इया' = 'पश्चिमी', 'मुख' + 'इया'.
• **Suffix 'वान' / 'मान' (Vaan / Maan)**: Attached to nouns to denote possession or attribute; e.g., 'धन' + 'वान' = 'धनवान' (wealthy), 'बुद्धि' + 'मान' = 'बुद्धिमान' (intelligent).
• **Suffix 'ईला' / 'लू' (Eela / Lu)**: Denotes abundance of a quality; e.g., 'शर्म' + 'ईला' = 'शर्मीला' (shy), 'दया' + 'लू' = 'दयालु' (merciful).
• **Suffix 'कार' / 'वाला' (Kaar / Vaala)**: Used as Taddhit suffixes for occupational nouns; e.g., 'कथा' + 'कार' = 'कथाकार' (storyteller), 'दूध' + 'वाला' = 'दूधवाला' (milkman).
• **Suffix 'इत' (It)**: Added to nouns to form qualifying adjectives; e.g., 'प्रकृति' + 'इत' = 'प्राकृतिक', 'हर्ष' + 'इत' = 'हर्षित' (delighted).
• **Abstract Noun Formation**: Taddhit suffixes are the primary mechanism in Hindi for converting concrete nouns and adjectives into abstract emotional or state concepts.
