• **Chapter Overview**: The chapter "कौशल की सालगिरह" is designed to introduce students to the **औ (au) matra** through a narrative centered on a birthday celebration.
• **Phonetic Focus**: The primary linguistic objective is the mastery of the **औ** sound, which is produced by opening the mouth wide and rounding the lips.
• **Visual Symbol**: The **औ** matra is represented by the symbol **ौ**, which is placed above the consonant it modifies.
• **Character Introduction**: The protagonist of the story is **कौशल**, whose name serves as the primary anchor for the **औ** sound.
• **Contextual Setting**: The story revolves around the event of a **सालगिरह** (anniversary/birthday), providing a festive backdrop for vocabulary acquisition.
• **Linguistic Structure**: The chapter utilizes simple sentence structures suitable for Class 1 learners to reinforce the **औ** sound in various positions within words.
• **Vocabulary Foundation**: Key words introduced include **कौशल** (Kaushal), **मौसी** (Mausi), **पकौड़ी** (Pakodi), and **खिलौना** (Khilona).
• **Cognitive Association**: By associating the **औ** matra with objects like toys and food, students build stronger memory retention.
• **Grammatical Integration**: The chapter demonstrates how the **ौ** symbol changes the pronunciation of base consonants like 'क' to 'कौ' and 'म' to 'मौ'.
• **Narrative Flow**: The story follows a chronological sequence of events leading up to the celebration of Kaushal’s special day.
• **Cultural Context**: The celebration of a birthday reflects common social interactions, making the text relatable to young students.
• **Visual Literacy**: Students are encouraged to identify the **औ** matra in the accompanying illustrations to bridge the gap between text and image.
• **Phonemic Awareness**: The chapter emphasizes the difference between **ओ** (o) and **औ** (au) sounds to prevent common pronunciation errors.
• **Word Recognition**: Repetitive usage of words like **दौड़** (daud) and **पौधा** (paudha) helps in sight-word recognition.
• **Educational Goal**: The ultimate goal is to enable students to read and write words containing the **औ** matra independently.
