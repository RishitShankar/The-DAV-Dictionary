• **Computer System**: A computer is an electronic machine that works on electricity and performs various tasks like drawing, playing games, and solving sums.
• **Main Parts**: A computer consists of four main parts: Monitor, CPU, Keyboard, and Mouse.
• **Peripheral Devices**: These are additional parts connected to the computer to perform specific functions, such as printers, speakers, and scanners.
• **Monitor**: The **Monitor** looks like a television screen and is used to display the work done on the computer.
• **Visual Output**: Everything typed or drawn on the computer appears on the monitor screen for the user to see.
• **Screen Types**: Monitors come in different shapes and sizes, such as flat-panel LCD or LED screens.
• **CPU**: The **CPU (Central Processing Unit)** is known as the 'Brain of the Computer' because it controls all other parts.
• **Processing**: The CPU processes information and tells the other parts of the computer what to do.
• **CPU Cabinet**: The CPU is kept inside a box called the **CPU Cabinet** or System Unit.
• **Keyboard**: The **Keyboard** is a flat, rectangular board that contains many small buttons called keys.
• **Typing**: Keys are used to type letters, numbers, and symbols into the computer.
• **Input Device**: The keyboard is primarily used to give instructions or 'input' to the computer.
• **Mouse**: The **Mouse** is a small pointing device that usually has two or three buttons and a scroll wheel.
• **Mouse Pad**: A mouse is kept on a special mat called a **Mouse Pad** to help it move smoothly.
• **Pointing**: The mouse helps in pointing at items on the screen, selecting objects, and drawing pictures.
• **Clicking**: Pressing the mouse buttons is called 'clicking', which helps in performing actions on the computer.
• **Cursor**: When you move the mouse, a small arrow moves on the screen, which is known as the **Mouse Pointer** or cursor.

• **Computer Hardware**: All the physical parts of a computer that you can touch and feel are called **Hardware**.
• **Monitor Functionality**: The monitor allows the user to watch movies, play games, and view pictures clearly.
• **Power Button**: Every monitor has a power button located on the front or side to turn the display on and off.
• **Screen Clarity**: Modern monitors provide high-definition visuals, making text and images sharp and easy to read.
• **CPU Role**: The CPU performs all the calculations and logical operations required by the computer programs.
• **Storage**: The CPU cabinet also houses storage devices like the Hard Disk, which saves all your files and folders.
• **Cooling Fan**: Inside the CPU cabinet, there is a small fan that keeps the internal parts cool while the computer is working.
• **Keyboard Layout**: A standard keyboard has a layout similar to a typewriter, making it familiar for users to learn.
• **Alphabet Keys**: These keys are used to type words and sentences, usually arranged in the QWERTY format.
• **Number Keys**: These keys are used to type numbers or perform simple mathematical calculations.
• **Special Keys**: Keyboards include special keys like the **Spacebar**, which is the longest key used to add a space between words.
• **Enter Key**: The **Enter Key** is used to move to the next line or to give a command to the computer.
• **Backspace Key**: This key is used to erase characters typed to the left of the cursor.
• **Delete Key**: This key is used to remove characters or objects selected on the screen.
• **Mouse Movement**: Moving the mouse on the pad moves the pointer on the screen in the same direction.
• **Left Click**: The left button is used for selecting items or clicking on icons.
• **Right Click**: The right button is used to open a menu of options related to the selected item.
• **Scroll Wheel**: The wheel between the buttons is used to move up and down on a page or document.

• **Printer**: A **Printer** is an output device used to take the work done on the computer onto a sheet of paper.
• **Hard Copy**: The paper output produced by a printer is called a **Hard Copy**.
• **Soft Copy**: The information displayed on the monitor screen is referred to as a **Soft Copy**.
• **Types of Printers**: Common types include Inkjet printers, which use ink, and Laser printers, which are faster.
• **Speakers**: **Speakers** are output devices used to listen to music, sounds, and voices from the computer.
• **Sound Quality**: Speakers allow multiple people to hear the audio output from the computer simultaneously.
• **Headphones**: **Headphones** are used to listen to sound privately without disturbing others around you.
• **Microphone**: A **Microphone** is an input device used to record your voice or other sounds into the computer.
• **Voice Input**: Microphones are essential for voice chatting, recording songs, or giving voice commands to the computer.
• **UPS**: **UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)** keeps the computer running for a short time if the electricity goes off.
• **Battery Backup**: The UPS acts as a battery backup, protecting the computer from sudden power cuts.
• **Data Loss Prevention**: By providing backup power, the UPS prevents the loss of unsaved work during power failures.
• **Scanner**: A **Scanner** is a device that reads images or text from a paper and copies them into the computer.
• **Digital Conversion**: The scanner converts physical documents into digital files that the computer can store and edit.
• **Webcam**: A **Webcam** is a small camera used to capture live videos and photos to be sent over the internet.
• **Video Conferencing**: Webcams are used for video calls, allowing people to see each other while talking.
• **Joystick**: A **Joystick** is an input device primarily used for playing games on the computer.
• **Control**: It allows the user to control the movement of characters or objects in a game with ease.

• **System Unit**: The **System Unit** is the main box that contains the CPU, memory, and other essential components.
• **Ports**: The back of the system unit has various **Ports** where cables for the monitor, keyboard, and mouse are plugged in.
• **Power Cable**: This cable connects the computer to the main electricity socket to provide power.
• **Data Cables**: These cables carry information between the computer and its peripheral devices.
• **Ergonomics**: It is important to sit in a correct posture while using the computer to avoid physical strain.
• **Monitor Distance**: Keep the monitor at a comfortable distance from your eyes to prevent eye strain.
• **Keyboard Position**: Keep the keyboard at a height where your wrists are comfortable while typing.
• **Mouse Handling**: Hold the mouse gently and move it with your whole arm rather than just your wrist.
• **Cleanliness**: Keep the computer parts clean and free from dust to ensure they work properly for a long time.
• **Food and Drink**: Never eat or drink near the computer, as spills can damage the delicate electronic parts.
• **Gentle Touch**: Always press the keys on the keyboard and buttons on the mouse gently.
• **Cable Management**: Ensure that all wires are organized and not tangled to prevent tripping or accidental disconnection.
• **Shut Down**: Always turn off the computer using the proper 'Shut Down' command before switching off the power button.
• **Energy Saving**: Turn off the monitor and speakers when they are not in use to save electricity.
• **Device Connection**: Never try to plug or unplug cables while the computer is turned on unless instructed.
• **Teacher Guidance**: Always ask for help from a teacher or elder if you are unsure how to connect a device.
• **Computer Lab Rules**: Follow the specific rules of the computer lab to maintain a safe and productive environment.
• **Careful Handling**: Treat all computer parts with care as they are expensive and sensitive machines.

• **Input Process Output (IPO) Cycle**: Computers work on the principle of Input, Processing, and Output.
• **Input**: The data and instructions we give to the computer using devices like the keyboard and mouse.
• **Processing**: The action performed by the CPU on the input data to make it meaningful.
• **Output**: The final result shown on the monitor or printed on paper after processing.
• **Input Devices**: These are tools that allow us to enter data into the computer, such as the Keyboard, Mouse, and Microphone.
• **Processing Device**: The CPU is the only processing device in the basic computer system.
• **Output Devices**: These are tools that show the results of the processing, such as the Monitor, Printer, and Speakers.
• **Keyboard Keys**: The keyboard contains Alphabet keys (A-Z), Number keys (0-9), and Special keys.
• **Spacebar Function**: The Spacebar is the longest key on the keyboard and is used to provide spaces between typed characters.
• **Enter Key Function**: The Enter key is used to tell the computer that the instruction is complete and to move to the next line.
• **Mouse Clicks**: A single click selects an item, while a double-click opens an item or program.
• **Right-Click Function**: A right-click opens a context menu that shows various options for the selected item.
• **Monitor Screen**: The screen is also called the VDU (Visual Display Unit).
• **Printer Utility**: Printers are used to create permanent records of digital work on paper.
• **Speaker Utility**: Speakers are used to enjoy multimedia content like music and educational videos.
• **Microphone Utility**: Microphones are used for voice recording and communication applications.
• **UPS Utility**: The UPS ensures that the computer does not shut down abruptly during a power failure.
• **Scanner Utility**: Scanners are used to digitize photographs and handwritten notes.

• **Monitor Types**: CRT (Cathode Ray Tube) monitors were bulky and heavy, while modern LCD/LED monitors are thin and light.
• **Keyboard Types**: Standard keyboards have 104 keys, but some may have more for multimedia controls.
• **Mouse Types**: Optical mice use a light sensor to track movement instead of a rubber ball used in older mechanical mice.
• **Wireless Devices**: Many modern keyboards and mice are wireless, connecting to the computer via Bluetooth or USB receivers.
• **CPU Speed**: The speed of the CPU determines how fast the computer can perform tasks and calculations.
• **Monitor Resolution**: Higher resolution on a monitor means clearer and more detailed images.
• **Printer Speed**: Measured in pages per minute (PPM), indicating how fast a printer can produce a hard copy.
• **Speaker Channels**: Some speakers come in sets (2.1 or 5.1) to provide surround sound experiences.
• **Microphone Sensitivity**: This determines how well the microphone picks up sound from a distance.
• **UPS Capacity**: The capacity is measured in VA (Volt-Ampere), which determines how long it can power the computer.
• **Scanner Resolution**: Measured in DPI (Dots Per Inch), higher DPI results in better quality digital images.
• **Webcam Quality**: Measured in megapixels, higher megapixels provide clearer video during calls.
• **Joystick Sensitivity**: High-quality joysticks allow for precise control in flight simulators and racing games.
• **Hardware Maintenance**: Regularly dusting the keyboard and cleaning the monitor screen with a soft cloth is essential.
• **Static Electricity**: Avoid touching the internal parts of the CPU cabinet to prevent damage from static electricity.
• **Power Surge Protection**: A UPS also protects the computer from voltage fluctuations that can damage hardware.
• **Peripheral Compatibility**: Ensure that the devices you connect are compatible with your computer's operating system.
• **Driver Software**: Some devices require 'drivers' to be installed on the computer to function correctly.
