• **Chapter Title:** 'मेले की सैर' (A Visit to the Fair) focuses on the linguistic application of the **'ए' (e) matra** (vowel sign े).
• **Linguistic Focus:** The chapter introduces words where the 'ए' sound is placed above the consonant, creating a specific phonetic shift.
• **Phonetic Rule:** The 'ए' matra is represented by the symbol 'े' which is placed on top of the standing line of a consonant.
• **Sound Mechanism:** When 'ए' (e) is added to a consonant, the sound becomes elongated and sharp, distinct from the short 'e' (ि) sound.
• **Contextual Setting:** The narrative revolves around a visit to a village or town fair, providing a relatable environment for Class 1 students.
• **Vocabulary Foundation:** The chapter serves as a bridge between simple two-letter words and more complex vowel-integrated vocabulary.
• **Visual Learning:** The text is designed to be read alongside illustrations of fairground activities to reinforce word-object association.
• **Grammatical Structure:** It emphasizes the correct placement of the matra to avoid common spelling errors in Hindi script.
• **Character Introduction:** The chapter introduces characters like Ramesh, Suresh, and Mahesh, whose names utilize the 'ए' matra.
• **Setting the Scene:** The fair is described as a place of joy, movement, and variety, reflecting the cultural essence of DAV curriculum values.
• **Word Formation:** The process of combining consonants (e.g., र + म + े + श = रमेश) is the primary pedagogical technique used here.
• **Reading Fluency:** Regular practice of these words helps students transition from syllable-by-syllable reading to word-level recognition.
• **Visualizing the Fair:** The chapter describes items like 'झूला' (swing), 'मिठाई' (sweets), and 'खिलौने' (toys) to build descriptive vocabulary.
• **Linguistic Diversity:** It introduces both nouns (names of people/things) and verbs (actions performed at the fair) using the target matra.
• **Educational Objective:** To enable students to identify, read, and write words containing the 'ए' matra with 100% accuracy.

• **Grammar Rule: Placement:** The 'ए' matra (े) is always placed on the top of the vertical bar of the consonant.
• **Exception Handling:** If a consonant does not have a vertical bar (like 'र'), the matra is placed on the curve.
• **The 'र' Exception:** When 'ए' is added to 'र', it becomes 'रे' (re), which is a crucial learning point.
• **Syllable Breakdown:** Breaking words like 'स-ह-े-ल-ी' helps students understand the internal structure of the word.
• **Vowel Consistency:** The 'ए' sound remains consistent regardless of the consonant it is attached to.
• **Contrastive Analysis:** Comparing 'ए' (e) with 'अ' (a) and 'आ' (aa) helps in identifying the specific vowel shift.
• **Writing Practice:** Students are encouraged to trace the 'े' symbol to develop motor skills for Hindi script.
• **Visual Cues:** The textbook uses color-coding for the 'ए' matra to make it stand out from the rest of the word.
• **Phonetic Drill:** Repeating words aloud helps in internalizing the 'e' sound.
• **Sentence Expansion:** Starting with two-word sentences and moving to longer, descriptive sentences.
• **Contextual Meaning:** Understanding that 'मेले की सैर' is not just a title but a summary of the chapter's plot.
• **Vocabulary Expansion:** Learning words beyond the textbook through association with the fair theme.
• **Logical Sequence:** The chapter follows a chronological order: going to the fair, seeing things, and returning home.
• **Emphasis on Clarity:** Ensuring that the 'े' is clearly written and not confused with the 'ै' (ai) matra.
• **Teacher-Led Reading:** The chapter is designed for guided reading to ensure correct pronunciation of the 'e' sound.

• **Common Mistake 1:** Confusing 'े' (e) with 'ै' (ai). The 'े' has one stroke, while 'ै' has two.
• **Common Mistake 2:** Placing the matra on the wrong part of the consonant. It must be on the vertical bar.
• **Common Mistake 3:** Mispronouncing the 'e' sound as 'i' or 'a'. It must be a distinct 'e' sound.
• **Common Mistake 4:** Forgetting the 'Purna Viram' (।) at the end of sentences.
• **Common Mistake 5:** Incorrectly joining letters when writing words like 'केले'.
