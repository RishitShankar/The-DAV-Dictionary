• **Chapter Overview:** 'राजू का सपना' (Raju's Dream) is a narrative lesson designed for Class 2 students to understand the power of imagination, goal setting, and the importance of hard work.
• **Protagonist Introduction:** The central character is Raju, a young, curious, and imaginative boy who represents the inquisitive nature of children.
• **Theme of Dreams:** The chapter explores the concept of 'सपना' (Dream), defining it as a mental vision that inspires children to think beyond their immediate surroundings.
• **Setting the Scene:** The story begins with Raju lying down to sleep, which serves as the transition point from reality to the world of his imagination.
• **The Concept of Imagination:** The text emphasizes that dreams are the first step toward achieving future ambitions and personal growth.
• **Character Traits:** Raju is depicted as a dreamer who possesses a positive outlook on life and a desire to achieve something meaningful.
• **Literary Purpose:** The chapter aims to improve vocabulary related to daily life, emotions, and aspirations in the Hindi language.
• **Linguistic Focus:** The lesson utilizes simple yet descriptive Hindi sentences to help students grasp narrative flow and sentence structure.
• **Moral Foundation:** The underlying moral is that while dreaming is essential, it must be coupled with determination and effort in waking life.
• **Visual Imagery:** The narrative encourages students to visualize the events, fostering creative thinking and cognitive development.
• **Vocabulary Building:** Key words like 'सपना' (Dream), 'सोना' (Sleep), 'सोचना' (Thinking), and 'उड़ान' (Flight) are introduced to enhance descriptive language.
• **Emotional Connection:** The story connects with the reader by validating the common childhood experience of having big dreams.
• **Narrative Structure:** The story follows a logical progression: Raju goes to sleep, enters a dream state, experiences a series of events, and wakes up inspired.
• **Educational Objective:** To teach students how to express their thoughts and aspirations clearly in Hindi.
• **Role of Dreams:** The chapter highlights that dreams are a reflection of a child's inner interests and hidden talents.

• **Key Term: 'सपना' (Dream):** A series of thoughts, images, or emotions occurring during sleep; in this context, a goal or aspiration.
• **Key Term: 'कल्पना' (Imagination):** The faculty or action of forming new ideas, images, or concepts of external objects not present to the senses.
• **Key Term: 'मेहनत' (Hard Work):** The act of putting in consistent effort to achieve a desired outcome.
• **Key Term: 'प्रेरणा' (Inspiration):** The process of being mentally stimulated to do or feel something, especially to do something creative.
• **Key Term: 'लक्ष्य' (Goal):** The object of a person's ambition or effort; an aim or desired result.
• **Key Term: 'साहस' (Courage):** The ability to do something that frightens one; strength in the face of pain or grief.
• **Key Term: 'आश्चर्य' (Wonder):** A feeling of surprise mingled with admiration, caused by something beautiful, unexpected, or unfamiliar.
• **Key Term: 'विश्वास' (Belief/Confidence):** Acceptance that a statement is true or that something exists; trust in one's own abilities.
• **Key Term: 'प्रयास' (Attempt):** An effort made to achieve or attain something.
• **Key Term: 'सफलता' (Success):** The accomplishment of an aim or purpose.
• **Key Term: 'विचार' (Thought):** An idea or opinion produced by thinking or occurring suddenly in the mind.
• **Key Term: 'उड़ान' (Flight):** The action or process of flying through the air; metaphorically, reaching new heights.
• **Key Term: 'दुनिया' (World):** The earth, together with all its countries, peoples, and natural features; in the story, the dream world.
• **Key Term: 'खुशी' (Happiness):** The state of being happy; a key emotion Raju feels in his dream.
• **Key Term: 'सीख' (Lesson):** A piece of knowledge or wisdom gained from an experience.
