• **Term - अपठित गद्यांश:** An unseen prose passage used to evaluate comprehension, vocabulary, and analytical skills.

• **Term - आश्रम:** A residential community hermitage dedicated to spiritual growth, simplicity, and collective labor.
• **Term - सादगी:** The philosophical and practical lifestyle choice of living simply without material excess or ostentation.
• **Term - स्वावलंबन:** Self-reliance, involving the execution of personal and communal tasks independently without hired help.
• **Term - आतिथ्य:** The quality and practice of receiving and entertaining guests warmly and equitably.
• **Term - श्रमदान:** Voluntary contribution of physical labor for community welfare and institutional upkeep.
• **Term - अनुशासन:** Adherence to established rules, codes of conduct, and internal self-restraint.
• **Term - संज्ञा (Noun):** A word representing a person, place, thing, or abstract state (e.g., अतिथि, आश्रम, सादगी).
• **Term - सर्वनाम (Pronoun):** A word used in place of a noun to maintain narrative flow (e.g., वह, वे, जो).
• **Term - विशेषण (Adjective):** A word that describes or modifies a noun (e.g., कठिन नियम, सादा जीवन).
• **Term - क्रिया (Verb):** A word indicating an action, state, or occurrence in a sentence (e.g., आना, रहना, करना).
• **Term - उपसर्ग (Prefix):** A morpheme added before a root word to alter its meaning (e.g., अनु + शासन).
• **Term - प्रत्यय (Suffix):** A morpheme added at the end of a root word to form new parts of speech (e.g., सुंदर + ता).
