• **Non-Conjunct Structure (अमात्रिक शब्द)**: Words that do not use any dependent vowel modifiers, keeping the phonetic decoding simple for UKG learners.

• **Phonetic Decomposition**: The mechanical process of breaking down complex four-letter words into individual consonant units for spelling mastery.
• **Letter 'उ' (U)**: The starting independent vowel of the word 'उबटन', serving as an essential building block for early phonetic reading.
• **Letter 'ब' (Ba)**: The second phonetic component in 'उबटन', introducing bilabial plosive sounds to UKG students.
• **Letter 'ट' (Ta)**: The retroflex plosive consonant occupying the third position in the word 'उबटन'.
• **Letter 'न' (Na)**: The nasal alveolar consonant that successfully closes the four-letter word 'उबटन'.
• **Word Synthesis**: Combining 'उ', 'ब', 'ट', and 'न' to form the complete, meaningful vocabulary item 'उबटन' (Ubtan).
• **Artistic Rendering**: Coloring the 'उबटन' container in the drawing book to anchor the meaning of the word in the student's visual memory.
• **Cultural Context**: Introducing children to traditional Indian health practices through art, aligning with DAV's cultural values.
• **Letter 'म' (Ma)**: The bilabial nasal consonant initiating the second word 'मल' in the chapter's core sentence.
• **Letter 'ल' (La)**: The lateral approximant consonant completing the word 'मल', meaning to rub or massage.
• **Action Integration**: Connecting the art of coloring the rubbing motion to the linguistic concept of the word 'मल'.
• **Letter 'क' (Ka)**: The velar plosive consonant used twice in the sentence, appearing in 'कर' and 'कसरत'.
• **Letter 'र' (Ra)**: The alveolar trill consonant that features prominently in 'कर' and ends the word 'कसरत'.
• **Word Combination 'कर'**: A two-letter auxiliary action word meaning 'having done' or 'do', linking the steps in the sentence.
• **Visual Flow**: Ensuring the illustrations on the page flow logically from applying ubtan to performing physical exercise.
