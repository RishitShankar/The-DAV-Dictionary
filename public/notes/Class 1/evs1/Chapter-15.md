---PAGE-BREAK---

•   **Chapter 15: Let us meet them** introduces us to many important people who help us in our daily lives.
•   These people are called **community helpers** because they work for the good of everyone in our community or neighborhood.
•   Every person in our community has a special job or work that helps others.
•   We meet different community helpers almost every day, even if we don't always realize it.
•   Learning about these helpers helps us understand how our society works and how everyone depends on each other.
•   It is very important to **respect** all community helpers and be thankful for their hard work.
•   No job is small or big; every job is important because it contributes to making our lives easier and better.
•   Imagine if there was no one to deliver letters or no one to fix our shoes; our lives would be very difficult.
•   This chapter will help us identify different helpers, understand what they do, and know some of the tools they use.
•   We will learn about people who help us stay healthy, learn new things, keep our surroundings clean, and much more.
•   Recognizing these helpers also teaches us about different professions and the value of work.
•   Some helpers work in specific places like schools or hospitals, while others work on the streets or in our homes.
•   They all work hard to provide us with services, goods, and safety.
•   Being polite and kind to community helpers is a good habit that we should all practice.
•   This chapter encourages us to appreciate the people who make our lives comfortable and safe.
•   We will explore various roles, from those who teach us to those who protect us.
•   Understanding their roles helps us know whom to approach when we need specific help.
•   For example, if we are sick, we know we need to see a doctor or a nurse.
•   If we want to send a letter, we know a postman will help us.
•   Let's begin our journey to meet these wonderful people who help us every day.

---PAGE-BREAK---

•   **The Doctor:** A doctor is a very important community helper who helps us when we are sick or injured.
•   **Role of a Doctor:** Doctors examine us, find out what is wrong, and give us medicines or treatment to make us feel better.
•   They also advise us on how to stay healthy, like eating good food and exercising.
•   **Working Place:** Doctors usually work in a **hospital** or a **clinic**.
•   A **hospital** is a big building where many doctors and nurses work to treat many patients.
•   A **clinic** is usually a smaller place where a doctor sees patients.
•   **Tools of a Doctor:** Doctors use special tools to check our health.
•   **Stethoscope:** This is a tool doctors use to listen to our heartbeat and breathing.
•   **Thermometer:** This tool is used to check our body temperature to see if we have a fever.
•   **Medicines:** Doctors prescribe medicines that help us recover from illness.
•   **Syringe:** Used to give injections when needed, which helps us get better quickly.
•   **Bandage:** Used to cover wounds and help them heal.
•   **The Nurse:** A nurse is another very important helper who works closely with doctors.
•   **Role of a Nurse:** Nurses take care of patients, give them medicines, change bandages, and make sure they are comfortable.
•   They help doctors during operations and other medical procedures.
•   **Working Place:** Nurses also work in hospitals and clinics, just like doctors.
•   **Tools of a Nurse:** Nurses use many of the same tools as doctors, like thermometers and syringes, and also keep records of patients.
•   Both doctors and nurses are dedicated to keeping us healthy and safe.
•   We should always be polite and cooperative with them when we visit a hospital or clinic.

---PAGE-BREAK---

•   **The Teacher:** A teacher is a very special person who helps us learn new things every day.
•   **Role of a Teacher:** Teachers teach us how to read, write, count, and understand the world around us.
•   They help us learn about different subjects like English, Math, EVS, and Art.
•   Teachers also teach us good manners, discipline, and how to be kind to others.
•   They guide us to become good and responsible citizens.
•   **Working Place:** Teachers primarily work in a **school**.
•   A **school** is a place where children go to learn and grow.
•   Teachers also teach in colleges and universities for older students.
•   **Tools of a Teacher:** Teachers use many things to help us learn effectively.
•   **Books:** Textbooks and storybooks are essential tools for teaching and learning.
•   **Blackboard/Whiteboard:** Teachers write on these boards to explain lessons to the whole class.
•   **Chalk/Markers:** Used to write on the blackboard or whiteboard.
•   **Duster/Eraser:** Used to clean the board.
•   **Pencils, Pens, Notebooks:** Students use these, and teachers guide their use.
•   **Maps and Globes:** Used to teach about different places in the world.
•   **Charts and Diagrams:** Visual aids that help explain complex topics easily.
•   Teachers spend a lot of time preparing lessons and activities to make learning fun and interesting.
•   They encourage us to ask questions and explore new ideas.
•   We should always listen to our teachers, follow their instructions, and respect them.
•   Teachers play a huge role in shaping our future and helping us achieve our dreams.
•   They are like second parents who care for us and guide us during our school hours.

•   **The Postman:** A postman is a community helper who brings us letters and parcels from our friends and family.
•   **Role of a Postman:** The main job of a postman is to deliver mail, which includes letters, postcards, and small packages, to our homes and offices.
•   They collect mail from post offices and then sort them according to addresses before delivering them.
•   Postmen help people stay connected by delivering messages from one place to another, sometimes across long distances.
•   **Working Place:** Postmen work for the **post office**.
•   The **post office** is a place where we can buy stamps, send letters, and parcels.
•   Postmen spend most of their working day outdoors, walking or riding bicycles/scooters to deliver mail.
•   **Tools of a Postman:** Postmen use specific items to carry out their duties.
•   **Postbag:** A large bag used to carry many letters and parcels safely.
•   **Letters and Postcards:** The items they deliver, containing written messages.
•   **Stamps:** Small sticky papers that we put on letters to pay for their delivery service.
•   **Uniform:** Postmen often wear a special uniform so people can easily recognize them.
•   They work in all kinds of weather, whether it's sunny, rainy, or cold, to ensure our mail reaches us.
•   It is important to have a clear address and a proper letterbox for the postman to deliver mail easily.
•   The postman's work helps us communicate with people who live far away.
•   Before phones and the internet, postmen were the primary way people communicated over distances.
•   Even today, sending a physical letter or card can be a very special way to connect with loved ones.
•   We should always be polite and friendly to our postman when they visit our homes.
•   Their dedication ensures that important documents and heartfelt messages reach their destinations.

•   **The Police Officer:** A police officer is a brave community helper who protects us and keeps our community safe.
•   **Role of a Police Officer:** Police officers maintain law and order, which means they make sure everyone follows the rules and laws of the country.
•   They help prevent crimes and catch people who break the law.
•   Police officers also help people who are in trouble, like finding lost children or helping during accidents.
•   They control traffic on busy roads to prevent accidents and ensure smooth movement of vehicles.
•   **Working Place:** Police officers work from a **police station**.
•   A **police station** is the main office for police in a particular area, where people can report crimes or seek help.
•   They also patrol the streets, parks, and other public places to ensure safety.
•   **Tools of a Police Officer:** Police officers use various tools to perform their duties effectively.
•   **Uniform:** They wear a special uniform that makes them easily recognizable and shows their authority.
•   **Whistle:** Used to direct traffic or alert people.
•   **Walkie-talkie/Radio:** Used to communicate with other police officers and the police station.
•   **Lathi/Baton:** A stick used for self-defense or to control crowds if necessary.
•   **Police Car/Motorcycle:** Used for patrolling and responding quickly to emergencies.
•   **Handcuffs:** Used to restrain individuals who are arrested.
•   Police officers are trained to handle difficult and dangerous situations.
•   They work day and night to ensure our safety and peace.
•   We should always feel safe when we see a police officer and know that they are there to help us.
•   If we ever get lost or need help, we can always approach a police officer.
•   It is important to cooperate with police officers and follow their instructions for our own safety.
•   They are guardians of our community, working tirelessly to protect us from harm.

•   **The Firefighter:** A firefighter is a courageous community helper who puts out fires and rescues people from dangerous situations.
•   **Role of a Firefighter:** The primary job of a firefighter is to extinguish fires in homes, buildings, and forests.
•   They also rescue people and animals trapped in burning buildings, collapsed structures, or other emergencies.
•   Firefighters provide first aid to injured people at the scene of an accident or fire.
•   They educate the public about fire safety and how to prevent fires.
•   **Working Place:** Firefighters work from a **fire station**.
•   A **fire station** is where fire engines and other firefighting equipment are kept, and firefighters wait for emergency calls.
•   When there is a fire, they rush to the spot immediately.
•   **Tools of a Firefighter:** Firefighters use specialized equipment to fight fires and perform rescues.
•   **Fire Engine/Fire Truck:** A large vehicle that carries water, hoses, ladders, and other tools to the fire site.
•   **Hose:** A long, flexible pipe used to spray water or foam onto the fire.
•   **Ladder:** Used to reach high places in burning buildings or to rescue people from upper floors.
•   **Axe:** Used to break down doors or windows to gain entry or create ventilation.
•   **Protective Gear:** Firefighters wear special fire-resistant suits, helmets, and masks to protect themselves from heat, smoke, and falling debris.
•   **Oxygen Tank:** Carried on their backs to breathe in smoky environments.
•   Firefighters are highly trained professionals who risk their lives to save others.
•   Their work requires great bravery, strength, and quick thinking.
•   It is very important to call the fire department immediately if we see a fire.
•   We should never play with fire or firecrackers without adult supervision.
•   Firefighters are true heroes who protect our lives and property from the destructive power of fire.
•   We should always be grateful for their selfless service and courage.

•   **The Farmer:** A farmer is a very important community helper who grows food for all of us to eat.
•   **Role of a Farmer:** Farmers plant seeds, take care of crops, and harvest them when they are ready.
•   They grow various food items like wheat, rice, vegetables, fruits, and pulses.
•   Some farmers also raise animals like cows, chickens, and goats for milk, eggs, and meat.
•   Without farmers, we would not have fresh food on our plates every day.
•   **Working Place:** Farmers work in **fields** and on **farms**.
•   A **farm** is a piece of land used for growing crops or raising animals.
•   They spend long hours outdoors, working hard under the sun and rain.
•   **Tools of a Farmer:** Farmers use many tools, both simple and complex, for their work.
•   **Plough:** A tool used to turn over the soil before planting seeds.
•   **Tractor:** A powerful machine used for ploughing, planting, and harvesting on large farms.
•   **Seeds:** The starting point for growing any plant.
•   **Watering Can/Irrigation System:** Used to provide water to the crops.
•   **Sickle:** A hand tool with a curved blade used for harvesting crops.
•   Farmers' hard work ensures that we have nutritious food to stay healthy and strong.
•   We should never waste food, as it is a result of a farmer's immense effort.
•   **The Gardener:** A gardener is a community helper who takes care of plants, flowers, and trees in gardens and parks.
•   **Role of a Gardener:** Gardeners plant new plants, water them, remove weeds, and prune branches to keep gardens beautiful and healthy.
•   They help make our surroundings green and pleasant, which is good for our environment.
•   **Working Place:** Gardeners work in **gardens**, **parks**, and sometimes in our own backyards.
•   **Tools of a Gardener:** Gardeners use specific tools for plant care.
•   **Spade/Trowel:** Used for digging small holes and planting.
•   **Watering Can:** Used to water plants.
•   **Rake:** Used to gather leaves and debris.
•   **Shears/Pruners:** Used to trim plants and bushes.
•   Gardeners help create beautiful spaces where we can relax and enjoy nature.

•   **The Tailor:** A tailor is a community helper who stitches clothes for us.
•   **Role of a Tailor:** Tailors measure our bodies, cut fabric, and sew pieces together to make new clothes that fit us perfectly.
•   They can also repair torn clothes or alter clothes that are too big or too small.
•   Tailors help us look neat and presentable by providing us with well-fitting garments.
•   **Working Place:** Tailors usually work in a **tailor shop** or a small workshop.
•   Their shops are often filled with different types of fabrics, threads, and sewing machines.
•   **Tools of a Tailor:** Tailors use several tools for their craft.
•   **Sewing Machine:** The most important tool, used to stitch fabric quickly and neatly.
•   **Needle and Thread:** Used for hand-stitching, finishing, and small repairs.
•   **Scissors:** Used to cut fabric precisely according to patterns.
•   **Measuring Tape:** Used to take body measurements accurately.
•   **Chalk:** Used to mark patterns on the fabric before cutting.
•   **Iron:** Used to press clothes and make them smooth.
•   Tailors are skilled in creating various types of clothing, from simple shirts to elaborate dresses.
•   Their attention to detail ensures that our clothes are comfortable and stylish.
•   **The Cobbler:** A cobbler is a community helper who mends and repairs our shoes.
•   **Role of a Cobbler:** When our shoes get torn, the sole comes off, or a heel breaks, a cobbler fixes them.
•   They make our old shoes look new again and extend their life, saving us money.
•   Cobblers also polish shoes to make them shiny and clean.
•   **Working Place:** Cobblers often work in small shops or sometimes sit by the roadside with their tools.
•   **Tools of a Cobbler:** Cobblers use specific tools to repair shoes.
•   **Hammer:** Used to nail new soles or heels onto shoes.
•   **Nails:** Small metal pieces used to attach parts of the shoe.
•   **Glue:** Strong adhesive used to stick parts of the shoe together.
•   **Leather pieces:** Used to replace worn-out parts of shoes.
•   **Awl:** A pointed tool used to make holes in leather.
•   Cobblers help us keep our footwear in good condition, which is important for our comfort and hygiene.
•   Their skilled hands can transform a damaged shoe into a wearable one.

•   **The Carpenter:** A carpenter is a skilled community helper who works with wood to make and repair wooden items.
•   **Role of a Carpenter:** Carpenters make furniture like tables, chairs, beds, and cupboards.
•   They also build wooden doors, windows, and frames for houses.
•   If any wooden item in our home breaks, a carpenter can fix it.
•   Carpenters help make our homes and offices functional and beautiful with their wooden creations.
•   **Working Place:** Carpenters often work in a **workshop** where they have all their tools and wood.
•   They also visit homes and construction sites to install or repair wooden structures.
•   **Tools of a Carpenter:** Carpenters use a variety of tools, many of which are sharp and powerful.
•   **Saw:** Used to cut pieces of wood into desired shapes and sizes.
•   **Hammer:** Used to drive nails into wood to join pieces together.
•   **Nails and Screws:** Fasteners used to hold wooden parts securely.
•   **Measuring Tape:** Used to take accurate measurements of wood.
•   **Drill:** Used to make holes in wood.
•   **Planes:** Tools used to smooth the surface of wood.
•   **Wood:** The primary material a carpenter works with.
•   Carpenters require precision and creativity to craft sturdy and attractive wooden items.
•   Their work is essential for building and maintaining our homes and furniture.
•   **The Plumber:** A plumber is a community helper who installs and repairs water pipes, taps, and sanitary fittings.
•   **Role of a Plumber:** Plumbers fix leaky taps, clogged drains, and broken water pipes.
•   They install new sinks, toilets, and water heaters in our homes.
•   Plumbers ensure that we have a continuous supply of clean water and that wastewater is properly drained.
•   **Working Place:** Plumbers usually work in people's homes, offices, and construction sites where plumbing systems are needed.
•   **Tools of a Plumber:** Plumbers use specialized tools for working with pipes and water systems.
•   **Wrench:** A tool used to tighten or loosen nuts and bolts on pipes.
•   **Pipes:** The main components of a plumbing system, carrying water.
•   **Pliers:** Used for gripping and bending small pipes or wires.
•   **Pipe Cutter:** Used to cut pipes to the correct length.
•   **Sealant/Tape:** Used to prevent leaks in pipe joints.
•   Plumbers' work is crucial for maintaining hygiene and ensuring the proper functioning of water systems in our buildings.
•   Without them, we would face many problems with water supply and drainage.

•   **The Electrician:** An electrician is a community helper who installs and repairs electrical wiring and appliances.
•   **Role of an Electrician:** Electricians fix electrical faults, install new light fixtures, fans, and power outlets.
•   They ensure that electricity flows safely and correctly in our homes and buildings.
•   Working with electricity can be dangerous, so electricians are trained professionals.
•   **Working Place:** Electricians work in homes, offices, factories, and construction sites.
•   **Tools of an Electrician:** They use tools like screwdrivers, pliers, wire cutters, and testers to work with wires and circuits.
•   **The Sweeper:** A sweeper is a community helper who helps keep our surroundings clean.
•   **Role of a Sweeper:** Sweepers clean streets, parks, and public places by sweeping away dust, leaves, and litter.
•   They play a vital role in maintaining hygiene and preventing the spread of diseases.
•   **Working Place:** Sweepers work outdoors in various public areas.
•   **Tools of a Sweeper:** They use brooms, dustpans, and garbage bins.
•   **The Milkman:** A milkman is a community helper who delivers fresh milk to our homes every morning.
•   **Role of a Milkman:** Milkmen ensure that we have fresh milk for our breakfast and other needs.
•   They wake up very early to collect milk and deliver it to many houses.
