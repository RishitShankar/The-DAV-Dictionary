• **Detailed Word Meanings (शब्दार्थ)**: To master the chapter, students must understand specific literary terms embedded within the verses of 'समय'.
• **Term - 'समय' (Samay)**: Meaning time, duration, or a specific juncture; the primary subject of the poetic discourse.
• **Term - 'मूल्य' (Moolya)**: Meaning value, worth, or price; highlighting that time is priceless and beyond economic estimation.
• **Term - 'अवसर' (Avasar)**: Meaning opportunity, occasion, or favorable moment that requires immediate seizure.
• **Term - 'नदियाँ' (Nadiyaan)**: Meaning rivers; used metaphorically to represent the continuous, uninterrupted forward flow of time.
• **Term - 'कर्म' (Karma)**: Meaning action, deed, or duty; emphasizing that time must be filled with meaningful labor.
• **Term - 'भविष्य' (Bhavishya)**: Meaning the future; which is shaped entirely by actions performed in the present moment.
• **Term - 'वर्तमान' (Vartaman)**: Meaning the present moment; the only real, actionable time frame available to human beings.
• **Term - 'पछतावा' (Pachhatava)**: Meaning regret or remorse; the inevitable emotional consequence of wasting precious time.
• **Term - 'सफलता' (Safalta)**: Meaning success or achievement; attained only by those who respect and manage time.
• **Term - 'आलस्य' (Alasya)**: Meaning laziness or lethargy; identified as the prime enemy of time optimization.
• **Term - 'कर्तव्य' (Kartavya)**: Meaning duty or obligation; what a student must fulfill without unnecessary postponement.
• **Term - 'निश्चय' (Nishchay)**: Meaning determination, resolve, or firm decision to utilize every day productively.
• **Term - 'महान' (Mahan)**: Meaning great or noble; status achieved by historical figures who respected the clock.
• **Term - 'अनमोल' (Anmol)**: Meaning priceless or invaluable; an adjective repeatedly applied to the nature of time.
