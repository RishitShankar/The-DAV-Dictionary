• **Chapter Overview**: "The Swing" is a classic poem by Robert Louis Stevenson that captures the simple, universal joy of a child swinging in a garden.
• **Core Theme**: The poem explores the feeling of freedom, excitement, and the sensory experience of moving through the air.
• **Poetic Perspective**: The speaker is a young child who finds the act of swinging to be the most pleasant experience in the world.

• **The Act of Swinging**: The physical motion of the swing is described as a repetitive, rhythmic movement that creates a sense of flying.
• **Childhood Innocence**: The poem highlights how children find immense pleasure in simple, low-cost activities.

• **The Downward Motion**: The child describes the sensation of dropping back down toward the earth.
• **Perspective Shift**: As the child swings higher, their view of the world changes, offering a wider perspective.

• **Emotional Connection**: The poem serves as a nostalgic reminder of the simple joys of early childhood.
