## 📌 PAGE 2: FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES & THEORY DEEP-DIVE

### 🏛️ SOCIO-POLITICAL FOUNDATIONS OF THE NARRATIVE

*   **[GOLD] Imperial Exploitation Machinery:**
    *   The British Administration implemented rigid bureaucratic control over forest resources in South India during the early 20th century.
    *   The indigenous population, specifically the **कोया आदिवासी (Koya Tribals)**, lived a self-sustained life based on forest produce and traditional agriculture.
    *   The construction of a forest road through the steep hills of **गुडेम (Gudem)** served a dual imperial purpose: strategic troop mobility and exploitation of forest timber.

*   **[RED] The Theoretical Mechanics of 'बेगार' (Begari - Forced Labor):**
    *   **Definition:** *Begari* represents an oppressive system where human labor is extracted under coercion without monetary compensation or adequate subsistence.
    *   **Structural Violence:** Major Bastian treated tribal labor as disposable property. Refusal to work was met with physical violence, starvation, and destruction of dwelling units.
    *   **Psychological Impact:** Beyond physical exertion, *Begari* systematically eroded the personal dignity (*स्वाभिमान*) of tribal communities.

### 🛡️ PRINCIPLES OF GUERRILLA WARFARE (छापामार युद्ध के सिद्धांत)

1.  **[BLUE] Law of Terrain Asymmetry (भौगोलिक लाभ का नियम):**
    *   The dense forests (*ऐजेंसियों के जंगल*) provided cover that invalidated the conventional artillery and superior numerical strength of the British army.
    *   Koya tribals used traditional knowledge of hidden paths, natural caves, and camouflage.

2.  **[BLUE] Law of Strategic Raids (सामरिक छापे का नियम):**
    *   **Target Selection:** Police stations containing guns, ammunition, and gunpowder (*चिंतापल्ली, कृष्णदेवीपेटा, रंपाचोडवरम*).
    *   **Execution Speed:** Rapid surprise attacks followed by immediate withdrawal into inaccessible forest depths.

3.  **[BLUE] Law of Popular Intelligence (जन-सूचना सिद्धांत):**
    *   Every village tribal functioned as an undetected intelligence gatherer for Raju.
    *   British troop movements were signaled across mountain ridges long before soldiers arrived, rendering British ambushes ineffective.

## 📌 PAGE 5: PAGE-BY-PAGE TEXTBOOK HIGHLIGHTS & CORE FACTOIDS

### 📖 TEXTBOOK CHRONOLOGICAL MAPPING

#### **Section 1: The Oppression at Gudem Hills (गुडेम पहाड़ियों में अत्याचार)**
*   **[GOLD] Core Fact:** The British administration initiated the construction of a major road traversing the Gudem hills.
*   **[RED] Key Character Action:** Major Bastian, Tahsildar of Gudem sub-division, summoned local village heads (**मुक़द्दम**) and ordered them to provide laborers without any wage (*बेगार*).
*   **[BLUE] Tribal Reaction:** Tribal headmen like **गाम मल्लूट डोरा (Gam Mallu Dora)** and **गाम गंंटम डोरा (Gam Gantam Dora)** were terrified of British power but deeply wounded by the insult to their self-respect.
*   **[GREEN] Crucial Dialogue/Event:** Bastian shouted at the tribals, demanding labor immediately. When tribals expressed concern over lack of food and wages, Bastian threatened severe physical violence.

#### **Section 2: The Emergence of Alluri Sitarama Raju (राजू का आगमन)**
*   **[GOLD] Background Details:**
    *   Age: Arrived in Gudem hills at barely **18 years** old.
    *   Origins: Hailed from Andhra region; possessed high school education.
    *   Qualities: Deep knowledge of **Sanskrit, Telugu, English, Medicine (जड़ी-बूटी)**, and **Astrology**.
*   **[BLUE] Impact on Tribals:** He lived among tribals, treated their illnesses with forest herbs, and listened to their grievances. Tribals revered him as an incarnation of divine justice.

#### **Section 3: The Outbreak of Armed Rebellion (क्रांति का विस्फोट - अगस्त १९२२)**
*   **[GOLD] First Tactical Strike:** On **22 August 1922**, Raju led a force of tribals and attacked the **Chintapalli Police Station (चिंतापल्ली थाना)**.
*   **[RED] Raid Inventory:** Captured **26 guns (26 राइफ़लें)** and thousands of rounds of ammunition.
*   **[BLUE] Subsequent Raids:**
    *   **23 August 1922:** Attack on **Krishnadevipeta Police Station** (कृष्णदेवीपेटा थाना).
    *   **24 August 1922:** Attack on **Rampachodavaram Police Station** (रंपाचोडवरम थाना).
*   **[GREEN] Historical Protocol:** Raju personally maintained a ledger/register signing details of weapons liberated from British stations.

#### **Section 4: The British Counter-Offensive & Rutherford's Arrival (रदरफ़ोर्ड का दमन चक्र)**
*   **[RED] Major Military Deployment:** British deployed elite units including the **Assam Rifles (असम राइफ़ल्स)** and Malabar Special Police.
*   **[GOLD] Appointment of Rutherford:** Realizing Major Bastian's failure, the British government appointed **Rutherford** as Special Commissioner to suppress the rebellion.
*   **[BLUE] Brutal Collective Punishment:** Rutherford cut off supply routes, burned down entire tribal villages, destroyed grain stores, and tortured families to force Raju's capture.

#### **Section 5: Martyrdom of Raju (सर्वोच्च बलिदान - ७ मई १९२४)**
*   **[RED] The Ultimate Sacrifice:** Raju saw the intense suffering of his tribal followers due to Rutherford's scorched-earth strategy. To save his people from systematic starvation, Raju decided to surrender.
*   **[GOLD] Capture Event:** On **7 May 1924**, Raju was captured while near a river stream by Major Goodall's patrol.
*   **[RED] Execution:** Tied to a tree at **Koyyuru** village and shot dead without any trial under orders of British officers.
*   **[GREEN] Final Legacy:** Raju attained martyrdom at the young age of **27 years**, leaving behind an immortal legacy of freedom fighting.
