• **Chapter Overview:** 'चतुर चित्रकार' (The Clever Painter) is a humorous and didactic poem written by Ramnaresh Tripathi.
• **Core Theme:** The poem highlights the importance of presence of mind, wit, and intelligence when facing dangerous situations.
• **Protagonist:** The central character is a painter who is highly skilled in his craft and possesses a sharp, quick-thinking mind.
• **Setting:** The story is set in a dense, mysterious forest where the painter has gone to capture the beauty of nature on his canvas.
• **The Conflict:** The painter’s peaceful activity is interrupted by the sudden arrival of a ferocious lion.
• **Character Trait (Painter):** He is portrayed as an artist who loves nature and finds inspiration in the scenic beauty of the woods.
• **Character Trait (Lion):** The lion represents a lethal threat, described as a king of the forest who is hungry and dangerous.
• **Initial Reaction:** Upon seeing the lion, the painter experiences a moment of intense fear but quickly regains his composure.
• **Strategic Thinking:** The painter realizes that physical strength cannot defeat the lion, so he decides to use his intellect.
• **The Deception:** The painter pretends to be calm and tells the lion that he is there to paint a portrait of the king of the forest.
• **Artistic Pretext:** He convinces the lion to sit in a specific pose so that he can "paint" his picture accurately.
• **The Lion's Vanity:** The lion, blinded by his own ego and desire to be immortalized in a painting, agrees to the painter's request.
• **The Setup:** The painter asks the lion to sit quietly and look towards the other side, away from the canvas.
• **The Escape Plan:** While the lion is busy posing, the painter starts looking for a way to escape the forest safely.
• **Psychological Maneuver:** The painter uses the lion's pride against him, making him feel important and superior.
• **Literary Tone:** The poem uses a lighthearted and rhythmic tone to convey a serious lesson about survival.
• **Vocabulary Focus:** The poem introduces descriptive Hindi adjectives related to nature and animal behavior.
• **Moral Foundation:** The chapter teaches students that intelligence is a greater weapon than physical force.
