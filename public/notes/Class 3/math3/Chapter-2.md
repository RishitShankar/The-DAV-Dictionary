• **Addition**: The mathematical process of combining two or more numbers or quantities to find their total sum.
• **Sum**: The final result obtained after performing the operation of addition.
• **Addends**: The individual numbers that are being added together in an addition problem.
• **Plus Sign (+)**: The mathematical symbol used to represent the operation of addition.
• **Equals Sign (=)**: The symbol used to denote that the values on both sides of the equation are equivalent.
• **Place Value**: The value represented by a digit based on its position in a number (Ones, Tens, Hundreds).
• **Ones Place**: The rightmost position in a number, representing units from 0 to 9.
• **Tens Place**: The position to the left of the ones place, representing groups of ten.
• **Hundreds Place**: The position to the left of the tens place, representing groups of one hundred.
• **Column Addition**: A method of adding numbers by aligning them vertically according to their place values.
• **Alignment**: The process of ensuring that digits of the same place value are placed in the same column before adding.
• **Addition without Regrouping**: Adding numbers where the sum of digits in each column is 9 or less.
• **Addition with Regrouping**: Adding numbers where the sum of digits in a column is 10 or more, requiring the carry-over process.
• **Carrying Over**: The process of moving a value from a lower place value column to the next higher place value column.
• **Regrouping**: Converting 10 ones into 1 ten, or 10 tens into 1 hundred.
• **Mental Addition**: The ability to add numbers quickly in one's head without using paper or pencil.
• **Number Line**: A visual representation of numbers on a straight line used to demonstrate the concept of "counting forward" for addition.
• **Commutative Property**: The mathematical rule stating that changing the order of addends does not change the sum (e.g., 2+3 = 3+2).
