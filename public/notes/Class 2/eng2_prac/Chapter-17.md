• **Wing Structure:** The wings are designed for quick, agile flight, allowing them to escape danger easily.

• **Territorial Behavior:** Sparrows may defend their nesting site from other birds.
• **Communication Range:** Their chirps can be heard over short distances, keeping the family connected.
