• **The Concept of 'My':** 'My' is a possessive adjective used to show that something belongs to the speaker.
• **Using 'My' in Sentences:** Examples include "My name is...", "My school is...", and "My bag is blue."
• **The Concept of 'Your':** 'Your' is used when addressing someone else, such as "What is your name?"
• **The Concept of 'Her':** 'Her' is used to refer to something belonging to a female, like "Sheetal brushes her teeth."
• **Pronoun Agreement:** Ensuring that the pronoun matches the gender of the person being discussed.
• **Subject-Verb Agreement:** Ensuring that the verb matches the singular subject (e.g., Sheetal wakes up, not Sheetal wake up).
• **The Verb 'Is':** 'Is' is used to describe a state or identity, such as "Sheetal is a student."
• **The Verb 'Has':** 'Has' is used to show possession, such as "Sheetal has a new bag."
• **Sentence Expansion:** Adding details to sentences to make them more interesting, e.g., "Sheetal has a red bag."
• **Using Adjectives:** Placing adjectives before nouns, like "a happy girl" or "a clean uniform."
• **Punctuation Marks:** The role of the comma in lists and the full stop at the end of sentences.
• **Capitalization:** Why we capitalize the first letter of a sentence and proper nouns.
• **Word Families:** Grouping words that sound similar to help with spelling and reading.
• **Synonyms (Basic):** Learning that 'happy' and 'cheerful' can mean similar things.
• **Antonyms (Basic):** Learning that 'clean' is the opposite of 'dirty'.
