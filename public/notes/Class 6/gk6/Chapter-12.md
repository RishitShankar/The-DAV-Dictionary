• **Vocabulary Enrichment**: The chapter introduces several key Hindi terms and phrases that enhance students' linguistic proficiency.
• **Term: कीमत (Keemat)**: Meaning 'Price'—the central monetary value attached to the ten mangoes around which the entire conflict revolves.
• **Term: ईमानदारी (Eandari)**: Meaning 'Honesty'—the foundational virtue exhibited by the fruit vendor throughout the ordeal.
• **Term: चालाकी (Chaalaki)**: Meaning 'Cunningness' or 'Trickery'—the negative trait displayed by the customer to evade payment.
• **Term: न्याय (Nyaya)**: Meaning 'Justice'—the fair resolution delivered by the wise judge at the conclusion of the trial.
• **Term: गवाह (Gawah)**: Meaning 'Witness'—individuals or evidence present to corroborate the facts of the dispute.
• **Term: समझदारी (Samajhdari)**: Meaning 'Wisdom' or 'Sensibility'—the quality used by the judge to untangle the deceptive web.
