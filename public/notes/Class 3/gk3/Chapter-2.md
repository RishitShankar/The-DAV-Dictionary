• **Chapter Overview:** The chapter "चतुर कौवा" (The Clever Crow) is a classic moral fable integrated into the DAV Kid Wiz curriculum to teach students about problem-solving, persistence, and intelligence.
• **Core Theme:** The story revolves around a thirsty crow who uses his cognitive abilities to overcome a survival challenge.
• **Character Profile:** The crow is depicted as an intelligent, observant, and determined protagonist.
• **Setting:** The narrative takes place on a hot summer day, emphasizing the environmental challenge of extreme heat.
• **Primary Conflict:** The crow experiences intense thirst and struggles to find water in a dry landscape.
• **Cognitive Skill:** The crow demonstrates 'Problem Solving' by analyzing his surroundings instead of giving up.
• **Environmental Context:** The story highlights the importance of water as a vital resource for all living beings.
• **Observation:** The crow spots a pitcher (घड़ा) from a distance, which represents the first step in his survival strategy.
• **Initial Obstacle:** Upon reaching the pitcher, the crow discovers that the water level is too low for his beak to reach.
• **Critical Thinking:** The crow does not abandon the pitcher; he assesses the physical constraints of the vessel.
• **Scientific Principle:** The story subtly introduces the concept of 'Displacement'—an object occupying space in a liquid causes the liquid level to rise.
• **Strategic Planning:** The crow identifies small pebbles (कंकड़) nearby as the tools required to solve his problem.
• **Execution:** The crow begins the laborious process of picking up pebbles one by one and dropping them into the pitcher.
• **Persistence:** The crow shows 'Perseverance' by continuing his task despite the physical effort required.
• **Outcome:** As the pebbles accumulate at the bottom, the water level gradually rises toward the rim.
• **Success:** The crow successfully reaches the water and quenches his thirst, proving that intelligence overcomes adversity.
• **Moral Lesson:** The chapter teaches students that "Where there is a will, there is a way" (जहाँ चाह, वहाँ राह).
• **Educational Value:** It encourages children to think creatively when faced with difficult situations.
