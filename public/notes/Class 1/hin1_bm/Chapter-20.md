• **Step 1 - Preparation:** The first phase involves gathering all necessary ingredients and tools.
• **Step 2 - Cleaning:** The carrots must be washed under running water to ensure they are free from dirt.
• **Step 3 - Peeling:** Using a peeler or knife, the thin outer layer of the carrot is removed.
• **Step 4 - Grating:** The carrots are grated into fine, uniform pieces to ensure even cooking.
• **Step 5 - Heating:** The pan is placed on the stove, and ghee is added to heat up.
• **Step 6 - Sautéing:** The grated carrots are added to the hot ghee and stirred until they soften.
• **Step 7 - Milk Addition:** Milk is poured into the pan, submerging the carrots to facilitate boiling.
• **Step 8 - Boiling:** The mixture is boiled until the milk thickens and coats the carrots.
• **Step 9 - Sweetening:** Sugar is added according to taste, which further releases moisture from the carrots.
• **Step 10 - Reduction:** The mixture is cooked until all excess liquid evaporates, leaving a thick consistency.
• **Step 11 - Flavoring:** Cardamom powder is sprinkled to add a distinct, aromatic flavor.
• **Step 12 - Finishing:** Dry fruits are added to provide texture and richness to the dessert.
• **Step 13 - Serving:** The Halwa is transferred to a serving dish.
• **Step 14 - Presentation:** It is garnished with chopped nuts for a professional look.
• **Step 15 - Consumption:** The dish is enjoyed while warm, completing the culinary cycle.

• **Key Term: गाजर (Gajar):** A root vegetable, orange in color, used as the main ingredient.
• **Key Term: हलवा (Halwa):** A sweet, dense dessert made by cooking ingredients in milk and sugar.
• **Key Term: कद्दूकस (Kaddukas):** A kitchen tool with sharp edges used for grating vegetables.
• **Key Term: घिसना (Ghisna):** The action of rubbing a vegetable against a grater to create shreds.
• **Key Term: कड़ाही (Kadahi):** A deep, circular cooking vessel used for frying and cooking.
• **Key Term: घी (Ghee):** Clarified butter, used as a cooking fat for its rich flavor.
• **Key Term: इलायची (Elaichi):** A spice used for its strong, sweet, and aromatic flavor.
• **Key Term: बादाम (Badam):** An edible seed of a tree, used as a garnish for its crunch.
• **Key Term: काजू (Kaju):** A kidney-shaped seed, used for its creamy texture and taste.
• **Key Term: चीनी (Cheeni):** A crystalline substance used to add sweetness to food.
• **Key Term: दूध (Doodh):** A white liquid produced by mammals, used as a base for the Halwa.
• **Key Term: पकाना (Pakana):** The process of applying heat to food to make it edible and tasty.
• **Key Term: स्वाद (Swad):** The flavor or taste of the food.
• **Key Term: गरम (Garam):** Having a high temperature, often used to describe freshly cooked food.
• **Key Term: परोसना (Parosna):** The act of serving food to others.
