•   **Personal Hygiene: Brushing Teeth:** A significant part of the "राजा बेटा" poem often focuses on the importance of brushing teeth immediately after waking up.

•   **Fresh Breath:** Brushing ensures fresh breath, which is important for social interactions and overall confidence.

•   **Correct Technique:** While not explicitly detailed in the poem, the concept of brushing all surfaces of the teeth (front, back, and chewing surfaces) is a related GK fact.
•   **Importance of Tongue Cleaning:** Some advanced versions might even touch upon cleaning the tongue to remove bacteria and improve breath.
•   **Dental Check-ups:** The broader GK concept related to oral hygiene includes regular visits to the dentist for check-ups and cleaning.
•   **Impact on Overall Health:** Poor oral hygiene can affect overall health, as germs from the mouth can sometimes lead to other infections in the body.
•   **Lifelong Habit:** Brushing teeth is a lifelong habit that, once established in childhood, contributes significantly to long-term health.
•   **Self-Care:** The act of brushing teeth is an early lesson in self-care, teaching children to look after their own bodies.
•   **Role of Fluoride:** Toothpaste often contains fluoride, a substance that strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent cavities, a useful GK fact for parents to explain.
