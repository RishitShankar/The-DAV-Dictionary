• **Chapter Introduction:** Welcome to Chapter 3 of the DAV Class UKG Integrated Activity Book, titled "Our Helpers, School and Community," designed to introduce young learners to the vital individuals and institutions that form the foundation of their daily lives.
• **Core Concept of Community:** A community is defined as a social group of people living in the same place, sharing a common environment, and depending on one another for mutual support, safety, and well-being.
• **Role of Community Helpers:** Community helpers are essential workers, professionals, and service providers who dedicate their time and skills to maintain the smooth functioning of society and meet our daily needs.
• **Interdependence:** The chapter emphasizes that no individual can live in isolation; every person in a community relies on the specialized skills and services of various helpers to lead a comfortable and secure life.
• **Introduction to the School Environment:** The school is highlighted as the primary community hub for a child, serving as a second home where formal education, moral values, and social skills are cultivated.
• **Scope of Study:** This exhaustive academic note systematically categorizes all community helpers, school personnel, and civic institutions mentioned in the DAV Integrated Activity Book curriculum for holistic early childhood understanding.
• **Learning Objectives:** Young students are guided to recognize, respect, and appreciate the dignity of labor associated with every profession, fostering empathy and social awareness from an early age.
• **Visual Association:** The textbook utilizes pictorial representations to help UKG students visually identify community helpers through their distinctive uniforms, tools, and workplaces.
• **Vocabulary Building:** Key vocabulary words introduced in this chapter include helper, uniform, tool, hospital, police station, post office, school, teacher, doctor, and community.
• **Value Education Integration:** DAV curriculum standards integrate moral values into this chapter, teaching children to greet helpers politely, use magic words like "please" and "thank you," and treat everyone with equal respect.
• **Safety and Trust:** Children learn that community helpers such as police officers and firefighters are safe adults whom they can approach in times of emergency or distress.
• **Basic Economic Awareness:** The chapter subtly introduces the concept of services, explaining that helpers either provide a direct service or produce goods that benefit the entire neighborhood.
• **Structured Learning Pathway:** The 10-page study notes progress logically from individual community helpers to institutional support systems, culminating in a detailed study of the school ecosystem.
• **Pedagogical Approach:** The notes use simple, highly descriptive bullet points suitable for educators, parents, and academic review boards monitoring the DAV UKG curriculum standards.
• **Foundational Knowledge:** Mastery of this chapter prepares UKG students for advanced social studies concepts in higher primary grades by establishing a solid baseline of civic consciousness.

• **The Teacher:** The teacher is the primary educational helper inside the school, responsible for imparting literacy, numeracy, moral values, and creative skills to young learners.
• **Classroom Management:** Teachers create a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment where children can explore new concepts, engage in group activities, and build self-confidence.
• **The Principal:** The Principal is the administrative head of the school institution, ensuring that all academic and co-curricular operations run smoothly and efficiently.
• **School Safety Oversight:** The Principal, along with the administrative staff, implements strict safety protocols, supervises staff, and maintains discipline across the entire school campus.
• **The School Guard/Watchman:** The school guard or watchman is stationed at the main entrance to monitor all visitors, regulate entry and exit, and ensure the physical security of students and staff.
• **Gatekeeping Duties:** The guard checks identity cards, prevents unauthorized persons from entering the premises, and safeguards school property against theft or trespassing.
• **The School Cleaner/Sweeper:** Cleaners and housekeeping staff maintain hygiene across classrooms, corridors, restrooms, and playgrounds, preventing the spread of germs and diseases.
• **Sanitation Standards:** School cleaners ensure that dustbins are emptied regularly, floors are swept and mopped, and washrooms remain sanitary throughout the school day.
• **The School Bus Driver:** The bus driver safely transports children from their homes to school and back, strictly adhering to traffic rules, speed limits, and designated routes.
• **Transportation Safety:** Drivers undergo rigorous background checks and training to ensure passenger safety, handling vehicle maintenance and emergency road situations professionally.
• **The Bus Conductor / Attendant:** The bus conductor assists children while boarding and alighting from the vehicle, ensuring they are seated securely and managing bags.
• **Passenger Care:** Conductors keep order inside the school bus, help children cross roads safely after drop-off, and maintain attendance records of travelers.
• **The School Librarian:** The librarian manages the school library, cataloging books, maintaining a quiet reading atmosphere, and helping children choose age-appropriate storybooks.
• **The School Nurse / Medical Attendant:** The nurse provides immediate first aid for minor cuts, scrapes, or sudden illnesses, notifying parents and administering prescribed basic care.
• **Support Staff Value:** Every school worker, from the gardener to the administrative clerk, plays an indispensable role in making the educational institution a welcoming second home.

• **The Doctor:** A doctor is a vital healthcare professional who examines sick patients, diagnoses illnesses, prescribes medicines, and guides individuals on maintaining good physical health.
• **The Hospital Environment:** Doctors work primarily in hospitals and clinics, utilizing diagnostic instruments such as stethoscopes, thermometers, and blood pressure monitors.
• **Preventive Care:** Beyond treating diseases, doctors administer vaccinations and educate community members on hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention strategies.
• **The Nurse:** Nurses work alongside doctors in hospitals and clinics, providing direct patient care, monitoring vital signs, and administering medications as prescribed.
• **Compassionate Caretakers:** Nurses offer emotional support and physical comfort to hospitalized patients, dressing wounds and ensuring patient comfort around the clock.
• **The Chemist / Pharmacist:** A pharmacist is a healthcare professional who dispenses prescribed medications, checks dosages, and advises customers on the correct usage of pharmaceutical products.
• **Medicine Management:** Pharmacists maintain an inventory of life-saving drugs, syrups, and medical supplies, ensuring that quality standards are strictly met.
• **The Dentist:** A dentist specializes in oral hygiene, examining teeth and gums, filling cavities, performing cleanings, and educating children on proper brushing techniques.
• **Dental Prevention:** Dentists teach young learners the importance of reducing sugary food consumption to prevent tooth decay and maintain a bright, healthy smile.
• **The Police Officer:** Police officers maintain law and order, protect citizens from harm, investigate crimes, and ensure public safety across neighborhoods and commercial areas.
• **Traffic Control:** Traffic police officers regulate vehicular movement on busy roads, prevent traffic jams, and penalize drivers who violate road safety regulations.
• **Emergency Assistance:** Citizens can dial emergency helpline numbers to summon police assistance during thefts, accidents, or any security threat.
• **The Firefighter:** Firefighters bravely extinguish accidental fires in buildings, forests, and vehicles, using high-pressure water hoses, ladders, and specialized protective gear.
• **Rescue Operations:** Beyond fire suppression, firefighters rescue trapped individuals from burning structures, car crashes, and natural disasters, acting as frontline heroes.
• **Fire Safety Awareness:** Firefighters visit schools and communities to educate people on fire prevention, stop-drop-and-roll techniques, and safe evacuation procedures.

• **The Postman / Mail Carrier:** The postman is a trusted community helper who collects, sorts, and delivers letters, greeting cards, parcels, and official documents to our doorsteps.
• **Postal Network:** Postmen connect distant families and businesses by maintaining a reliable communication link through the national postal service network.
• **Uniform Identification:** Traditionally recognized by their khaki uniforms and leather mailbags, postmen navigate designated neighborhood routes in all weather conditions.
• **The Banker:** Bankers work in financial institutions, managing customer savings, processing deposits and withdrawals, and providing loans for business and personal growth.
• **Financial Literacy:** Bankers teach the community the importance of saving money securely for future needs, education, and emergencies.
• **The Grocer / Shopkeeper:** The grocer owns and operates the neighborhood grocery store, supplying daily essentials such as grains, pulses, oil, spices, and household items.
• **Retail Convenience:** Local grocers save community members time and travel by making essential food items easily accessible within walking distance.
• **The Milkman / Dairy Supplier:** The milkman delivers fresh milk and dairy products directly to households early in the morning, ensuring families have nutritious breakfast supplies.
• **Supply Chain Consistency:** Milk suppliers coordinate with dairy farms to maintain an uninterrupted daily supply chain regardless of weather or seasonal changes.
• **The Baker:** A baker prepares and sells fresh bread, cakes, pastries, biscuits, and cookies, using ovens, flour, sugar, and various baking ingredients.
• **Culinary Art:** Bakers combine creativity and culinary science to provide celebratory treats for birthdays, anniversaries, and community festivals.
• **The Tailor:** A tailor measures, cuts, stitches, and alters fabrics to create well-fitting clothing, school uniforms, and traditional attire for community members.
• **Garment Repair:** Tailors also mend torn clothing and adjust sizes, promoting sustainability by extending the usable lifespan of textiles.
• **The Cobbler:** A cobbler repairs damaged shoes, sandals, boots, and leather goods using specialized awls, hammers, threads, and strong adhesives.
• **Footwear Maintenance:** Cobblers save consumers money by restoring worn-out footwear rather than forcing them to purchase brand-new items.

• **The Plumber:** A plumber installs, repairs, and maintains piping systems for drinking water, sewage, and drainage in residential and commercial buildings.
• **Water System Integrity:** Plumbers fix leaking faucets, burst pipes, blocked drains, and malfunctioning water heaters to ensure continuous access to clean water.
• **The Electrician:** An electrician deals with electrical wiring, circuit breakers, switches, lighting fixtures, and electrical appliances in homes and offices.
• **Electrical Safety:** Electricians handle dangerous electrical currents with specialized insulated tools, preventing short circuits, shocks, and fire hazards.
• **The Carpenter:** A carpenter works with wood to design, construct, and repair furniture such as beds, tables, chairs, cabinets, doors, and window frames.
• **Woodworking Craftsmanship:** Carpenters use saws, hammers, chisels, measuring tapes, and sandpaper to transform raw timber into functional, beautiful household goods.
• **The Painter:** A painter applies decorative coats of paint, varnish, and wallpaper to the interior and exterior walls of houses and buildings.
• **Aesthetic Enhancement:** Painters protect building surfaces from weathering and moisture damage while beautifying living spaces with vibrant colors.
• **The Gardener / Mali:** A gardener plants, waters, prunes, and maintains lawns, flowers, shrubs, and trees in public parks and private gardens.
• **Environmental Care:** Gardeners enhance neighborhood greenery, control weed growth, and cultivate blooming flowers that purify the air and uplift community moods.
• **The Sweeper / Garbage Collector:** Sanitation workers sweep streets, collect household garbage, and manage waste disposal to keep neighborhoods clean and disease-free.
• **Public Health Protection:** Garbage collectors prevent vector-borne diseases by ensuring that waste does not accumulate in open public spaces.
• **The Barber / Hairdresser:** A barber or hairdresser cuts, styles, and grooms hair, providing personal care services to men, women, and children.
• **Hygiene and Grooming:** Regular haircuts and grooming sessions help individuals maintain a neat, professional, and clean personal appearance.
• **Community Interconnectedness:** The diverse array of trade workers proves that every profession, whether white-collar or blue-collar, holds immense dignity and importance.

• **Definition of a School:** A school is formally defined as an institution designed for learning where young students receive instruction from trained educators in a structured environment.
• **The School Building:** The physical structure of a school comprises multiple classrooms, administrative offices, a library, a playground, restrooms, and activity rooms.
• **Classrooms:** Classrooms are dedicated spaces equipped with blackboards, smartboards, student desks, chairs, and educational charts where daily lessons take place.
• **The Playground:** The playground is an outdoor recreational area where children play sports, run, climb, and develop gross motor skills during recess and physical education.
• **Assembly Ground:** The school assembly ground is where students gather every morning for prayers, national anthems, announcements, and physical exercise routines.
• **The Computer Lab:** Modern schools feature computer labs where students are introduced to basic digital literacy, educational software, and keyboarding skills.
• **The Art and Craft Room:** Art rooms provide creative spaces equipped with paints, clay, crayons, and craft paper for children to express their imagination visually.
• **The Music and Dance Room:** Specialized rooms allow students to explore rhythm, melody, traditional dances, and singing under the guidance of performing arts teachers.
• **The Medical Room / Sick Bay:** A designated quiet room with a bed and first-aid supplies is maintained to care for students who feel unwell during school hours.
• **School Rules and Discipline:** Schools enforce rules such as punctuality, wearing clean uniforms, raising hands before speaking, and respecting peers to maintain order.
• **Sharing and Caring:** The school environment teaches children vital social lessons about sharing toys, taking turns, helping classmates, and resolving minor conflicts peacefully.
• **Extracurricular Activities:** Schools organize sports meets, annual days, drawing competitions, and quizzes to nurture hidden talents beyond academics.
• **School Identity:** Every school has a distinct name, emblem, motto, uniform color code, and school song that foster a deep sense of pride and belonging among students.
• **Punctuality and Routine:** Attending school daily establishes healthy lifelong habits of time management, discipline, and structured daily routines.
• **Holistic Growth:** The DAV school philosophy emphasizes physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual growth, transforming young children into responsible future citizens.

• **Definition of a Community:** A community expands beyond the immediate family and school to include all the neighbors, local businesses, and public facilities in our surrounding area.
• **Neighborhood Living:** Living in a community requires cooperation, mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, and consideration for the rights and comfort of neighbors.
• **Public Parks:** Public parks are community green spaces equipped with swings, slides, jogging tracks, and benches where families relax and children play safely.
• **Parks Maintenance:** Community members and local municipal authorities share the responsibility of keeping public parks clean, litter-free, and safe for everyone.
• **Local Markets:** Local markets are commercial hubs consisting of various shops, vegetable vendors, and grocery stores where families purchase daily necessities.
• **The Hospital / Dispensary:** A neighborhood hospital provides 24-hour medical emergency care, specialized treatments, and health check-ups for community residents.
• **The Post Office Institution:** The local post office handles mail sorting, savings schemes, parcel bookings, and money transfers for the entire neighborhood.
• **The Police Station:** The local police station acts as the command center for law enforcement, crime prevention, and immediate emergency response in the area.
• **The Fire Station:** Strategically located fire stations house fire engines and trained firefighting crews ready to respond instantly to fire emergencies across the locality.
• **Places of Worship:** Temples, gurdwaras, mosques, and churches serve as spiritual community centers where people gather for prayer, peace, and social service.
• **Community Helpers in Action:** Residents interact with electricians, plumbers, sweepers, and delivery personnel daily, keeping the local infrastructure running smoothly.
• **Festivals and Celebrations:** Communities celebrate festivals like Diwali, Eid, Christmas, and Gurpurab together through community meals, dances, and cultural events.
• **Social Harmony:** Celebrating diverse cultural festivals fosters religious tolerance, unity, and communal harmony among residents of different backgrounds.
• **Civic Responsibilities:** Citizens demonstrate civic duty by not littering public roads, conserving water, planting trees, and respecting community property.
• **Neighborly Support:** Neighbors assist each other during emergencies, accept parcels during travel absences, and look out for one another's overall safety.

• **Understanding Community Services:** Community services are essential facilities and programs provided by the government or local authorities to enhance the quality of life for all residents.
• **Public Transport Systems:** Buses, metro trains, and public transit systems help community members commute affordably and efficiently across different parts of the city.
• **Waste Management Services:** Municipal waste collection trucks ensure that household garbage is systematically hauled away to processing or recycling plants daily.
• **Street Lighting:** Municipal authorities install and maintain streetlights along roads and pathways to ensure night-time visibility and deter criminal activity.
• **Water Supply Infrastructure:** Public water treatment plants pump treated, purified drinking water through underground pipelines directly to residential taps.
• **Electricity Grid Services:** Power generation and distribution companies maintain transformers and power lines to ensure uninterrupted electricity flow to homes and schools.
• **Public Libraries:** Local public libraries offer free access to books, magazines, and study areas, promoting lifelong reading habits across all age groups.
• **Civic Parks and Recreation:** City planners develop sports complexes, swimming pools, and community centers to encourage healthy, active lifestyles among citizens.
• **Animal Care and Veterinary Services:** Veterinary clinics and animal shelters care for stray and pet animals, providing medical treatments and promoting animal welfare.
• **Disaster Management Units:** Specialized emergency response teams are deployed by civic authorities to manage floods, earthquakes, and other natural calamities.
• **Road and Bridge Maintenance:** Public works departments repair potholes, repave roads, and maintain bridges to ensure safe vehicular and pedestrian travel.
• **Traffic Signals and Signage:** Automated traffic lights and road signs are installed at intersections to organize traffic flow and prevent road accidents.
• **Public Health Campaigns:** Local health departments organize vaccination camps, cleanliness drives, and awareness programs against diseases like dengue and malaria.
• **Sanitation and Drainage Maintenance:** Workers clear blocked storm drains and sewers regularly to prevent waterlogging during heavy monsoon rains.
• **Interdependent Ecosystem:** The seamless functioning of community services proves that every citizen depends on a vast network of unseen public workers.

• **Dignity of Labour Concept:** The DAV curriculum places strong emphasis on teaching young students the dignity of labor, asserting that every job is honorable and deserves equal respect.
• **Equal Value of Professions:** Whether a person is a doctor saving lives or a sweeper keeping streets clean, both contributions are equally vital for a healthy society.
• **Eradicating Social Bias:** Children are taught to shed prejudices associated with manual labor, understanding that physical work requires skill, endurance, and dedication.
• **Gratitude Towards Helpers:** Students learn to express heartfelt gratitude to school helpers, domestic staff, and delivery workers through polite words and appreciative gestures.
• **Teamwork in Society:** Just like puzzle pieces, every helper fits into a specific societal role; removing any single profession causes disruption in daily life.
• **Child-Friendly Classroom Discussions:** Teachers facilitate interactive role-play activities where UKG students dress up as doctors, postmen, teachers, and policemen.
• **Experiential Learning:** Role-playing helps young learners understand the specific tools, uniforms, and duties associated with each unique profession.
• **Appreciation Cards:** Students create handmade "Thank You" cards for school guards, cleaners, and bus drivers to internalize the value of gratitude.
• **Audio-Visual Aids:** Educational videos and flashcards depicting various community helpers are utilized in classrooms to reinforce visual recognition and memory.
• **Storytelling Integration:** Moral stories about helpful animals or cooperative villagers are narrated to teach the core message of mutual assistance and community spirit.
• **Interactive Quizzes:** Fun guessing games (e.g., "Who am I? I bring your letters. Answer: Postman") test comprehension and enhance vocabulary retention.
• **Parental Involvement:** Parents are encouraged to discuss their respective professions with children at home, broadening their understanding of adult work roles.
• **Moral Responsibility:** DAV values instill in children the understanding that as they grow, they too must contribute positively to their community and nation.
• **Empathy Development:** Learning about helpers who work in harsh weather conditions (like sweepers and traffic police) cultivates deep empathy in young minds.
