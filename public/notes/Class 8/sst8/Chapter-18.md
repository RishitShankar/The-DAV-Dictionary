## 🔬 FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES & THEORY DEEP-DIVE

### 🌋 [RED] Theory of Plate Tectonics
* **Lithospheric Structure:** The Earth's outermost shell, the lithosphere (crust and uppermost solid mantle), is fractured into several major and minor rigid slabs called **Tectonic Plates**.
* **Asthenosphere Dynamics:** These plates float on the partially molten, ductile upper mantle layer known as the **Asthenosphere**.
* **Convection Currents:** Heat generated from radioactive decay deep within the Earth's core creates slow circular convection currents within the mantle.
* **Plate Boundary Interactions:**
  * 🔴 **Convergent Boundaries:** Plates collide. One plate slips underneath another (subduction zone) or pushes upward, building mountain ranges (e.g., Indian Plate colliding with Eurasian Plate forming the Himalayas).
  * 🔵 **Divergent Boundaries:** Plates pull apart from each other, creating new crust as magma rises (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge).
  * 🟢 **Transform Boundaries:** Plates slide horizontally past one another along strike-slip faults (e.g., San Andreas Fault).

---

### ⚡ Elastic Rebound Theory
* **Proposed By:** Harry Fielding Reid (1910) following the 1906 San Francisco earthquake.
* **Mechanism of Strain Build-up:** As tectonic plates move, friction prevents them from sliding smoothly past each other. The rocks along fault planes lock together, but continuous plate movement subjects them to immense strain.
* **Deformation:** Rocks bend and deform elastically, storing strain energy over decades or centuries.
* **Rupture Point:** When the stored elastic strain exceeds the ultimate strength (yield limit) of the rock, sudden fracturing occurs along the fault line.
* **Energy Release:** The fractured rocks snap back (rebound) into an undeformed position. The stored strain energy is instantaneously released in the form of shockwaves—**Seismicity**.

---

### 🌊 [GREEN] Mechanism of Seismic Wave Propagation
* **Elastic Shockwaves:** Released energy travels outward in concentric spherical wave fronts from the point of fracture.
* **Medium Perturbation:** The kinetic energy vibrates the rock particles, transmitting displacement energy across elastic body media (Earth's mantle and crust).
* **Wave Transformation:** Body waves travel through Earth’s interior, but upon reaching the free surface of the planet, part of their energy converts into high-amplitude surface waves.

---

### 🏗️ Anthropogenic & Secondary Triggers
* **Reservoir-Induced Seismicity (RIS):** Impounding massive volumes of water behind mega-dams exerts mechanical weight (hydrostatic pressure) and increases pore fluid pressure in underlying faults, triggering earthquakes (e.g., Koyna Earthquake, Maharashtra, 1967).
* **Mining & Tunnelling:** Deep underground mining creates voids that lead to sudden roof collapse or stress adjustments, causing localized rockbursts.
* **Nuclear Explosions:** Underground detonations release shock waves equivalent to moderate tectonic earthquakes.
