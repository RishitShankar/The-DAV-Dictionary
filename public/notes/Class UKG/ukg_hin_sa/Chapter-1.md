• **Chapter Title:** आओ दोहराएँ (व्यंजन: क से ह तक) marks the foundational revision phase for Upper Kindergarten (UKG) students under the DAV Dayanand Anglo-Vedic curriculum.
• **Subject Context:** Shabd Abhyas (शब्द अभ्यास) focuses on building Hindi vocabulary, proper pronunciation, and visual recognition of letters and words.
• **Core Objective:** The primary goal of this chapter is to systematically revise all Hindi consonants (व्यंजन - Vyanjan) ranging sequentially from 'क' to 'ह'.
• **Curriculum Alignment:** Designed strictly according to the DAV Public School Board guidelines for early childhood linguistic development in the regional language.
• **Phonetic Foundation:** Emphasizes correct articulation, phonetic sounds, and mouth placement for each consonant to ensure speech clarity in young learners.
• **Visual Recognition:** Connects each Hindi consonant with clear pictorial representations to enhance cognitive association between letters and physical objects.
• **Stroke Order Mastery:** Reinforces proper writing direction, pencil grip, and sequential stroke formation for every single consonant from 'क' to 'ह'.
• **Sequential Learning:** Follows the traditional, highly structured alphabetical order of the Devanagari script to build systematic memory retention.
• **Vocabulary Building:** Introduces basic, everyday Hindi words associated with each consonant to expand the active vocabulary of Class UKG students.
• **Cognitive Development:** Promotes visual-spatial skills through tracing, matching, and identifying letters amidst grid structures and picture banks.
• **Bilingual Transition:** Bridges spoken Hindi comprehension with written symbols, helping children connect oral language to visual text.
• **Foundational Grammar Role:** Prepares students for advanced phonetic combinations, including matras (vowel signs) and conjunct consonants in later chapters.
• **Interactive Engagement:** Encourages multisensory learning where students see, speak, trace, and write each consonant repeatedly.
• **Teacher Facilitation Note:** Instructors are guided to use flashcards, blackboard demonstrations, and chanting methods during classroom delivery.
• **Parental Support Framework:** Encourages guided home revision using daily object association to reinforce classroom learning of consonants 'क' to 'ह'.
• **Assessment Preparation:** Lays the groundwork for formative evaluations by testing letter identification, sequence completion, and word association.

• **First Consonant:** **'क' (Ka)** is the starting letter of the primary Devanagari consonant series in the DAV Shabd Abhyas curriculum.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'क':** Produced as an unvoiced velar plosive sound, requiring a sharp release of breath from the back of the mouth.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'क':** Commonly taught words include **कमल (Kamal - Lotus)**, **कबूतर (Kabutar - Pigeon)**, and **कलम (Kalam - Pen)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **कमल (Lotus)** is universally used as the standard textbook visual cue for letter 'क' due to its cultural and aesthetic familiarity.
• **Stroke Formation for 'क':** Involves drawing a vertical standing line, followed by a loop on the left and a curve extending to the right, capped with a top horizontal line (Shirorekha).
• **Second Consonant:** **'ख' (Kha)** follows immediately after 'क' in the traditional consonant sequence.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ख':** An aspirated voiceless velar plosive sound, produced with an audible breath puff.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ख':** Standard teaching words include **खरगोश (Khargosh - Rabbit)**, **खटमल (Khatmal - Bedbug)**, and **खिड़की (Khidki - Window)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **खरगोश (Rabbit)** serves as the primary visual anchor for 'ख', helping children remember the letter through animal recognition.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ख':** Requires a curved body resembling the letter 'र', connected to a standing line with an internal loop.
• **Third Consonant:** **'ग' (Ga)** is the third letter of the Devanagari vyanjan varg (kavarg).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ग':** A voiced velar plosive sound generated with vocal cord vibration.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ग':** Frequently introduced words include **गमला (Gamla - Flowerpot)**, **गधा (Gadha - Donkey)**, and **गाय (Gaay - Cow)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **गमला (Flowerpot)** is the standard textbook image used to anchor the visual memory of 'ग' for young learners.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ग':** Consists of a short hanging curve attached to a full vertical standing line.
• **Shirorekha Rule:** Emphasizes that all letters ('क', 'ख', 'ग') must be completed with a neat, continuous top horizontal line.

• **Fourth Consonant:** **'घ' (Gha)** represents the fourth sound in the initial velar consonant group (कवर्ग) of the Hindi alphabet.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'घ':** An aspirated voiced velar plosive, requiring deeper vocalization and a controlled breath release.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'घ':** Standard textbook examples include **घर (Ghar - House)**, **घड़ी (Ghari - Clock)**, and **घास (Ghaas - Grass)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **घर (House)** and **घड़ी (Clock)** are utilized as primary visual symbols for 'घ' due to their frequent presence in a child's daily environment.
• **Stroke Formation for 'घ':** Formed by drawing two connected upward curves opening to the right, joined by a vertical standing line.
• **Fifth Consonant:** **'ङ' (Nga)** is the nasal consonant concluding the first set (कवर्ग) of the Devanagari script.
• **Usage Context in UKG:** While taught as part of the complete alphabet sequence, its standalone vocabulary is limited at the UKG level.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ङ':** A velar nasal sound produced by blocking airflow through the mouth and routing it through the nose.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ङ':** Involves a small left-facing hook followed by a distinct side dot or small curve configuration.
• **Transition to Second Group:** Marks the completion of the first five letters (कवर्ग), preparing students to transition to the palatal consonant series (चवर्ग).
• **Sixth Consonant:** **'च' (Cha)** initiates the second major consonant group known as 'चवर्ग' (Chavarg).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'च':** An unvoiced postalveolar affricate sound produced with the tongue touching the roof of the mouth.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'च':** Core learning words include **चम्मच (Chamch - Spoon)**, **चरखा (Charkha - Spinning Wheel)**, and **चूहा (Chuha - Mouse)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **चम्मच (Spoon)** is the classic visual representation for 'च' because every child interacts with dining utensils daily.
• **Stroke Formation for 'च':** Created by drawing a horizontal midline, a downward loop, and a straight vertical standing line.
• **Letter Continuity:** Reinforces the smooth pedagogical transition from throat sounds (velar) to palate sounds (palatal).

• **Seventh Consonant:** **'छ' (Chha)** is the second letter of the palatal consonant series (चवर्ग) in the Hindi alphabet.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'छ':** An aspirated unvoiced postalveolar affricate sound, characterized by a sharp burst of air.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'छ':** Essential DAV curriculum words include **छतरी (Chhatri - Umbrella)**, **छत (Chhat - Roof)**, and **छवि (Chhavi - Image)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **छतरी (Umbrella)** is universally recognized by UKG students as the definitive visual anchor for the letter 'छ'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'छ':** Requires drawing two adjoining bottom loops, a small upper twist, and a vertical standing line.
• **Eighth Consonant:** **'ज' (Ja)** occupies the third position in the palatal group (चवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ज':** A voiced postalveolar affricate sound produced with vocal cord vibration and tongue-palate contact.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ज':** Standard vocabulary words comprise **जहाज (Jahaj - Ship)**, **मग (Mag - Mug - variant contexts)**, and **जल (Jal - Water)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **जहाज (Ship)** or **जग (Jug)** serves as the primary illustrative image for 'ज' in early learners' textbooks.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ज':** Formed with a horizontal middle bar, a small loop, and a vertical standing line.
• **Ninth Consonant:** **'झ' (Jha)** is the fourth letter of the palatal vyanjan set.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'झ':** An aspirated voiced postalveolar affricate sound requiring forceful breath support.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'झ':** Key instructional words include **झंडा (Jhanda - Flag)**, **झूला (Jhula - Swing)**, and **झरना (Jhharna - Waterfall)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **झंडा (Flag)** and **झूला (Swing)** evoke strong national and recreational associations for children, aiding memory retention.
• **Stroke Formation for 'झ':** Involves intricate curves resembling the English letter 'S' connected to a vertical line via a horizontal tie.
• **Tenth Consonant:** **'ञ' (Nya)** is the nasal consonant that completes the palatal group (चवर्ग).

• **Eleventh Consonant:** **'ट' (Ta)** begins the third major consonant classification known as the retroflex group (टवर्ग - Tavarg).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ट':** A retroflex unvoiced plosive produced by curling the tip of the tongue back against the hard palate.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ट':** Primary curriculum words include **टमाटर (Tamatar - Tomato)**, **टब (Tab - Tub)**, and **टोपी (Topi - Cap)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **टमाटर (Tomato)** is the standard bright red icon used to visually anchor the letter 'ट' for young students.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ट':** Drawn as a small top hook extending into a semi-circular lower bowl.
• **Twelfth Consonant:** **'ठ' (Tha)** is the second letter of the retroflex series (टवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ठ':** An aspirated retroflex unvoiced plosive, uttered with a distinct burst of breath.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ठ':** Common teaching words include **ठेला (Thela - Cart)**, **ठग (Thag - Swindler/Thief)**, and **ठंड (Thand - Cold)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **ठेला (Cart)** represents the primary visual cue for 'ठ' in standard DAV primary texts.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ठ':** Consists of a small top hook closing into a full circular loop beneath the Shirorekha.
• **Thirteenth Consonant:** **'ड' (Da)** occupies the third slot in the retroflex consonant family.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ड':** A voiced retroflex plosive produced with vocal cord engagement and tongue curling.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ड':** Key words taught include **डमरू (Damru - Small Hand Drum)**, **डाकघर (Dakghar - Post Office)**, and **डंडा (Danda - Stick)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **डमरू (Damru)** is an iconic cultural instrument utilized as the visual symbol for 'ड'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ड':** Formed with a short vertical drop followed by a sweeping bottom curve turning inward.
• **Fourteenth Consonant:** **'ढ' (Dha)** is the fourth retroflex consonant in sequence.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ढ':** An aspirated voiced retroflex plosive.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ढ':** Standard words include **ढक्कन (Dhakkan - Lid)**, **ढोलक (Dholak - Hand Drum)**, and **ढाल (Dhaal - Shield)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **ढक्कन (Lid)** or **ढोलक (Drum)** provides the contextual image for learning 'ढ'.

• **Fifteenth Consonant:** **'ण' (Na)** is the retroflex nasal consonant that concludes the third consonant group (टवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ण':** A retroflex nasal sound produced by curling the tongue tip upward to touch the roof of the mouth while routing air through the nose.
• **Usage Context in UKG:** Emphasized for spelling and pronunciation accuracy in words derived from Sanskrit and formal Hindi.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ण':** Requires precise coordination of curved strokes and a vertical stem beneath the top line.
• **Sixteenth Consonant:** **'त' (Ta)** opens the fourth major classification, known as the dental consonant series (तवर्ग - Tavarg).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'त':** A dental unvoiced plosive produced by placing the tip of the tongue against the upper teeth.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'त':** Core educational words include **तरबूज (Tarbooj - Watermelon)**, **ताला (Tala - Lock)**, and **तारा (Tara - Star)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **तरबूज (Watermelon)** and **ताला (Lock)** serve as vibrant visual anchors for the letter 'त'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'त':** Comprises a vertical line with a central loop extending outward to form a smooth curve.
• **Seventeenth Consonant:** **'थ' (Tha)** is the second letter of the dental group (तवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'थ':** An aspirated dental unvoiced plosive generated with an audible stream of breath against the teeth.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'थ':** Standard vocabulary features **थर्मस (Thermos - Flask)**, **थैला (Thaila - Bag)**, and **थकान (Thakan - Fatigue)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **थर्मस (Flask)** and **थैला (Bag)** are everyday household items used as visual memory aids for 'थ'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'थ':** Features a looped head connected to a downward vertical line with an enclosed top curve.
• **Eighteenth Consonant:** **'द' (Da)** holds the third position in the dental consonant series.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'द':** A voiced dental plosive produced with vocal cord vibration and teeth-tongue contact.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'द':** Essential words include **दवात (Dawat - Inkpot)**, **दवा (Dawa - Medicine)**, and **दरवाजा (Darwaza - Door)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **दवात (Inkpot)** is the traditional textbook illustration associated with the letter 'द'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'द':** Formed by a top hook curving downward and looping backward to rest on the baseline.

• **Nineteenth Consonant:** **'ध' (Dha)** is the fourth letter of the dental consonant group (तवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ध':** An aspirated voiced dental plosive requiring controlled breath and vocal cord activation.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ध':** Prominent curriculum words include **धनुष (Dhanush - Bow)**, **धोबी (Dhobi - Washerman)**, and **धरती (Dharti - Earth)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **धनुष (Bow)** serves as the classic historical and visual representation for the letter 'ध'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ध':** Created by drawing a small looped head that opens into a downward curve, joined to a vertical standing line.
• **Twentieth Consonant:** **'न' (Na)** concludes the dental consonant group as the primary dental nasal sound.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'न':** A dental nasal sound produced with the tongue touching the upper teeth while air escapes through the nose.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'न':** Core learning words comprise **नल (Nal - Tap)**, **नयन (Nayan - Eye)**, and **नमस्ते (Namaste - Greetings)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **नल (Water Tap)** is universally recognized by young learners as the definitive visual anchor for 'न'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'न':** Consists of a horizontal loop mid-stroke connected to a clean vertical standing line.
• **Twenty-First Consonant:** **'प' (Pa)** inaugurates the fifth major consonant category, known as the labial group (पवर्ग - Pavarg).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'प':** An unvoiced bilabial plosive produced by pressing both lips together and releasing them with a burst of air.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'प':** Essential textbook words include **पतंग (Patang - Kite)**, **पेड़ (Ped - Tree)**, and **पानी (Paani - Water)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **पतंग (Kite)** is a vibrant, highly engaging visual cue that excites UKG students during the letter 'प' lesson.
• **Stroke Formation for 'प':** Formed by a vertical standing line with an attached semi-rectangular or curved hook on the left side.
• **Twenty-Second Consonant:** **'फ' (Pha)** is the second letter of the labial consonant set (पवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'फ':** An aspirated unvoiced bilabial plosive produced with an audible breath puff from the lips.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'फ':** Standard learning items include **फल (Phal - Fruit)**, **फूल (Phool - Flower)**, and **फावड़ा (Phavda - Spade)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **फल (Assorted Fruits)** represents a healthy, colorful visual reminder for the letter 'फ'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'फ':** Similar in structure to 'प' with an extended loop curling outward on the right side.

• **Twenty-Third Consonant:** **'ब' (Ba)** occupies the third position in the labial consonant group (पवर्ग).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ब':** A voiced bilabial plosive produced by closing both lips and releasing them with vocal cord vibration.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ब':** Frequent curriculum words include **बत्तख (Battakh - Duck)**, **बस (Bas - Bus)**, and **बिल्ली (Billi - Cat)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **बत्तख (Duck)** and **बस (Bus)** are common visual aids used to reinforce the letter 'ब' recognition.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ब':** Involves an enclosed loop attached to a straight vertical standing line.
• **Twenty-Fourth Consonant:** **'भ' (Bha)** is the fourth letter of the labial series.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'भ':** An aspirated voiced bilabial plosive requiring deeper vocal engagement and breath control.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'भ':** Standard textbook examples include **भालू (Bhalu - Bear)**, **भवन (Bhavan - Building)**, and **भोजन (Bhojan - Food)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **भालू (Bear)** is a popular animal illustration that instantly captures student attention for the letter 'भ'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'भ':** Features a top loop attached to a curved body, joined to a vertical standing line with a distinct break at the head.
• **Twenty-Fifth Consonant:** **'म' (Ma)** concludes the labial consonant group as the primary bilabial nasal sound.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'म':** A bilabial nasal sound produced by closing both lips and routing the voice through the nasal cavity.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'म':** Core learning words comprise **मछली (Machhli - Fish)**, **मटर (Matar - Peas)**, and **कमल (Kamal - Lotus)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **मछली (Fish)** is universally loved by children, making it an exceptional visual anchor for 'म'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'म':** Formed by a closed loop that transitions into a vertical standing line under the Shirorekha.
• **Consonant Group Summary:** Marks the completion of the 25 plosive consonants (Sparsh Vyanjan) spanning from 'क' to 'म'.

• **Twenty-Sixth Consonant:** **'य' (Ya)** initiates the semivowel or approximant category (अंतःस्थ व्यंजन - Antahstha Vyanjan).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'य':** A palatal approximant sound produced with the tongue gliding toward the palate.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'य':** Standard textbook words include **यज्ञ (Yagya - Ritual Sacrifice)**, **यान (Yaan - Vehicle/Aircraft)**, and **यात्रा (Yatra - Journey)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **यज्ञ (Yagya)** or **यान (Aircraft)** serves as the primary visual illustration for the letter 'य'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'य':** Involves an open top curve linked to a descending vertical standing line.
• **Twenty-Seventh Consonant:** **'र' (Ra)** is the second approximant consonant in the Devanagari sequence.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'र':** An alveolar approximant or trill sound produced near the alveolar ridge.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'र':** Essential learning words include **रथ (Rath - Chariot)**, **रस्सी (Rassi - Rope)**, and **रोज़ (Roz - Daily)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **रथ (Chariot)** is the traditional visual icon associated with the letter 'र'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'र':** One of the simplest strokes, drawn as a single continuous downward curve.
• **Twenty-Eighth Consonant:** **'ल' (La)** occupies the third position in the approximant category.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ल':** An alveolar lateral approximant produced with airflow passing along the sides of the tongue.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ल':** Key vocabulary includes **लट्टू (Lattu - Top/Toy)**, **लड़की (Ladki - Girl)**, and **लड्डू (Laddu - Sweet)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **लट्टू (Spinning Top)** is an engaging traditional toy illustration used for letter 'ल' recognition.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ल':** Formed by a curved body attached to a vertical standing line without upper loops.
• **Twenty-Ninth Consonant:** **'व' (Va)** concludes the approximant series as a labiodental semivowel.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'व':** A labiodental approximant produced by touching the upper teeth to the lower lip.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'व':** Core words include **वकील (Vakeel - Lawyer)**, **वर्षा (Varsha - Rain)**, and **वन (Van - Forest)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **वकील (Lawyer)** or **वर्षा (Rain clouds)** serves as the visual anchor for 'व'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'व':** Consists of an oval loop connected directly to a vertical standing line.

• **Thirtieth Consonant:** **'श' (Sha)** begins the sibilant or fricative category (ऊष्म व्यंजन - Ooshm Vyanjan).
• **Phonetic Sound of 'श':** A voiceless palatal sibilant sound produced with a hissing breath through the palate.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'श':** Essential DAV textbook words include **शलजम (Shaljam - Turnip)**, **शेर (Sher - Lion)**, and **शहर (Shahar - City)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **शलजम (Turnip)** and **शेर (Lion)** are standard visual cues used for the letter 'श'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'श':** Formed by an upper loop connected to a slanting stroke and a vertical standing line.
• **Thirty-First Consonant:** **'ष' (Sha)** is the retroflex sibilant fricative in the Devanagari script.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ष':** A retroflex fricative sound produced with the tongue curled back.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ष':** Primary learning words include **षट्कोण (Shatkon - Hexagon)** and related formal vocabulary.
• **Pictorial Association:** **षट्कोण (Hexagon)** is universally utilized as the geometric visual anchor for 'ष'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ष':** Resembles the letter 'प' with an intersecting diagonal line running through its center.
• **Thirty-Second Consonant:** **'स' (Sa)** occupies the dental sibilant position in the fricative set.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'स':** A voiceless alveolar sibilant sound produced with a gentle hiss against the teeth.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'स':** Common words include **सेब (Seb - Apple)**, **सूरज (Suraj - Sun)**, and **साइकिल (Saikil - Bicycle)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **सेब (Apple)** and **सूरज (Sun)** are beloved, highly recognizable images for young learners studying 'स'.
• **Stroke Formation for 'स':** Formed by a curved top, a middle loop, and a straight vertical standing line.
• **Thirty-Third Consonant:** **'ह' (Ha)** concludes the standard primary consonant sequence ('क' से 'ह' तक) in the DAV curriculum.
• **Phonetic Sound of 'ह':** A voiceless glottal fricative sound produced by the friction of breath in the larynx.
• **Associated Vocabulary for 'ह':** Final core words include **हाथ (Haath - Hand)**, **हाथी (Haathi - Elephant)**, and **हंस (Hans - Swan)**.
• **Pictorial Association:** **हाथी (Elephant)** and **हाथ (Hand)** provide memorable visual conclusions to the consonant revision chapter.
• **Stroke Formation for 'ह':** Involves an upper curve, a middle loop, and a sweeping bottom tail.
• **Chapter Culmination:** Successfully mastering 'ह' completes the foundational recitation and writing sequence of all 33 primary Devanagari consonants for Class UKG.
