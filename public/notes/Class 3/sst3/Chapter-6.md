• **Concept of Work**: Work is defined as any activity, whether physical or mental, that is performed to achieve a specific result or to earn a livelihood.
• **Necessity of Work**: Every human being performs some form of work to fulfill basic needs like food, clothing, and shelter.
• **Division of Labor**: Work is distributed among different people in society to ensure that all needs are met efficiently and systematically.
• **Dignity of Labor**: This is the principle that all types of jobs, whether manual or intellectual, are equally important and deserve respect.
• **Occupations**: An occupation is the specific work that a person does to earn money to support themselves and their family.
• **Economic Activities**: These are activities that involve the production, distribution, or consumption of goods and services for money.
• **Non-Economic Activities**: These are tasks performed out of love, affection, or duty, such as a mother cooking for her family, which are not done for wages.
• **Professional Work**: Jobs that require specialized training, education, or degrees, such as doctors, engineers, and teachers.
• **Skilled Labor**: Work that requires specific technical skills or training, such as carpentry, plumbing, or electrical work.
• **Unskilled Labor**: Work that primarily requires physical effort rather than specialized training, such as manual labor in construction.
• **Service Sector**: This sector includes people who provide services rather than physical goods, such as bankers, lawyers, and bus drivers.
• **Primary Sector**: This involves the extraction of raw materials from nature, such as farming, fishing, and mining.
• **Secondary Sector**: This involves the manufacturing and processing of raw materials into finished products, such as factory work.
• **Tertiary Sector**: This sector supports the other two by providing essential services like transportation, communication, and trade.
• **Interdependence**: No person can fulfill all their needs alone; therefore, we rely on the work of others to survive and thrive.
• **Community Helpers**: Individuals who provide essential services to the community, such as police officers, firefighters, and sanitation workers.
