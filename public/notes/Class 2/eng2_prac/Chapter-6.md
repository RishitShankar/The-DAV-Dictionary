• **Hull**: The main body of the boat that sits in the water, providing the structure for the vessel.
• **Keel**: A structure along the bottom of the boat that helps it stay upright and prevents it from drifting sideways.
• **Windward**: The direction from which the wind is blowing, a key concept for sailors to understand.
• **Leeward**: The direction towards which the wind is blowing, opposite to the windward side.
• **Tacking Maneuver**: The process of changing the boat's direction relative to the wind to move forward in a zig-zag pattern.
• **Jibing**: Another way to change direction, involving turning the back of the boat through the wind.
• **The Compass**: A tool used for navigation, helping the captain know which direction the boat is heading.
• **Life Jacket Function**: It provides buoyancy, keeping the wearer afloat even if they fall into the water.
• **Water Safety Rules**: Never lean too far over the side, listen to the captain, and always stay seated when moving.
• **The Importance of Weather**: Wind speed and direction are the most critical factors for a successful sailing trip.
