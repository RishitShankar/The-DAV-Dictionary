• **Welcome to Class 5 Science**: Introduction to the DAV Dayanand Anglo-Vedic academic curriculum for 'My Living World', focusing on the human body as a complex living machine.
• **Chapter Overview**: Comprehensive study of Chapter 1, titled 'My Body', which explores how our body is structured, functions, and maintains life.
• **The Human Machine**: The human body is compared to a marvelous, highly organized machine composed of millions of microscopic working units.
• **Cells as Building Blocks**: **Cell**: The fundamental, structural, and functional unit of life from which all living organisms are built.
• **Multicellular Nature**: Human beings are **multicellular organisms**, meaning our bodies are made up of billions of specialized cells working in coordinated harmony.
• **Grouping of Cells**: Similar cells that perform a specific, specialized function group together to form distinct biological units called tissues.
• **Tissue Formation**: **Tissue**: A collection of similar cells grouped together to carry out a particular task, such as muscle tissue or nerve tissue.
• **From Tissues to Organs**: Different types of tissues combine and work together to form larger, functional structures known as organs.
• **Definition of Organs**: **Organ**: A specialized body part made of multiple tissues that performs a specific, vital function for the body, such as the heart, lungs, or stomach.
• **Major Internal Organs**: The human body houses several vital internal organs protected inside body cavities, including the brain, heart, lungs, stomach, liver, and kidneys.
• **Definition of Organ System**: **Organ System**: A group of various organs that work together in a coordinated manner to perform a major life process.
• **Overview of Human Systems**: The human body functions through several interconnected organ systems, including the skeletal, muscular, digestive, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, and excretory systems.
• **Interdependence of Systems**: No organ system works in isolation; all systems depend on one another to maintain health, balance, and survival of the entire organism.
• **Body Framework**: The human body possesses a strong internal framework that gives it shape, support, and protection against external injuries.
• **Introduction to Bones**: Bones are hard, rigid living structures composed mostly of calcium and phosphorus that form the core framework of our body.

• **The Skeletal System**: **Skeletal System**: The intricate framework composed of all the bones and cartilage in the human body that provides shape and structural support.
• **Total Bones in Adults**: An adult human skeletal system typically consists of **206 bones**, varying in size, shape, and specific function.
• **Bones in Infants**: At birth, a human baby is born with approximately **300 smaller bones**, many of which gradually fuse together during growth and development.
