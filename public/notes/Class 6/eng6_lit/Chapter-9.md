• **Key Vocabulary - Seed**: Term: **Seed**; Definition: The fertilized, ripened ovule of a flowering plant containing an embryo that can germinate to produce a new plant.
• **Key Vocabulary - Sapling**: Term: **Sapling**; Definition: A young tree, especially one with a slender trunk, growing out of its initial developmental phase.
• **Key Vocabulary - Canopy**: Term: **Canopy**; Definition: The overhead habitat formed by the crown of mature trees, blocking direct sunlight and creating a shaded understory.
• **Key Vocabulary - Foliage**: Term: **Foliage**; Definition: Plant leaves collectively, especially as displayed in a vibrant green or autumnal color palette.
• **Key Vocabulary - Roots**: Term: **Roots**; Definition: The underground organs of a plant that absorb water and minerals, anchor the plant firmly, and store nutrients.
• **Key Vocabulary - Trunk**: Term: **Trunk**; Definition: The main woody stem of a tree that supports the branches and connects the root system to the upper canopy.
• **Key Vocabulary - Bark**: Term: **Bark**; Definition: The outermost protective layer of stems and roots of woody plants, safeguarding internal tissues from damage.
• **Key Vocabulary - Branch**: Term: **Branch**; Definition: A secondary woody stem or limb growing from the main trunk or from another significant limb of a tree.
• **Key Vocabulary - Shade**: Term: **Shade**; Definition: Comparative darkness and coolness caused by shelter from direct sunlight, provided densely by the tree's leaves.
• **Key Vocabulary - Ecosystem**: Term: **Ecosystem**; Definition: A biological community of interacting organisms (birds, insects, plants) and their physical environment.
• **Key Vocabulary - Biodiversity**: Term: **Biodiversity**; Definition: The variety of plant and animal life in the world or in a particular habitat, exemplified by the tree's visitors.
• **Key Vocabulary - Interdependence**: Term: **Interdependence**; Definition: The mutual reliance between two or more living things, central to the relationship between the tree and wildlife.
• **Key Vocabulary - Sustenance**: Term: **Sustenance**; Definition: Food and other necessities supporting life and growth, provided by the tree's fruits and leaves.
• **Key Vocabulary - Resilience**: Term: **Resilience**; Definition: The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties, demonstrated by the plant withstanding weather.
• **Key Vocabulary - Sanctuary**: Term: **Sanctuary**; Definition: A place of safety, refuge, and protection, such as the branches and hollows of the tree for birds.
