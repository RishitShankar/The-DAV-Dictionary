• **Thematic Focus:** The chapter centers on the festival of Diwali, providing a cultural context for linguistic learning.
• **Phonetic Goal:** The primary objective is to help students recognize, pronounce, and write words containing the long 'ई' sound.
• **Visual Association:** The text uses the imagery of Diwali to make the learning of the 'ई' matra intuitive and relatable for young learners.
• **Linguistic Structure:** The chapter progresses from simple two-letter words to complex sentences involving the 'ई' matra.
• **Diwali Significance:** The chapter highlights Diwali as the "Festival of Lights," introducing vocabulary related to celebration.
• **Vocabulary Building:** Students learn essential words like 'दीपावली' (Diwali), 'दीपक' (lamp), 'मिठाई' (sweets), and 'सीटी' (whistle).
• **Grammatical Concept:** The 'ई' matra is represented by the symbol 'ी', which is placed to the right of a consonant.
• **Sound Duration:** Unlike the 'ि' (short 'i') matra, the 'ई' (long 'i') matra requires a longer duration of sound during pronunciation.
• **Sentence Construction:** The chapter demonstrates how to combine nouns and verbs using the 'ई' matra to form meaningful sentences.
• **Cultural Integration:** By linking language to festivals, the DAV curriculum ensures that students connect Hindi learning with Indian traditions.
• **Word Recognition:** The chapter emphasizes the visual recognition of the 'ी' symbol attached to various consonants like 'द', 'प', 'स', and 'न'.
• **Contextual Learning:** Words are presented in the context of a story about a family preparing for Diwali.
• **Phonetic Clarity:** The lesson encourages clear articulation of the long 'ee' sound to distinguish it from the short 'i' sound.
• **Foundational Skill:** Mastering this chapter is essential for reading more complex Hindi literature in subsequent chapters.

• **Concept of Matra:** A 'Matra' is the symbolic representation of a vowel when it is attached to a consonant.
• **The 'ई' Symbol:** The long 'ई' vowel is represented by the sign 'ी', which is added to the right side of the consonant.
• **Pronunciation Rule:** When 'ी' is added to a consonant, the sound of the consonant is elongated, creating a "long vowel" effect.
• **Example - 'द' + 'ी':** When the 'ी' matra is added to 'द', it becomes 'दी', forming the base for words like 'दीपावली'.
• **Example - 'प' + 'ी':** When the 'ी' matra is added to 'प', it becomes 'पी', as seen in words like 'पीली' (yellow).
• **Example - 'स' + 'ी':** When the 'ी' matra is added to 'स', it becomes 'सी', used in words like 'सीटी'.
• **Example - 'न' + 'ी':** When the 'ी' matra is added to 'न', it becomes 'नी', as in 'नानी' (maternal grandmother).
• **Visual Identification:** Students must learn to identify the vertical line and the curve above it that constitute the 'ी' matra.
• **Comparison:** The chapter implicitly teaches the difference between 'दि' (short) and 'दी' (long) to prevent common spelling errors.
• **Word Formation:** The chapter shows that 'दी' + 'पा' + 'व' + 'ल' + 'ी' creates the word 'दीपावली'.
• **Syllabic Breakdown:** Breaking words into syllables helps students master the rhythm of the 'ई' matra.
• **Consistency:** The 'ी' matra is always placed after the consonant, never before or below it.
• **Writing Practice:** The chapter encourages the consistent practice of drawing the vertical line of the 'ी' matra with precision.
• **Auditory Training:** Teachers are encouraged to speak words aloud so students can hear the "long" nature of the 'ई' sound.
• **Pattern Recognition:** Students are taught to look for the 'ी' symbol in every word they encounter in the chapter.
