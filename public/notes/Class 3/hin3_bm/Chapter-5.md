• **Chapter Title:** बहादुर दोस्त (Bahadur Dost) is a heartwarming story about the bond between a young boy named Amit and his loyal dog, Sheru.
• **Theme:** The chapter emphasizes themes of friendship, loyalty, courage, and the protective nature of animals towards their human companions.
• **Character Introduction (Amit):** Amit is a young, innocent boy who lives in a house with his parents and his pet dog, Sheru.
• **Character Introduction (Sheru):** Sheru is described as a brave, intelligent, and highly alert dog who acts as a guardian for the family.
• **Setting the Scene:** The story begins by establishing the daily routine of Amit and Sheru playing together in the courtyard.
• **The Bond:** Amit considers Sheru not just a pet, but his best friend, sharing his meals and playtime with him.
• **The Parents' Perspective:** Amit’s parents appreciate Sheru’s presence because he keeps the house safe from strangers and potential threats.
• **The Incident (The Night):** The core conflict arises on a dark, quiet night when the family is fast asleep.
• **The Intruder:** A thief enters the house silently, intending to steal valuables, unaware of the presence of the vigilant dog.
• **Sheru’s Alertness:** Despite being asleep, Sheru’s keen senses detect the unusual movement and the scent of an intruder.
• **The Confrontation:** Sheru does not bark immediately; he waits for the right moment to protect his family.
• **The Action:** As the thief approaches the room where Amit is sleeping, Sheru jumps up and confronts the intruder.
• **The Struggle:** Sheru barks loudly and fiercely, creating a commotion that wakes up the entire household.
• **The Thief’s Reaction:** Startled by the dog’s aggression and the sudden noise, the thief panics and tries to escape.
• **The Outcome:** The parents rush out of their room, and the thief, realizing he has been caught, flees the premises in fear.
• **Safety Restored:** The family is safe, and they realize that a major theft was prevented solely due to Sheru’s bravery.

• **Moral Lesson:** The chapter teaches students that animals are sentient beings capable of immense loyalty and love.
• **Vocabulary (Bahadur):** **Bahadur (बहादुर):** Means brave or courageous; someone who does not fear danger.
• **Vocabulary (Dost):** **Dost (दोस्त):** Means friend; a companion who stands by you in times of need.
• **Vocabulary (Sahasi):** **Sahasi (साहसी):** A synonym for brave, often used to describe someone who takes action in difficult situations.
• **Vocabulary (Vafadar):** **Vafadar (वफादार):** Means loyal or faithful; a quality that defines the relationship between a dog and its owner.
• **Vocabulary (Chor):** **Chor (चोर):** Means a thief; someone who enters a house illegally to steal.
• **Vocabulary (Sachet):** **Sachet (सचेत):** Means alert or vigilant; being aware of one's surroundings.
• **Vocabulary (Suraksha):** **Suraksha (सुरक्षा):** Means protection or security; the act of keeping someone safe from harm.
• **Vocabulary (Sahyog):** **Sahyog (सहयोग):** Means cooperation or help; working together for a common goal.
• **Vocabulary (Darr):** **Darr (डर):** Means fear; the emotion the thief felt when confronted by Sheru.
• **Vocabulary (Prem):** **Prem (प्रेम):** Means love; the foundation of the bond between Amit and Sheru.
• **Vocabulary (Parivar):** **Parivar (परिवार):** Means family; the unit that Sheru considers his own to protect.
• **Vocabulary (Raat):** **Raat (रात):** Means night; the time when the incident took place.
• **Vocabulary (Aawaz):** **Aawaz (आवाज़):** Means sound or voice; the barking of the dog that alerted the parents.
