• **Chapter Overview**: Welcome to the DAV Dictionary Academic Notes for Class 5 Hindi Literature (भाषा माधुरी), Chapter 14, titled "बुद्धिमान राजा" (The Wise King).
• **Core Theme**: The chapter explores the timeless narrative that true strength, leadership, and conflict resolution lie not in physical might or weapons, but in sharp intellect, patience, and wisdom.
• **Literary Genre**: This chapter is presented in the form of a classic moral story (नैतिक कथा) designed to instill values of critical thinking and peaceful problem-solving among young learners.
• **Primary Protagonist**: The central figure of the story is a wise and just king who governs his kingdom with utmost care, fairness, and thoughtful deliberation.
• **Setting the Scene**: The narrative begins by introducing a peaceful kingdom where subjects lived harmoniously under the benevolent rule of the wise monarch.
• **Inciting Incident**: The plot thickens when a complex dispute arises within the kingdom, posing a severe challenge to the local administration and threatening peace.
• **Nature of the Dispute**: The conflict involves two parties fighting over a valuable possession, with neither side willing to compromise or yield to the other.
• **Failure of Conventional Methods**: Initial attempts by lower officials and ministers to resolve the dispute fail completely, as both parties remain adamant and emotionally charged.
• **Escalation to the King**: Recognizing the gravity and complexity of the deadlock, the matter is finally brought before the wise king for a definitive judgment.
• **Royal Demeanor**: Upon receiving the case, the king does not rush to make a hasty decision; instead, he displays exemplary calmness, composure, and judicial temperament.
• **The King’s Strategy**: The king decides to test the true nature and intentions of both claimants through a clever psychological observation rather than relying solely on witness testimonies.
• **Observation of Human Behavior**: The wise king carefully analyzes the body language, tone of voice, and emotional reactions of the disputing individuals during the hearing.
• **The Clever Test**: Formulating a unique and unexpected test, the king proposes a seemingly harsh or unconventional solution to see how each party reacts in real-time.
• **Revelation of Truth**: Through this ingenious test, the true intentions of the claimants are unmasked effortlessly, distinguishing genuine care from selfish greed.
• **Restoration of Justice**: Armed with undeniable clarity, the king delivers a verdict that is fair, logical, and instantly accepted by all observers as the absolute truth.
