• **मौसम (Weather):** The short-term state of the atmosphere at a specific place and time, including temperature, humidity, precipitation, and wind.
• **जलवायु (Climate):** The long-term average of weather patterns in a particular region over a long period, typically 30 years or more.
• **सूर्य (The Sun):** The primary source of energy for all weather phenomena on Earth, driving the water cycle and atmospheric circulation.
• **वायुमंडल (Atmosphere):** The layer of gases surrounding the Earth that acts as a blanket, regulating temperature and protecting life.
• **तापमान (Temperature):** A measure of how hot or cold the air is, primarily determined by the intensity of solar radiation reaching the surface.
• **आर्द्रता (Humidity):** The amount of water vapor present in the air, which significantly influences the feeling of heat or cold.
• **वर्षा (Precipitation):** Any form of water, liquid or solid, that falls from the atmosphere to the Earth's surface, including rain, snow, sleet, and hail.
• **वायु दाब (Air Pressure):** The weight of the atmosphere pressing down on the Earth's surface, which changes with altitude and temperature.
• **पवन (Wind):** The movement of air from areas of high pressure to areas of low pressure, caused by uneven heating of the Earth.
• **मौसम के कारक (Factors of Weather):** Temperature, humidity, wind speed, wind direction, atmospheric pressure, and cloud cover.
• **ऋतु (Seasons):** Distinct periods of the year characterized by specific weather patterns, resulting from the Earth's tilt and revolution around the Sun.
• **ग्रीष्म ऋतु (Summer Season):** Characterized by high temperatures, long days, and short nights due to the direct rays of the Sun.
• **शीत ऋतु (Winter Season):** Characterized by low temperatures and shorter days, occurring when a hemisphere is tilted away from the Sun.
• **वर्षा ऋतु (Monsoon/Rainy Season):** A period of significant rainfall, crucial for agriculture and replenishing water bodies.
• **वसंत ऋतु (Spring Season):** A transitional season following winter, marked by the blooming of flowers and moderate temperatures.
• **पतझड़ (Autumn/Fall):** A transitional season following summer, characterized by the shedding of leaves and cooling temperatures.

---PAGE-BREAK---

• **जल चक्र (Water Cycle):** The continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth, driven by solar energy.
• **वाष्पीकरण (Evaporation):** The process by which liquid water from oceans, lakes, and rivers turns into water vapor due to heat.
• **संघनन (Condensation):** The process where water vapor cools down and turns back into liquid water droplets, forming clouds.
• **बादल (Clouds):** Visible masses of condensed water vapor or ice crystals suspended in the atmosphere.
• **कोहरा (Fog):** A thick cloud of tiny water droplets suspended in the atmosphere at or near the Earth's surface, reducing visibility.
• **ओस (Dew):** Tiny drops of water that form on cool surfaces at night when atmospheric water vapor condenses.
• **पाला (Frost):** Frozen dew that forms when the temperature drops below the freezing point of water.
• **ओलावृष्टि (Hail):** Solid precipitation in the form of balls or irregular lumps of ice, often occurring during thunderstorms.
• **बर्फबारी (Snowfall):** Precipitation in the form of ice crystals, occurring when temperatures are below freezing in the clouds and near the ground.
• **लू (Heat Wave):** A period of excessively hot weather, often accompanied by high humidity, common in North India during summer.
• **चक्रवात (Cyclone):** A large-scale air mass that rotates around a strong center of low atmospheric pressure, often causing heavy rain and strong winds.
• **तूफान (Storm):** A violent disturbance of the atmosphere with strong winds and usually rain, thunder, lightning, or snow.
• **तड़ित (Lightning):** A giant spark of electricity in the atmosphere between clouds, the air, or the ground.
• **गड़गड़ाहट (Thunder):** The sound caused by lightning, which occurs because the lightning heats the air rapidly, causing it to expand.
• **मौसम पूर्वानुमान (Weather Forecasting):** The application of science and technology to predict the state of the atmosphere for a future time and location.
• **मौसम विज्ञान (Meteorology):** The scientific study of the atmosphere that focuses on weather processes and forecasting.

---PAGE-BREAK---

• **थर्मामीटर (Thermometer):** An instrument used to measure the temperature of the air, typically calibrated in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
• **बैरोमीटर (Barometer):** An instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure, which helps in predicting weather changes.
• **हाइग्रोमीटर (Hygrometer):** An instrument used to measure the humidity or the amount of water vapor in the atmosphere.
• **रेन गेज (Rain Gauge):** A device used by meteorologists and hydrologists to gather and measure the amount of liquid precipitation over a set period.
• **एनीमोमीटर (Anemometer):** An instrument used for measuring the speed of wind, a common weather station tool.
• **विंड वेन (Wind Vane):** A tool used to show the direction from which the wind is blowing.
• **मौसम केंद्र (Weather Station):** A facility, either on land or sea, with instruments and equipment for observing atmospheric conditions.
• **उपग्रह (Satellite):** Weather satellites orbit the Earth and provide images of cloud patterns, storms, and temperature data.
• **मौसम मानचित्र (Weather Map):** A map showing the state of the weather over a large area at a given time, using symbols for different conditions.
• **समताप रेखा (Isotherm):** A line on a map connecting points having the same temperature at a given time.
• **समदाब रेखा (Isobar):** A line on a map connecting points having the same atmospheric pressure at a given time.
• **जलवायु परिवर्तन (Climate Change):** Long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, largely driven by human activities like burning fossil fuels.
• **ग्लोबल वार्मिंग (Global Warming):** The unusually rapid increase in Earth’s average surface temperature over the past century primarily due to greenhouse gases.
• **ग्रीनहाउस प्रभाव (Greenhouse Effect):** The process by which radiation from a planet's atmosphere warms the planet's surface to a temperature above what it would be without its atmosphere.
• **ओजोन परत (Ozone Layer):** A region of Earth's stratosphere that absorbs most of the Sun's ultraviolet radiation, protecting the climate.

• **समुद्र का प्रभाव (Maritime Effect):** Coastal areas generally have milder climates because water heats up and cools down more slowly than land.
• **महाद्वीपीय प्रभाव (Continental Effect):** Inland areas experience greater temperature extremes because land heats and cools rapidly.
• **ऊंचाई (Altitude):** As altitude increases, the temperature generally decreases, which is why mountains are cooler than plains.
• **अक्षांश (Latitude):** Regions closer to the equator receive more direct sunlight and are generally warmer than regions near the poles.
• **महासागरीय धाराएं (Ocean Currents):** Large-scale movements of water that can transport heat from the equator to the poles, influencing coastal climates.
• **पर्वत श्रृंखलाएं (Mountain Ranges):** Mountains can block moisture-laden winds, causing rain on one side (windward) and a dry climate on the other (leeward).
• **वर्षा छाया क्षेत्र (Rain Shadow):** The dry area on the leeward side of a mountain range that receives significantly less rainfall.
• **वनस्पति (Vegetation):** Plants influence the local climate by releasing water vapor through transpiration and providing shade.
• **शहरीकरण (Urbanization):** Cities often experience the "Urban Heat Island" effect, where concrete and asphalt trap heat, making them warmer than rural areas.
• **मानसून (Monsoon):** A seasonal change in the direction of the prevailing winds of a region, often associated with heavy rainfall.
• **दक्षिण-पश्चिम मानसून (Southwest Monsoon):** The primary rainy season in India, bringing moisture from the Indian Ocean to the subcontinent.
• **उत्तर-पूर्वी मानसून (Northeast Monsoon):** A secondary rainy season that affects parts of Southern India during the winter months.
• **लू (Loo):** A strong, dusty, hot, and dry summer wind from the west which blows over the western Indo-Gangetic Plain region of India and Pakistan.
• **पश्चिमी विक्षोभ (Western Disturbance):** An extratropical storm originating in the Mediterranean region that brings sudden winter rain to the northwestern parts of the Indian subcontinent.
• **काल बैसाखी (Kal Baisakhi):** Localized thunderstorms and heavy rainfall in the Indian states of West Bengal and Assam during the summer.

• **मौसम का प्रभाव (Impact of Weather):** Weather dictates what we wear, what we eat, the types of houses we build, and our daily activities.
• **कृषि पर प्रभाव (Impact on Agriculture):** Weather is the most critical factor for crop production; timely rain and appropriate temperatures are essential for growth.
• **स्वास्थ्य पर प्रभाव (Impact on Health):** Extreme weather conditions can lead to heatstroke, hypothermia, or the spread of vector-borne diseases like malaria.
• **कपड़ों का चुनाव (Choice of Clothing):** We wear cotton clothes in summer to allow sweat evaporation and woolen clothes in winter to trap body heat.
• **आवास (Housing):** Houses in cold regions have sloped roofs to shed snow, while houses in hot regions have thick walls to keep interiors cool.
• **परिवहन (Transportation):** Fog, heavy rain, and snow can disrupt air, rail, and road transport, leading to delays and accidents.
• **ऊर्जा की खपत (Energy Consumption):** Extreme temperatures increase the demand for electricity for heating (in winter) and cooling (in summer).
• **जल संसाधन (Water Resources):** Weather patterns determine the availability of fresh water in rivers, lakes, and groundwater reservoirs.
• **पर्यटन (Tourism):** Weather conditions significantly influence travel choices, with people preferring hill stations in summer and beaches in winter.
• **जीव-जंतु (Wildlife):** Animals have adapted to specific weather conditions; some hibernate in winter, while others migrate to warmer climates.
• **प्रवास (Migration):** Many bird species migrate thousands of miles to escape harsh winter weather and find food.
• **हाइबरनेशन (Hibernation):** A state of minimal activity and metabolic depression in endotherms, allowing animals to survive winter food shortages.
• **एस्टीवेशन (Aestivation):** A state of animal dormancy, similar to hibernation, but taking place during the summer to avoid heat and dryness.
• **अनुकूलन (Adaptation):** The process by which organisms adjust to their environment, including weather-related challenges.
• **मौसम की चेतावनी (Weather Warning):** Official alerts issued by meteorological departments to warn the public about dangerous weather conditions.

• **वायुमंडल की परतें (Layers of Atmosphere):** The atmosphere is divided into layers: Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere, and Exosphere.
• **क्षोभमंडल (Troposphere):** The lowest layer of the atmosphere where all weather phenomena, such as clouds and rain, occur.
• **समताप मंडल (Stratosphere):** The layer above the troposphere, containing the ozone layer; it is generally calm and used by jet aircraft.
• **मध्यमंडल (Mesosphere):** The layer where most meteors burn up upon entering the atmosphere.
• **तापमंडल (Thermosphere):** The layer where temperatures increase significantly with altitude; it contains the ionosphere.
• **बहिर्मंडल (Exosphere):** The outermost layer of the atmosphere, where the air is extremely thin and merges into space.
• **वायु (Air):** A mixture of gases, primarily nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%), essential for life and weather.
• **कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड (Carbon Dioxide):** A greenhouse gas that traps heat in the atmosphere, playing a crucial role in global temperature regulation.
• **जल वाष्प (Water Vapor):** The gaseous form of water, which is the source of all clouds and precipitation.
• **वायुमंडलीय दबाव (Atmospheric Pressure):** Measured in units called millibars or hectopascals using a barometer.
• **उच्च दाब (High Pressure):** Generally associated with clear skies and fair weather as air sinks and warms.
• **निम्न दाब (Low Pressure):** Generally associated with clouds, precipitation, and stormy weather as air rises and cools.
• **जेट स्ट्रीम (Jet Streams):** Narrow bands of strong wind in the upper atmosphere that steer weather systems around the globe.
• **एल नीनो (El Niño):** A climate pattern that describes the unusual warming of surface waters in the eastern tropical Pacific Ocean, affecting global weather.
• **ला नीना (La Niña):** A climate pattern that describes the cooling of surface ocean waters along the tropical west coast of South America, opposite to El Niño.

• **मौसम और संस्कृति (Weather and Culture):** Festivals in India are often tied to seasonal changes, such as Makar Sankranti, Holi, and Baisakhi.
• **त्योहार और मौसम (Festivals and Seasons):** Many Indian festivals celebrate the harvest, which is directly dependent on the success of the monsoon.
• **भोजन और मौसम (Food and Seasons):** Seasonal diets are common; for example, consuming cooling foods like watermelon in summer and warming foods like nuts in winter.
• **लोक कथाएं (Folklore):** Many traditional proverbs and folk tales are based on observations of weather patterns passed down through generations.
• **मौसम के संकेत (Weather Signs):** Traditional knowledge includes observing animal behavior, cloud shapes, and wind direction to predict local weather.
• **बादलों के प्रकार (Types of Clouds):** Clouds are classified by their appearance and altitude: Cirrus (high), Cumulus (puffy), and Stratus (layered).
• **सिरस बादल (Cirrus Clouds):** Thin, wispy, high-altitude clouds made of ice crystals, often indicating a change in weather.
• **कपासी बादल (Cumulus Clouds):** Fluffy, white clouds with flat bases, often seen on sunny days; they can grow into storm clouds.
• **स्तरी बादल (Stratus Clouds):** Low-level, uniform, grayish clouds that often cover the entire sky like a blanket, bringing light rain or drizzle.
• **वर्षास्तरी बादल (Nimbostratus Clouds):** Dark, thick, low-level clouds that bring continuous, steady rain or snow.
• **कपासी-वर्षा बादल (Cumulonimbus Clouds):** Towering, dense clouds associated with thunderstorms, heavy rain, lightning, and sometimes tornadoes.
• **दृश्यता (Visibility):** The distance at which an object can be clearly seen, which is severely affected by fog, mist, or heavy rain.
• **वायु गुणवत्ता (Air Quality):** The degree to which the air in a given area is pollution-free, often influenced by weather conditions like temperature inversions.
• **तापमान व्युत्क्रमण (Temperature Inversion):** A phenomenon where air temperature increases with altitude, often trapping pollutants near the ground.
• **ग्रीनहाउस गैसें (Greenhouse Gases):** Gases like methane, carbon dioxide, and nitrous oxide that contribute to the warming of the atmosphere.

• **सौर विकिरण (Solar Radiation):** The energy emitted by the Sun, which is the fundamental driver of Earth's weather and climate.
• **परावर्तन (Reflection):** The process by which solar energy is bounced back into space by clouds, ice, and other surfaces (albedo).
• **अवशोषण (Absorption):** The process by which the Earth's surface and atmosphere take in solar energy, converting it into heat.
• **ऊष्मा स्थानांतरण (Heat Transfer):** Heat moves through the atmosphere via conduction, convection, and radiation.
• **संवहन (Convection):** The transfer of heat by the movement of fluids (liquids or gases); warm air rises, and cool air sinks.
• **चालन (Conduction):** The transfer of heat through direct contact between the Earth's surface and the air immediately above it.
• **विकिरण (Radiation):** The transfer of energy through electromagnetic waves, such as sunlight reaching the Earth.
• **स्थानीय पवनें (Local Winds):** Winds that blow over a limited area, such as sea breezes and land breezes.
• **समुद्री समीर (Sea Breeze):** A cool wind that blows from the sea toward the land during the day.
• **स्थलीय समीर (Land Breeze):** A cool wind that blows from the land toward the sea during the night.
• **पर्वतीय समीर (Mountain Breeze):** A wind that blows down a mountain slope at night as the air cools.
• **घाटी समीर (Valley Breeze):** A wind that blows up a mountain slope during the day as the air warms.
• **वायु द्रव्यमान (Air Mass):** A large body of air with relatively uniform temperature and humidity characteristics.
• **वाताग्र (Front):** The boundary where two different air masses meet, often leading to changes in weather.
• **शीत वाताग्र (Cold Front):** The leading edge of a cooler air mass replacing a warmer air mass, often causing thunderstorms.
• **उष्ण वाताग्र (Warm Front):** The leading edge of a warmer air mass replacing a cooler air mass, often causing steady rain.

• **पर्यावरण संरक्षण (Environmental Protection):** Protecting the environment is essential to stabilize the climate and prevent extreme weather events.
• **वृक्षारोपण (Afforestation):** Planting trees helps regulate temperature, absorb carbon dioxide, and maintain the water cycle.
• **नवीकरणीय ऊर्जा (Renewable Energy):** Using solar, wind, and hydro energy reduces reliance on fossil fuels, mitigating climate change.
• **प्रदूषण नियंत्रण (Pollution Control):** Reducing emissions from vehicles and industries helps maintain air quality and prevents atmospheric changes.
• **सतत विकास (Sustainable Development):** Meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
• **जल संरक्षण (Water Conservation):** Efficient use of water is vital, especially in regions prone to drought and irregular rainfall.
• **अपशिष्ट प्रबंधन (Waste Management):** Proper disposal of waste prevents the release of harmful gases like methane into the atmosphere.
• **जागरूकता (Awareness):** Educating people about weather patterns and climate change is the first step toward building a resilient society.
• **आपदा प्रबंधन (Disaster Management):** Developing strategies to minimize the impact of extreme weather events like floods, droughts, and cyclones.
• **मौसम संबंधी डेटा (Meteorological Data):** Historical records of weather are essential for scientists to understand long-term climate trends.
• **आधुनिक तकनीक (Modern Technology):** Advanced computers and supercomputers are used to run complex climate models for accurate predictions.
• **नागरिक विज्ञान (Citizen Science):** Ordinary people contributing to weather data collection, such as reporting rainfall or temperature in their local area.
• **जलवायु अनुकूलन (Climate Adaptation):** Adjusting systems and practices to prepare for the impacts of a changing climate.
• **जलवायु शमन (Climate Mitigation):** Actions taken to reduce the severity of climate change by lowering greenhouse gas emissions.
• **भविष्य की चुनौतियां (Future Challenges):** Dealing with rising sea levels, shifting agricultural zones, and increased frequency of extreme weather events.
