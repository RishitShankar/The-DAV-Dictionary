Privacy Policy for The DAV Dictionary

Last Updated: October 2026

The DAV Dictionary ("we", "our", or "platform"), created and operated by Head Boy Rishit Shankar for DAV Public School students, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital well-being of all student scholars, teachers, and parents.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When signing in using Google OAuth or student registration, we collect your display name, email address, profile photo, class, and section.
  • Academic Usage Data: Reading preferences across the 70+ textbook library, question-answer study history, homework submissions, and attendance logs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To personalize your 11-dimension digital campus dashboard and provide secure student ID verification.
  • To power Rishi AI textbook-grounded answers for CBSE and DAV curriculum questions.
  • To enforce our One Man, One Device single-session protection protocol.

3. Data Protection & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade student personal data with any third-party advertisers. All communications are encrypted over SSL/TLS.
