# PAGE 2: FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES & THEORY DEEP-DIVE

### 🔬 ANATOMY OF DIRECT SPEECH VS. INDIRECT SPEECH

#### 1. DIRECT SPEECH (ACTUAL UTTERANCE)
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: Represents the exact, verbatim words spoken by a speaker enclosed within quotation marks (inverted commas).
*   `[GREEN]` **Structural Components**:
    *   `[GOLD]` **Reporting Subject**: The entity producing the speech (e.g., *Rohan*).
    *   `[GOLD]` **Reporting Verb**: The verb introducing the spoken words (e.g., *said*, *says*, *remarked*).
    *   `[GOLD]` **Reporting Object**: The listener to whom speech is addressed (e.g., *to Meera*).
    *   `[GOLD]` **Reported Speech**: The actual quoted text enclosed within `"..."` (e.g., `"I have completed my work."`).
*   `[RED]` **Punctuation Standards**:
    *   ▪ A comma separates the Reporting Clause from the Reported Speech.
    *   ▪ The first word inside quotation marks always begins with a **Capital Letter**.
    *   ▪ Terminal punctuation marks (period, question mark, exclamation mark) remain **inside** the quotation marks.

#### 2. INDIRECT SPEECH (REPORTED SUBSTANCE)
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: Reports the substance of a speaker's message without quoting their exact words, adapting perspective, time, and spatial references to the reporter's context.
*   `[GREEN]` **Structural Components**:
    *   `[GOLD]` **Introductory / Reporting Clause**: Contains subject, modified verb (e.g., *told*, *asked*), and object.
    *   `[GOLD]` **Linking Subordinator / Conjunction**: Connects clauses (e.g., *that*, *if*, *whether*, *to*, *WH-words*).
    *   `[GOLD]` **Reported Clause**: Re-structured, backshifted declarative statement derived from reported speech.
*   `[RED]` **Punctuation Stripping Rules**:
    *   ▪ Remove quotation marks (`"..."`).
    *   ▪ Remove the comma separating the reporting clause and reported speech.
    *   ▪ Convert all terminal punctuation (question marks `?`, exclamation marks `!`) into a simple period (`.`).

### 🧬 THE TRI-FOLD TRANSFORMATION FRAMEWORK

*   `[GOLD]` **Pillar I: Tense Modification (Backshifting Algorithm)**
    *   ▪ Dictated strictly by the tense of the **Reporting Verb**.
    *   ▪ Applies **ONLY** if the Reporting Verb is in a **Past Tense** (*said*, *told*, *asked*, etc.).
    *   ▪ Shifts every present tense verb one step back into its corresponding past tense form.

*   `[GOLD]` **Pillar II: Personal Pronoun Realignment (S-O-N Principle)**
    *   ▪ Shifts perspective from original speaker/listener to third-person reporter context.
    *   ▪ Prevents ambiguity in tracking subjects and objects.

*   `[GOLD]` **Pillar III: Deictic Adjustment (Spatial & Temporal Anchors)**
    *   ▪ Converts psychological/physical proximity into distance (e.g., *here* ➔ *there*).
    *   ▪ Converts immediate time frames into relative historical time frames (e.g., *now* ➔ *then*).

### 📐 THE UNIVERSAL TENSE TRANSFORMATION ALGORITHM

### 🛑 THE ABSOLUTE ZERO-TENSE-SHIFT LAWS (EXCEPTIONS)

*   `[RED]` **LAW 1: PRESENT / FUTURE REPORTING VERB RULE**
    *   ▪ If the Reporting Verb is in **Present** (*says*, *has said*) or **Future** (*will say*, *shall announce*), **DO NOT BACKSHIFT** the verb tense in reported speech.
    *   ▪ *Formula*: `Present/Future Reporting Verb + Reported Speech ➔ Retain Original Tense`.

*   `[RED]` **LAW 2: UNIVERSAL TRUTHS & SCIENTIFIC FACTS**
    *   ▪ Universal verities, scientific laws, geographic facts, and mathematical truths **NEVER CHANGE TENSE**, regardless of reporting verb past status.
    *   ▪ *Formula*: `Past Reporting Verb + Universal Truth ➔ Retain Present Tense`.

*   `[RED]` **LAW 3: HABITUAL ACTIONS & PROVERBS/IDIOMS**
    *   ▪ Daily routines, habitual inclinations, or age-old adages remain invariant in tense.
    *   ▪ *Formula*: `Past Reporting Verb + Habitual/Proverb ➔ Retain Simple Present`.

*   `[RED]` **LAW 4: HISTORICAL FACTS & UNCHANGED PAST TIME**
    *   ▪ Historical facts with specific dates or time frames do not shift to Past Perfect.
    *   ▪ *Formula*: `Past Reporting Verb + Historical Fact ➔ Retain Simple Past (V2)`.

### 🛠️ MODAL AUXILIARY TRANSFORMATION EQUATIONS

*   `[BLUE]` **Will / Shall** `➔` **Would / Should** (*"shall"* becomes *"would"* for 3rd person statements, *"should"* for advice).
*   `[BLUE]` **Can** `➔` **Could**
*   `[BLUE]` **May** `➔` **Might**
*   `[GREEN]` **Must** `➔`
    *   ▪ **Had to** (for immediate, specific present obligation)
    *   ▪ **Would have to** (for future obligation)
    *   ▪ **Must** (for universal moral duty/permanent prohibition; no change)
*   `[RED]` **INVARIANT MODALS** (No Change): **Would, Could, Should, Might, Ought to, Used to, Had better**.

# PAGE 4: EXHAUSTIVE TECHNICAL GLOSSARY & PRECISION DEFINITIONS

### 📖 ALPHABETICAL TECHNICAL LEXICON

#### 1. BACKSHIFTING
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The grammatical process of shifting verb tenses backwards into the past when reporting speech that took place prior to the moment of reporting.
*   `[GOLD]` **Context**: Applied strictly when the main reporting verb is inflected for past tense.

#### 2. DEICTIC CENTRE
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The reference point in space, time, and personal identity anchored to the speaker's current orientation during utterance.
*   `[GOLD]` **Context**: Shifted in reported speech to match the reporter’s perspective (e.g., temporal/spatial re-anchoring).

#### 3. EXCLAMATORY CONVERSION
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The structural reconfiguration of interjection-based or expressive utterances into assertive sentences governed by emotion-specific reporting verbs.
*   `[GOLD]` **Context**: Requires deleting words like *Hurrah*, *Alas*, *Bravo*, and adding phrases like *exclaimed with joy/sorrow/applause*.

#### 4. IMPERATIVE CONVERSION
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The transformation of commands, advice, requests, or entreaties into non-finite infinitive clauses (`to + V1` or `not to + V1`).
*   `[GOLD]` **Context**: Drops words like *Please*, *Kindly*, and replaces *said to* with *ordered*, *requested*, *advised*, or *forbade*.

#### 5. INTRODUCTORY CONJUNCTION
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: Syntactic connector linking the principal reporting clause to the subordinate reported clause.
*   `[GOLD]` **Variants**: `that` (Assertive/Exclamatory), `if` / `whether` (Yes/No Interrogative), `to` (Imperative), `WH-word` (Open Interrogative).

#### 6. NOMINATIVE SHIFT
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The modification of personal nominative, accusative, and possessive pronouns based on the identity of reporting entities.
*   `[GOLD]` **Governing Engine**: Derived strictly via the S-O-N structural algorithm.

#### 7. OPTATIVE CONVERSION
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The transformation of wishes, prayers, or curses (often starting with *May*) into indirect assertive reporting clauses.
*   `[GOLD]` **Context**: Uses reporting verbs like *wished*, *prayed*, *blessed*, *cursed* followed by `that + Subject + might + V1`.

#### 8. REPORTED CLAUSE
*   `[BLUE]` **Definition**: The subordinate clause in an indirect speech construction containing the restructured message of the original speaker.

#### 9. REPORTING
