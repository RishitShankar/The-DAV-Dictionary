• **अमन (Aman)**: This chapter introduces the foundational concept of two-letter words (दो अक्षर वाले शब्द) in the Hindi language.
• **वर्णमाला (Alphabet)**: The chapter assumes basic knowledge of Hindi vowels (स्वर) and consonants (व्यंजन).
• **शब्द निर्माण (Word Formation)**: The core mechanism involves joining two independent consonants to form a meaningful two-letter word.
• **अमात्रिक शब्द (Non-vowel words)**: These words do not contain any vowel signs (मात्रा), relying solely on the inherent 'a' sound (अ) in each consonant.
• **अमन (Aman)**: The titular character represents a common Hindi name used to illustrate the joining of 'अ' + 'म' + 'न'.
• **ध्वनि (Phonetic Sound)**: Each letter in these words carries a short, neutral sound, which is the building block of Hindi phonetics.
• **अक्षर (Syllable/Letter)**: The combination of two distinct letters creates a single unit of meaning known as a word (शब्द).
• **सरलता (Simplicity)**: The chapter focuses on simple, everyday vocabulary to help Class 1 students grasp the concept of word synthesis.
• **उच्चारण (Pronunciation)**: Proper articulation of each letter is essential to ensure the word is read correctly.
• **क्रम (Sequence)**: The order of letters is fixed; changing the sequence changes the meaning of the word.
• **दृश्य बोध (Visual Recognition)**: Students are encouraged to associate the written form of the word with the object or action it represents.
• **अक्षर संयोजन (Letter Combination)**: The process of combining letters like 'घ' + 'र' to form 'घर' (house).
• **मौखिक अभ्यास (Oral Practice)**: Reading aloud is the primary method for mastering the flow of two-letter words.
• **शब्द भंडार (Vocabulary)**: The chapter introduces a foundational set of words including 'नल', 'जल', 'फल', and 'बस'.
• **लेखन कौशल (Writing Skill)**: Students learn to write letters closely to signify they belong to the same word.
