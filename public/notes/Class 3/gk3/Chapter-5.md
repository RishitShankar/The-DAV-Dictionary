• **Definition of a Friend:** A true friend is someone who stands by your side during difficult situations and protects you from harm.

• **Emotional Intelligence:** Recognizing fear in oneself and others is a key component of emotional maturity discussed in the text.
• **Trust:** The foundation of any strong friendship is mutual trust and the belief that the other person will act in your best interest.
• **Social Values:** The DAV curriculum promotes the idea that individual bravery contributes to a safer and more harmonious society.
• **Self-Confidence:** The narrative encourages children to believe in their own capabilities to handle difficult tasks.
• **Communication:** The importance of signaling for help or alerting others when a dangerous situation arises is a critical lesson.
• **Integrity:** Acting with honesty and courage even when no one is watching is the hallmark of a "Brave Friend."

• **Key Term: 'Resilience' (Lachilapan):** The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.
• **Key Term: 'Altruism' (Paropkar):** The belief in or practice of disinterested and selfless concern for the well-being of others.
• **Key Term: 'Loyalty' (Nishtha):** A strong feeling of support or allegiance to a friend.
• **Key Term: 'Decision' (Nirnay):** A conclusion or resolution reached after consideration.

• **Key Term: 'Safety' (Suraksha):** The condition of being protected from or unlikely to cause danger, risk, or injury.

• **Key Term: 'Responsibility' (Uttardayitva):** The state or fact of having a duty to deal with something.
