• **Concept of Shabd (शब्द)**: A *Shabd* is an independent, meaningful combination of letters (वर्णों का सार्थक मेल) existing in isolation, such as dictionary entries like 'कृष्ण', 'सुदामा', or 'दौड़ना'.
• **Transition to Pad (पद)**: When an independent *Shabd* is placed within a structured sentence or poetic line to express a complete thought, it is no longer called a *Shabd* but is termed a **पद (Pad)**.
• **Grammatical Binding**: A word becomes a *Pad* because it gets bound by grammatical rules (व्याकरण के नियमों में बंध जाना) like gender, number, person, tense, and case based on its interaction with other words.
• **Example from Context**: In isolation, 'मित्र' is a *Shabd*, but in the poetic line 'कृष्ण सुदामा के मित्र थे', the words function as *Pads* due to their active syntactical roles.
• **Definition of Pad Parichay (पद-परिचय)**: *Pad Parichay* refers to the complete grammatical identification and description of a *Pad* as it appears in a specific sentence.
• **Synonyms for Pad Parichay**: It is also known as **पद-व्याख्या** (Word Parsing) or **शब्द-व्याकरणिक परिचय** (Grammatical Introduction of a Word).
• **Core Purpose**: The primary goal of *Pad Parichay* is to reveal the exact structural, morphological, and syntactical identity of every single word in a given text.
• **Information Required in Parsing**: To fully parse a word, one must state its Part of Speech (*भेद*), Sub-type (*उपभेद*), Gender (*लिंग*), Number (*वचन*), Person (*पुरुष*), Case (*कारक*), and its syntactic relation (*संबंध*) with other words in the sentence.
• **The Five Main Categories**: The grammatical categories for parsing words in Hindi are: 1. संज्ञा (Noun), 2. सर्वनाम (Pronoun), 3. विशेषण (Adjective), 4. क्रिया (Verb), and 5. अव्यय / अविकारी (Indeclinables/Particles).
• **Role of Context in Poetry**: In poetic chapters like *Sudama Charit*, words are often inverted or structured for poetic meter (*छंद*), making careful contextual analysis essential before attempting *Pad Parichay*.
• **Distinction Between Shabd and Pad**: Students must always remember the golden rule: "शब्द स्वतंत्र होते हैं, पद बंधे होते हैं" (Words are free, pads are bound).
• **Impact of Inflection**: Inflectional suffixes (कारक चिन्ह, विभक्ति) transform a base *Shabd* into a fully functional *Pad* within a sentence architecture.
• **Syntactical Harmony**: *Pad Parichay* demonstrates how words maintain harmony and agreement (*अन्वय*) regarding gender and number within a poetic verse.
• **Analytical Skill Development**: Practicing *Pad Parichay* sharpens a student's analytical skills, helping them decode complex literary structures with ease.
• **Foundational Utility**: This grammatical tool is foundational not just for Hindi in Class 7, but for all advanced syntax, translation, and literary criticism in higher grades.

• **Introduction to Sangya (संज्ञा)**: *Sangya* (Noun) is the first major category in *Pad Parichay*, defined as the name of any person, place, thing, emotion, or state (किसी व्यक्ति, वस्तु, स्थान, भाव या अवस्था के नाम को संज्ञा कहते हैं).
• **Sub-types of Sangya**: Sangya is classified into three primary sub-types in modern Hindi grammar: 1. व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा (Proper Noun), 2. जातिवाचक संज्ञा (Common Noun), and 3. भाववाचक संज्ञा (Abstract Noun).
• **Vyakti Vachak Sangya (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा)**: Refers to a specific, unique person, place, or thing, such as 'कृष्ण', 'सुदामा', or 'द्वारिका' found within the context of *Sudama Charit*.
• **Jati Vachak Sangya (जातिवाचक संज्ञा)**: Refers to a whole class, category, or species of entities, such as 'मित्र' (friend), 'राजा' (king), 'महल' (palace), or 'पैर' (foot).
• **Bhav Vachak Sangya (भाववाचक संज्ञा)**: Refers to intangible concepts, emotional states, qualities, or actions, such as 'मित्रता' (friendship), 'गरीबी' (poverty), 'दया' (compassion), or 'पीड़ा' (pain) central to the poem's theme.
• **Parameters for Parsing Sangya**: When parsing a *Sangya Pad*, the student must specify: 1. Sangya type, 2. Gender (पुल्लिंग/स्त्रीलिंग), 3. Number (एकवचन/बहुवचन), 4. Case (कारक), and 5. Verb/Sentence relation (क्रिया के साथ संबंध).
• **Parsing Example 1 (Proper Noun)**: For the word 'कृष्ण' in 'कृष्ण ने सुदामा को गले लगाया': *व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा, पुल्लिंग, एकवचन, कर्ता कारक, 'लगाया' क्रिया का कर्ता*.
• **Parsing Example 2 (Proper Noun - Sudama)**: For the word 'सुदामा' in the same line: *व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा, पुल्लिंग, एकवचन, कर्म कारक, 'लगाया' क्रिया का कर्म*.
• **Parsing Example 3 (Abstract Noun)**: For the word 'मित्रता' in 'उनकी मित्रता अमर है': *भाववाचक संज्ञा, स्त्रीलिंग, एकवचन, कर्ता कारक, 'है' क्रिया का मुख्य संदर्भ*.
• **Identifying Gender in Nouns**: In Hindi, nouns have inherent gender; masculine entities like 'सुदामा' are पुल्लिंग, while abstract qualities like 'दया' are typically स्त्रीलिंग.
• **Identifying Number in Nouns**: Number determines whether a noun represents a single entity (एकवचन) like 'पैर' or multiple entities (बहुवचन) like 'पैर' (contextually derived).
• **Role of Karak (कारक) in Nouns**: Case markers (विभक्तियाँ) such as 'ने', 'को', 'से', 'में', 'पर' dictate the exact *Karak* of the noun during parsing.
• **Common Pitfalls in Sangya Parichay**: Confusing Proper Nouns with Common Nouns (e.g., treating 'मित्र' as a proper name instead of a common noun class) is a common error to avoid.
• **Thematic Relevance to Chapter**: The chapter *Sudama Charit* provides rich nouns like 'तंदुल' (rice grains), 'पोटली' (bundle), and 'द्वारपाल' (gatekeeper), which are excellent practice material for noun parsing.
• **Thorough Documentation**: Every noun in a line of poetry must be analyzed individually, noting how even minor objects carry heavy grammatical weight.

• **Introduction to Sarvanam (सर्वनाम)**: *Sarvanam* (Pronoun) is the grammatical category comprising words used in place of nouns to avoid repetition and maintain linguistic fluency.
• **Six Sub-types of Sarvanam**: Pronouns are divided into six distinct sub-types: 1. पुरुषवाचक (Personal), 2. निश्चयवाचक (Demonstrative), 3. अनिश्चयवाचक (Indefinite), 4. संबंधवाचक (Relative), 5. प्रश्नवाचक (Interrogative), and 6. निजवाचक (Reflexive).
• **Purush Vachak Sarvanam (पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Divided into three persons: उत्तम पुरुष (First person - मैं, हम), मध्यम पुरुष (Second person - तू, तुम, आप), and अन्य पुरुष (Third person - वह, वे, यह, ये).
• **Relevance in Poetic Dialogues**: *Sudama Charit* features direct conversations between Krishna and Sudama, making *Purush Vachak* and *Nij Vachak* pronouns extremely prominent in the text.
• **Nishchay Vachak Sarvanam (निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Points out definite objects or persons near or far, such as 'यह' (this) or 'वह' (that).
• **Anishchay Vachak Sarvanam (अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Indicates indefinite entities where specificity is lacking, such as 'कोई' (someone) or 'कुछ' (something).
• **Sambandh Vachak Sarvanam (संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Establishes a correlation between two clauses or entities, such as 'जो-सो' (who/which - that).
• **Prashna Vachak Sarvanam (प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Used for asking questions, such as 'कौन' (who) or 'क्या' (what).
• **Nij Vachak Sarvanam (निजवाचक सर्वनाम)**: Represents self-reference or ownership without direct emphasis on another noun, primarily using the pronoun 'आप' (self).
• **Parameters for Parsing Sarvanam**: When parsing a pronoun, students must detail: 1. Sarvanam type, 2. Sub-category (if Purushvachak), 3. Gender, 4. Number, 5. Person, 6. Case, and 7. Verb relation.
• **Parsing Example 1 (Personal Pronoun)**: For 'मैं' in 'मैं आया हूँ': *पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम, उत्तम पुरुष, पुल्लिंग (वक्ता के अनुसार), एकवचन, कर्ता कारक, 'आया' क्रिया का कर्ता*.
• **Parsing Example 2 (Demonstrative Pronoun)**: For 'वह' in 'वह कौन है?': *निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम, पुल्लिंग, एकवचन, कर्ता कारक, 'है' क्रिया से संबंधित*.
• **Distinction Between Sarvanam and Sarvanamik Visheshan**: A pronoun becomes a pronominal adjective if it directly precedes and qualifies a noun (e.g., 'यह महल' vs. 'यह सुंदर है').
• **Contextual Pronouns in Chapter**: Words like 'तुम', 'हम', 'जो', and 'सो' appear frequently in the devotional verses, requiring careful tracking of the speaker and listener.
• **Accuracy in Pronoun Parsing**: Ensuring the correct identification of 'Purush' (Person) is vital, as mixing up Uttam, Madhyam, and Anya is a frequent student error.

• **Introduction to Visheshan (विशेषण)**: *Visheshan* (Adjective) is the grammatical category of words that qualify or describe nouns or pronouns, adding descriptive depth to language.
• **Four Primary Sub-types of Visheshan**: Adjectives are classified into four main categories: 1. गुणवाचक (Qualitative), 2. संख्यावाचक (Numeral), 3. परिमाणवाचक (Quantitative), and 4. सार्वनामिक / संकेतवाचक (Pronominal/Demonstrative).
• **Gun Vachak Visheshan (गुणवाचक विशेषण)**: Describes qualities, characteristics, states, colors, or shapes, such as 'गरीब' (poor), 'सुंदर' (beautiful), 'दयालुकृत' (merciful), or 'दीन' (wretched) in relation to Sudama.
• **Sankhya Vachak Visheshan (संख्यावाचक विशेषण)**: Indicates the countable number of nouns, divided into निश्चित (Definite, e.g., 'चार दिन') and अनिश्चित (Indefinite, e.g., 'कुछ लोग').
• **Pariman Vachak Visheshan (परिमाणवाचक विशेषण)**: Indicates the measurable quantity or weight of items, divided into निश्चित (Definite) and अनिश्चित (Indefinite, e.g., 'थोड़ा चावल').
• **Sarvanamik Visheshan (सार्वनामिक विशेषण)**: When a pronoun is placed immediately before a noun to qualify it, it functions as an adjective rather than a pure pronoun.
• **Concept of Visheshya (विशेष्य)**: The noun or pronoun that is being qualified by the adjective is known as the *Visheshya*.
• **Concept of Pravisheshan (प्रविशेषण)**: Words that modify or show the intensity of an adjective itself (e.g., 'बहुत' in 'बहुत गरीब') are called *Pravisheshan*.
• **Parameters for Parsing Visheshan**: When parsing an adjective, students must state: 1. Visheshan type, 2. Gender, 3. Number, 4. Case (matching the Visheshya), and 5. The specific *Visheshya* it qualifies.
• **Parsing Example 1 (Qualitative Adjective)**: For 'दीन' in 'दीन सुदामा': *गुणवाचक विशेषण, पुल्लिंग, एकवचन, 'सुदामा' संज्ञा (विशेष्य) की विशेषता प्रकट कर रहा है*.
• **Parsing Example 2 (Quantitative Adjective)**: For 'थोड़े' in 'थोड़े चावल': *परिमाणवाचक विशेषण (अनिश्चित), पुल्लिंग, बहुवचन, 'चावल' संज्ञा (विशेष्य) का परिमाण बता रहा है*.
• **Agreement Rule**: Adjectives in Hindi generally agree in gender, number, and case with the noun (*Visheshya*) they modify.
• **Poetic Adjectives in Sudama Charit**: The poem uses powerful emotive adjectives to portray Sudama's condition, such as 'विद्यर्ण' (torn clothes) or 'मलिन' (dusty/dirty feet).
• **Avoiding Confusion**: Students must carefully distinguish whether a word is acting independently as a noun/pronoun or modifying another word as an adjective.
• **Exhaustive Practice**: Mastering adjective parsing ensures students can accurately capture the vivid imagery and descriptive beauty created by the poet.

• **Introduction to Kriya (क्रिया)**: *Kriya* (Verb) is the grammatical category denoting an action, occurrence, state of being, or physical process in a sentence (जिस शब्द से किसी काम के करने या होने का बोध हो, उसे क्रिया कहते हैं).
• **Basis of Classification**: Verbs are primarily classified based on two main criteria: 1. कर्म के आधार पर (Based on Object), and 2. रचना के आधार पर (Based on Structure).
• **Sakarmak Kriya (सकर्मक क्रिया)**: Verbs that require an object (*Karma*) to complete their meaning, where the fruit of the action falls on the object (e.g., 'कृष्ण ने माला पहनी').
• **Akarmak Kriya (अकर्मक क्रिया)**: Verbs that do not require an object, where the action is self-contained within the subject (e.g., 'सुदामा रोए' or 'कृष्ण हंसे').
• **Structural Sub-types of Kriya**: Includes सामान्य क्रिया (Simple), संयुक्त क्रिया (Compound), नामधातु क्रिया (Denominative), प्रेरणार्थक क्रिया (Causative), and पूर्वकालिक क्रिया (Conjunctive).
• **Concept of Dhatu (धातु)**: The root form of a verb from which all its conjugated forms are derived (e.g., 'पढ़', 'चल', 'हँस', 'रो') is called the *Dhatu*.
• **Parameters for Parsing Kriya**: When parsing a verb, the student must specify: 1. Kriya type (Sakarmak/Akarmak), 2. Root form (Dhatu), 3. Voice (वाच्य - कर्तृ/कर्म/भाव), 4. Gender, 5. Number, 6. Person, 7. Tense (काल), and 8. Syntactic relation.
• **Parsing Example 1 (Intransitive Verb)**: For 'रोए' in 'सुदामा रोए': *अकर्मक क्रिया, धातु 'रो', कर्तृवाच्य, पुल्लिंग, बहुवचन (आदरार्थ), अन्य पुरुष, भूतकाल, 'सुदामा' कर्ता का संबंध*.
• **Parsing Example 2 (Transitive Verb)**: For 'गले लगाया' in 'कृष्ण ने सुदामा को गले लगाया': *सकर्मक क्रिया, संयुक्त क्रिया, धातु 'गले+लगा', कर्तृवाच्य, पुल्लिंग, एकवचन, अन्य पुरुष, भूतकाल, 'कृष्ण' कर्ता और 'सुदामा' कर्म से संबंध*.
• **Role of Tense (काल)**: Verbs indicate the time of action—भूतकाल (Past), वर्तमान काल (Present), or भविष्यत् काल (Future)—which must be explicitly stated during parsing.
• **Role of Voice (वाच्य)**: Identifying whether the sentence is in कर्तृवाच्य (Active), कर्मवाच्य (Passive), or भाववाचक (Impersonal) voice is mandatory for complete verb parsing.
• **Honorific Usage (आदरार्थ)**: In poetic texts depicting divine or revered figures like Lord Krishna, plural verbs are often used for singular subjects out of respect, which must be noted in number and person.
• **Dynamic Nature of Verbs**: Verbs drive the narrative forward in *Sudama Charit*, tracking transitions from poverty and journey to royal reception.
• **Precise Dhatu Identification**: Students must drill down to find the exact root verb, even when prefixes or compound formations obscure the base form.
• **Comprehensive Verb Mastery**: Thorough parsing of verbs confirms a student's grip on the temporal and action-oriented dynamics of Hindi syntax.

• **Introduction to Avyay / Avikari (अव्यय / अविकारी शब्द)**: Words that do not undergo any morphological changes based on gender, number, person, or case are called *Avyay* or *Avikari* (Indeclinables).
• **Four Primary Sub-types of Avyay**: Indeclinables are divided into four main categories: 1. क्रियाविशेषण (Adverb), 2. संबंधबोधक (Preposition/Postposition), 3. समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction), and 4. विस्मयादिबोधक (Interjection).
• **Kriya Visheshan (क्रियाविशेषण)**: Words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs by indicating manner, time, place, or quantity (e.g., 'अचानक', 'यहाँ', 'कल', 'तेजी से').
• **Four Sub-types of Kriya Visheshan**: 1. रीतिवाचक (Manner), 2. कालवाचक (Time), 3. स्थानवाचक (Place), and 4. परिमाणवाचक (Quantity).
• **Sambandh Bodhak (संबंधबोधक)**: Words that link a noun or pronoun to other words in the sentence, typically preceded by case markers like 'के पास', 'के बिना', 'के आगे', 'के ऊपर'.
• **Samuchchay Bodhak (समुच्चयबोधक)**: Conjunctions that join words, phrases, or clauses together, such as 'और', 'तथा', 'किंतु', 'परंतु', 'क्योंकि'.
• **Vismayadi Bodhak (विस्मयादिबोधक)**: Interjections that express sudden emotions like joy, sorrow, surprise, or anger, such as 'अरे!', 'वाह!', 'हाय!', 'धिक्कार!'.
• **Parameters for Parsing Avyay**: When parsing an indeclinable, the student must state: 1. Avyay type, 2. Sub-type, and 3. The specific word, phrase, or clause it modifies or connects.
• **Parsing Example 1 (Adverb)**: For 'तेजी से' in 'सुदामा तेजी से चले': *क्रियाविशेषण, रीतिवाचक क्रियाविशेषण, 'चले' क्रिया की विशेषता बता रहा है*.
• **Parsing Example 2 (Conjunction)**: For 'और' in 'कृष्ण और सुदामा मित्र थे': *समुच्चयबोधक अव्यय, समानाधिकरण समुच्चयबोधक, 'कृष्ण' और 'सुदामा' दो संज्ञा शब्दों को जोड़ रहा है*.
• **Parsing Example 3 (Interjection)**: For 'अरे' in 'अरे! तुम आ गए': *विस्मयादिबोधक अव्यय, आश्चर्य भाव व्यक्त कर रहा है*.
• **Absence of Inflection**: Unlike nouns and verbs, students must remember never to write gender, number, or case for *Avyay* words, as they are *Avikari* (unchangeable).
• **Emotional Resonance in Poetry**: *Vismayadi Bodhak* and emphatic particles (*निपात* like 'ही', 'भी', 'तक') appear frequently in poetic outbursts, adding intense emotional flavor.
• **Syntactical Glue**: Avyay words act as the structural glue of the poem, ensuring smooth transitions between narrative shifts.
• **Error Prevention in Parsing**: A common mistake is attempting to assign gender to an adverb; strict adherence to the *Avikari* rule prevents this.

• **Comprehensive Line-by-Line Parsing Methodology**: To successfully parse a poetic line from *Sudama Charit*, students must isolate each word sequentially from left to right.
• **Step 1 - Word Isolation**: Extract the exact word as it appears in the text, ensuring no typographical alterations are made.
• **Step 2 - Contextual Evaluation**: Determine the exact semantic role of the word within the specific narrative context of Lord Krishna and Sudama's meeting.
• **Step 3 - Categorization**: Identify the primary grammatical class (*शब्द भेद*) among Sangya, Sarvanam, Visheshan, Kriya, or Avyay.
• **Step 4 - Detailed Attributes**: List all relevant morphological sub-properties (Gender, Number, Person, Case, Voice, Tense, Dhatu, etc.) systematically.
• **Step 5 - Syntactical Mapping**: Explicitly state the relationship of the parsed word with other governing words in the sentence (*अन्वय / संबंध*).
• **Illustration Verse**: Consider a typical line from devotional poetry: "देखि सुदामा की दीन दशा, करइ करुणा करइ निधि रोये।"
• **Parsing Word 1 ('देखि')**: *पूर्वकालिक क्रिया (या कृदंत क्रिया), धातु 'देख', संबंध 'रोये' क्रिया से पूर्व होने वाली क्रिया को दर्शाना*.
