• **Chapter Overview:** The chapter "फैलती चप्पलें" (Expanding Slippers) is a humorous and insightful story from the DAV Class 4 Hindi textbook, *Bhasha Madhuri*.
• **Core Theme:** The story revolves around the innocent curiosity and logical reasoning of a young child regarding the physical changes in objects over time.
• **Protagonist:** The central character is a young boy who observes the world through a lens of wonder and scientific curiosity.
• **The Conflict:** The boy notices that his slippers, which once fit him perfectly, have become "tight" or "small" over time.
• **The Misconception:** The child mistakenly believes that the slippers themselves are shrinking or "failing to expand" rather than his own feet growing.
• **Innocence:** The narrative highlights the developmental stage of childhood where logic is applied based on limited life experience.
• **Parental Interaction:** The story includes interactions with parents who try to explain the concept of physical growth to the child.
• **Humor Element:** The humor arises from the child’s persistent attempts to "fix" the slippers to make them fit again.
