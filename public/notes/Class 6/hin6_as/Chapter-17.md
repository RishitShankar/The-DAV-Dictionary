• **Advanced Grammar Application**: Deep-dive into complex grammatical phenomena embedded in the chapter's syntax.
• **Sandhi (संधि)**: Rule-based blending of contiguous sounds in compound travel terms (e.g., सूर्य + उदय = सूर्योदय).
• **Samas (समास)**: Formation of compound words frequently found in formal Hindi prose (e.g., देश-भ्रमण, तीर्थयात्रा).
• **Tatpurusha Samas**: Compound words where the second element dominates, such as 'मार्गव्यय' (expenses of the route).
• **Karmadharay Samas**: Compounds involving descriptive relationships, like 'महापुरुष' (great soul/traveller).
• **Dvigu Samas**: Numeric compounds often denoting distances or time, such as 'त्रिदिवसीय' (three-day journey).
• **Bahuvrihi Samas**: Compounds yielding a third symbolic meaning, applicable in poetic descriptions of nature.
• **Upsarg (उपसर्ग)**: Analyzing prefixes attached to root words, such as 'वि-' in 'विदेश' (foreign country).
• **Pratyay (प्रत्यय)**: Examining suffixes that create abstract nouns, such as '-ता' in 'सुंदरता' (beauty) or '-त्व' in 'मित्रता'.
• **Vachya (वाच्य - Voice)**: Transforming active voice (*कर्तृवाच्य*) sentences into passive (*कर्मवाच्य*) or impersonal (*भाववाच्य*) voices.
• **Karthrivachya Example**: 'यात्री ने सुंदर दृश्य देखा' (The traveller saw the beautiful view).
• **Karmavachya Example**: 'यात्री द्वारा सुंदर दृश्य देखा गया' (The beautiful view was seen by the traveller).
• **Punctuation Rules**: Proper utilization of commas, full stops, inverted commas, and exclamation marks in descriptive writing.
• **Direct and Indirect Speech**: Converting dialogues spoken by travellers between direct (*प्रत्यक्ष*) and indirect (*अप्रत्यक्ष*) forms.
• **Error Correction**: Identifying and rectifying common grammatical errors in student compositions regarding tense agreement and gender.
