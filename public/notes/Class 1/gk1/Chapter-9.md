•   **Vowels (स्वर - Swar):** In Hindi, vowels are independent sounds that can be pronounced without the help of any other sound.
•   **Vowel Marks (मात्रा - Matra):** When vowels are combined with consonants to form words, they are represented by special symbols called vowel marks.
•   The vowel we are focusing on in this chapter is **ऐ (Ai)**.
•   **Pronunciation of ऐ (Ai):** The sound of 'ऐ' is similar to the 'ai' in English words like 'fair', 'hair', 'pair', or 'stair'. It is an open, slightly elongated 'e' sound.
•   It is important to differentiate 'ऐ' from 'ए' (e). 'ए' has a shorter 'e' sound (like 'e' in 'bed'), while 'ऐ' has a broader 'ai' sound.
•   **Symbol of ऐ की मात्रा:** The vowel mark for 'ऐ' is represented by two slanting lines placed above the consonant. It looks like this: **ै**.
•   When 'ऐ' मात्रा is added to a consonant, it changes the sound of that consonant.
•   **Example of adding ऐ की मात्रा:**
    *   क (ka) + ै (ai) = **कै (kai)**
    *   ख (kha) + ै (ai) = **खै (khai)**
    *   ग (ga) + ै (ai) = **गै (gai)**
    *   च (cha) + ै (ai) = **चै (chai)**
    *   प (pa) + ै (ai) = **पै (pai)**
    *   म (ma) + ै (ai) = **मै (mai)**
    *   र (ra) + ै (ai) = **रै (rai)**
    *   स (sa) + ै (ai) = **सै (sai)**
•   The two slanting lines (ै) always appear above the consonant, extending slightly to the right.
•   Understanding this symbol is crucial for correctly reading and writing words containing the 'ऐ' sound.
•   Students should practice writing the 'ऐ' मात्रा with various consonants to internalize its form and sound.
•   The 'ऐ' vowel mark is a fundamental component of Hindi orthography.
•   Mastering the 'ऐ' sound and its corresponding mark is a key step in developing Hindi literacy.
•   This chapter uses the story of Jhabru to provide practical examples of words with 'ऐ' मात्रा.
•   The visual recognition of the 'ै' symbol is as important as its auditory recognition.

    *   **Meaning:** An ox or a bull.

    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru might see an ox in a field, or an ox could be pulling a cart.
•   **Word 3: थैला (Thaila)**
    *   **Meaning:** A bag or a sack.
    *   **Breakdown:** थ + ै + ल + आ = थैला
    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru might carry a bag, or find a bag, or someone else in the story might have a bag.
•   **Word 4: पैसा (Paisa)**
    *   **Meaning:** Money, specifically a coin or a unit of currency.
    *   **Breakdown:** प + ै + स + आ = पैसा
    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru might find a coin, or someone might be counting money.
•   **Word 5: पैर (Pair)**
    *   **Meaning:** A foot or a leg.
    *   **Breakdown:** प + ै + र = पैर
    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru might run on his feet, or someone might hurt their foot.
•   **Word 6: मैदान (Maidan)**
    *   **Meaning:** A field, ground, or open space.
    *   **Breakdown:** म + ै + द + आ + न = मैदान
    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru is likely playing or running in a field.
•   **Word 7: पैदल (Paidal)**
    *   **Meaning:** On foot, walking.
    *   **Breakdown:** प + ै + द + ल = पैदल
    *   **Usage in story:** Jhabru might travel on foot.

•   Students should practice reading and writing these words, paying close attention to the 'ै' symbol.
•   Understanding the meaning of these words within the story context helps in better comprehension.
•   The repetition of the 'ऐ' sound in different words reinforces its recognition.

•   Beyond the specific words found in the "नटखट झबरू" story, it is essential to explore a wider range of common Hindi words that use the 'ऐ' मात्रा.
•   This expansion helps students generalize their understanding of the 'ऐ' sound and its symbol.
•   **Word: पैर (Pair)**
    *   **Meaning:** Foot or leg.
    *   **Example:** मेरे दो **पैर** हैं। (I have two feet.)
•   **Word: मैदान (Maidan)**
    *   **Meaning:** Field, ground, or open space.
    *   **Example:** बच्चे **मैदान** में खेलते हैं। (Children play in the field.)
•   **Word: पैदल (Paidal)**
    *   **Meaning:** On foot, walking.
    *   **Example:** मैं स्कूल **पैदल** जाता हूँ। (I go to school on foot.)
•   **Word: कैमरा (Camera)**
    *   **Meaning:** Camera. (A common borrowed word, but widely used).
    *   **Example:** मेरे पास एक नया **कैमरा** है। (I have a new camera.)
•   **Word: फैला (Phaila)**
    *   **Meaning:** Spread out, extended. (Past tense of फैलना - to spread).
    *   **Example:** उसने चादर **फैला** दी। (He spread the sheet.)
•   **Word: बैठना (Baithna)**
    *   **Meaning:** To sit.
    *   **Example:** कृपया यहाँ **बैठिए**। (Please sit here.)
•   **Word: तैरना (Tairna)**
    *   **Meaning:** To swim.
    *   **Example:** मुझे **तैरना** पसंद है। (I like to swim.)
•   **Word: गैया (Gaiya)**
    *   **Meaning:** Cow (an affectionate or poetic term).
    *   **Example:** **गैया** घास खा रही है। (The cow is eating grass.)
•   **Word: सैनिक (Sainik)**
    *   **Meaning:** Soldier.
    *   **Example:** **सैनिक** देश की रक्षा करते हैं। (Soldiers protect the country.)
•   **Word: भैया (Bhaiya)**
    *   **Meaning:** Brother (an affectionate term).
    *   **Example:** मेरे **भैया** बहुत अच्छे हैं। (My brother is very good.)
•   **Word: जैसा (Jaisa)**
    *   **Meaning:** Like, as, similar to.
    *   **Example:** तुम मेरे **जैसे** हो। (You are like me.)
•   **Word: वैसा (Vaisa)**
    *   **Meaning:** Like that, in that manner.
    *   **Example:** **वैसा** मत करो। (Don't do that.)
•   These examples help students recognize the 'ऐ' मात्रा in various contexts and expand their vocabulary.
•   Emphasize the correct pronunciation of each word, focusing on the 'ai' sound.
•   Encourage students to identify the 'ै' symbol in each of these words.
•   This section reinforces the versatility and commonality of the 'ऐ' vowel mark in Hindi language.
