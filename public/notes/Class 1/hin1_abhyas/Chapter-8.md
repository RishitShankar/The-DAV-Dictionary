• **Grammar Integration:** The chapter introduces the use of 'ने' (ne) as a postposition, which is a key grammatical concept in Hindi.
• **Usage of 'ने':** 'ने' is used with transitive verbs in the past tense (e.g., 'रमेश ने सेब खाया').
• **Subject-Verb Agreement:** The verb changes according to the object when 'ने' is used.
• **Example:** 'रमेश ने केला खाया' (Ramesh ate a banana).
• **Example:** 'रमेश ने केले खाए' (Ramesh ate bananas - plural object).
• **Concept of Plurality:** The 'ए' matra is also used to denote plural forms of masculine nouns ending in 'आ' (aa).
• **Rule:** 'आ' changes to 'ए' for plural (e.g., 'केला' becomes 'केले').
