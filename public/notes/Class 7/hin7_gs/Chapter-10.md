• **Chapter Overview**: Welcome to the DAV Dictionary Academic Notes for Class 7 Hindi - ज्ञान सागर, Chapter 10, titled "झंडा ऊँचा रहे हमारा" (Our Flag Must Fly High).
• **Literary Genre**: This chapter is a patriotic poem ('राष्ट्र-भक्ति कविता') composed by the renowned poet Shyamlal Gupta 'Parshad'.
• **Core Theme**: The central theme revolves around the national flag as a symbol of national pride, freedom, unity, and unwavering spirit.
• **Historical Significance**: The poem served as a powerful rallying cry during India's freedom struggle, inspiring countless revolutionaries and citizens.
• **Poet Introduction**: Shyamlal Gupta 'Parshad' was a dedicated freedom fighter and poet who poured his soul into writing inspiring nationalist verses.
• **Title Analysis**: The phrase "झंडा ऊँचा रहे हमारा" encapsulates the desire to keep the nation's honor, represented by the flag, flying high without ever bowing down.
• **Visual Imagery**: The poem creates strong visual imagery of a tri-color flag fluttering proudly against the sky, representing a newly awakened nation.
• **Emotional Resonance**: It evokes deep emotions of patriotism, sacrifice, and collective identity among students and readers of all ages.
• **DAV Curriculum Objective**: The DAV Public School Board includes this chapter to instill core values of nationalism, respect for national symbols, and civic duty.
• **Linguistic Style**: The poem uses simple yet powerful Khariboli Hindi, making it accessible, melodious, and easy to memorize for young learners.
• **Rhythm and Rhyme**: The verses possess a marching rhythm that naturally inspires a posture of attention, discipline, and reverence.
• **Symbolism of the Flag**: The national flag is portrayed not merely as a piece of cloth, but as the living soul and collective honor of millions of Indians.
• **Freedom Struggle Context**: The verses reflect the relentless struggle, trials, and ultimate triumph of Indian freedom fighters against colonial rule.
• **Universal Appeal**: While rooted in Indian nationalism, the poem champions universal human values of liberty, equality, and fearlessness.
• **Chapter Structure**: The comprehensive study notes on this chapter are systematically organized across ten detailed pages to ensure absolute academic mastery.
