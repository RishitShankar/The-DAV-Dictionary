• **Chapter Introduction:** The chapter "रोटी" (Roti) in the Class 2 Hindi curriculum focuses on the journey of a grain of wheat from the field to the dining plate.
• **Core Theme:** The narrative emphasizes the value of hard work, the importance of food, and the gratitude we should feel toward farmers.
• **Primary Character:** The story revolves around a young child who learns about the labor involved in making a simple roti.
• **Concept of Agriculture:** The chapter introduces the basic process of farming, starting with the preparation of the soil.
• **Sowing Process:** Farmers sow seeds in the field, which is the foundational step of food production.
• **Role of Nature:** The growth of wheat depends on sunlight, water (rain/irrigation), and the fertility of the soil.
• **Harvesting:** Once the wheat crop matures, it is cut and gathered by the farmer.
• **Threshing:** The process of separating the grain from the stalks is a crucial agricultural stage described in the chapter.
• **Milling:** The harvested grains are taken to a mill (chakki) to be ground into flour (aata).
• **Kneading:** The transformation of flour into dough (gundha hua aata) requires water and physical effort.
• **Rolling:** The dough is shaped into round discs, which is a skill that requires practice and patience.
• **Cooking:** The final step involves heating the roti on a hot plate (tava) until it puffs up.
• **Nutritional Value:** The chapter subtly teaches that roti provides energy and strength to the body.
• **Respect for Food:** A major moral takeaway is that food should never be wasted because many people work hard to produce it.
• **Vocabulary Focus:** Key terms like 'किसान' (Farmer), 'खेत' (Field), 'अनाज' (Grain), and 'मेहनत' (Hard work) are central to the lesson.

• **The Farmer's Role:** The farmer is depicted as the primary provider who spends long hours under the sun to grow crops.
• **Field Preparation:** Before sowing, the farmer plows the field to make the soil soft and ready for seeds.
• **Irrigation:** Water is essential for the crop; the chapter highlights the dependence of farming on timely water supply.
• **Crop Protection:** Farmers protect the growing wheat from pests and weeds to ensure a healthy harvest.
• **The Golden Crop:** When the wheat turns golden, it signifies that the crop is ready for harvesting.
• **Harvesting Tools:** Traditional tools like the sickle (darati) are often associated with the manual labor of harvesting.
• **Storage:** After harvesting, grains are stored safely to be used throughout the year.
• **Transportation:** The grain is transported from the fields to the market (mandi) or directly to the mill.
• **The Mill (Chakki):** The mill is where the hard grain is crushed into fine, soft flour.
• **Flour (Aata):** The quality of the roti depends on the quality of the flour produced at the mill.
• **Kitchen Essentials:** The kitchen is presented as a place of transformation where raw ingredients become edible meals.
• **Dough Preparation:** Adding water to flour and kneading it creates a smooth texture necessary for rolling.
• **Rolling Pin (Belan):** The 'belan' and 'chakla' are the primary tools used to give the roti its circular shape.
• **Heat Application:** The 'tava' must be at the right temperature to ensure the roti cooks evenly without burning.
• **Puffing Up:** A well-made roti puffs up due to the steam trapped inside, indicating it is perfectly cooked.

• **Term: किसान (Kisan):** A person who cultivates land and grows crops; the backbone of the food supply chain.
• **Term: खेत (Khet):** An area of land used for agricultural purposes, where wheat is grown.
• **Term: अनाज (Anaaj):** The edible part of the cereal plant, such as wheat, which is processed into flour.
• **Term: मेहनत (Mehnat):** Hard work or labor; the essential ingredient in the production of food.
• **Term: चक्की (Chakki):** A machine or device used to grind grains into flour.
• **Term: आटा (Aata):** The powder obtained by grinding wheat, used to make dough.
• **Term: गूँधना (Goonthna):** The process of mixing flour with water to create a pliable dough.
• **Term: बेलन (Belan):** A cylindrical tool used to roll out dough into a flat, circular shape.
• **Term: तवा (Tava):** A flat metal pan used for cooking rotis over a flame.
• **Term: फसल (Fasal):** The yield of a crop that is harvested at the end of a growing season.
• **Term: कटाई (Katai):** The process of cutting the mature crop from the field.
• **Term: बुवाई (Buwai):** The act of planting seeds in the soil.
• **Term: पोषण (Poshan):** Nutrition derived from food that helps the body grow and stay healthy.
• **Term: सम्मान (Samman):** Respect; the attitude we should have toward food and those who produce it.
• **Term: प्रक्रिया (Prakriya):** The sequence of steps followed to complete a task, such as making a roti.
