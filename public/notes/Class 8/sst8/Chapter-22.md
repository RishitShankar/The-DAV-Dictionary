# CLASS 8 SOCIAL SCIENCE (WE AND OUR WORLD)
## CHAPTER 22: SAFEGUARDING THE MARGINALISED
### STUDY NOTES & COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS

• **Concept of Marginalisation**: Marginalisation refers to a social process where certain individuals, groups, or communities are pushed to the fringes or margins of society, effectively denying them equal access to rights, opportunities, resources, and decision-making processes.
• **Marginalised Groups**: Populations that experience systemic exclusion, economic deprivation, social discrimination, and political disempowerment, primarily including Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), Other Backward Classes (OBCs), Religious Minorities, Women, Children, and Persons with Disabilities.
• **Social Exclusion**: The operational mechanism of marginalisation wherein specific communities are systematically blocked from or denied full access to various rights, opportunities, and resources that are normally available to members of a different group.
• **Mainstream Society**: The dominant socio-economic and cultural section of the population that controls political power, economic resources, social institutions, and cultural narratives, often marginalising minority or underprivileged groups.
